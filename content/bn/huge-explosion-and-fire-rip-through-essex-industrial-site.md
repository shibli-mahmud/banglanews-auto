The agency claims that the accused were planning to carry out attacks in Delhi and other parts of the country. The chargesheet was filed under various sections of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA) and the Indian Penal Code (IPC). The NIA has stated that the accused were inspired by the ideology of the Islamic State of Iraq and Syria (ISIS) and had been in communication with foreign handlers. They were allegedly planning to procure weapons and explosives for their terror activities. The agency has also claimed that the accused had conducted reconnaissance of potential targets and had even made attempts to recruit new members into their group. The investigation is still ongoing and the NIA is expected to file more chargesheets in the future. The case highlights the persistent threat of homegrown terrorism and the challenges faced by law enforcement agencies in preventing such attacks. This incident underscores the need for continuous vigilance and intelligence gathering to counter radicalization and disrupt terror networks operating within the country.
