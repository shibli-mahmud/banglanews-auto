ক্ষমতাসীন প্রধানমন্ত্রী অ্যান্ডি বার্নহামThe incoming prime minister of the country, a major policy shift, announced that the proposed digital identity card project would be cancelled. This is his first major policy pledge since taking office. His government 'will put its focus where people need it right now,' a spokesperson said. The spokesperson further said that the government will take all necessary steps to tackle the cost of living crisis and provide relief to the general public.  It is learned that the government will take initiatives to control inflation, reduce the prices of daily necessities, and create employment opportunities.  Meanwhile, the decision to cancel the digital ID project has been praised by many quarters of the public.  'The government's decision to prioritize the cost of living crisis over the digital ID project is commendable,' said one public representative. 'This will bring relief to the common people who are struggling with the rising cost of living.' The spokesperson added that the government will also review other ongoing projects and take steps to ensure that all government resources are used for the benefit of the public.  This policy change is expected to bring significant relief to the people of the country.
