মার্কিন প্রেসিডেন্ট ডোনাল্ড ট্রাম্প হরমুজ প্রণালী ব্যবহারকারী সকল কার্গো জাহাজের উপর আরোপিত ২০% শুল্ক আরোপের হুমকি প্রত্যাহার করেছেন। মাত্র ২৪ ঘন্টা পূর্বে দেওয়া এই ঘোষণা বিশ্বব্যাপী নৌ পরিবহন বাজারকে অনিশ্চয়তায় ফেলে দিয়েছিল। তবে, হোয়াইট হাউসের কর্মকর্তারা এইThe White House has announced a significant policy shift, with President Trump withdrawing his recent threat to impose a 20% fee on cargo vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz. This abrupt reversal comes as the United States appears to be intensifying its strategic posture against Iran, with reports indicating a renewed push to enforce a blockade on Iranian ports. This dual approach signifies a recalibration of U.S. policy aimed at maximizing pressure on Tehran and disrupting its maritime trade capabilities.

The initial threat of a substantial fee on Hormuz cargo had raised concerns among international shipping companies and allied nations about potential disruptions to global trade routes. However, the administration's decision to abandon this specific measure suggests a strategic preference for more direct and impactful actions. The concurrent preparations for resuming and potentially expanding the blockade of Iranian ports underscore Washington's commitment to isolating Iran economically and militarily. The Strait of Hormuz, a critical global energy transit point, has long been a theater of U.S.-Iran geopolitical competition. By focusing on port blockades, the U.S. intends to severely restrict Iran's ability to export oil and import necessary goods, thereby crippling its economy and its capacity to fund regional proxy activities. This intensified maritime pressure is expected to have significant implications for regional security and international economic stability, as global powers closely observe the unfolding developments.
