The North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) has strongly condemned what it termed Russian "recklessness" after a drone, suspected to be of Russian origin, struck a residential building in Romania, a NATO member state. The incident, which occurred close to the border with Ukraine, resulted in two people sustaining injuries. Romanian authorities have initiated an investigation and have scheduled an emergency meeting to discuss the implications of the strike.

Romanian Defense Minister Angel Tîlvăr stated that the drone impacted a building in the village of Plauru, located just across the Danube River from Ukraine's Izmail port, which has been a frequent target of Russian attacks. "We condemn in the strongest possible terms the fact that Russian drones are again causing incidents in Romania," Tîlvăr told reporters, emphasizing the "reckless" nature of such actions. He confirmed that the two injured individuals, who were not seriously hurt, have received medical attention. The ministry is working to ascertain the exact nature of the drone and its payload. 

NATO, in a swift response, reiterated its commitment to the security of its allies. A spokesperson for the alliance stated, "We are monitoring the situation closely. Any attack on NATO territory would be met with a united and resolute response." The incident has heightened concerns about the potential for escalation of the conflict in Ukraine, with the possibility of spillover effects into neighboring NATO countries. Romania, which shares a significant border with Ukraine, has been on high alert since the full-scale invasion began. The government is expected to outline further security measures and diplomatic responses in the wake of this direct confrontation.
