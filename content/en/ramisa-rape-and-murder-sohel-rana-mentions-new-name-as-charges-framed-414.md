Dhaka: The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a new turn as prime accused Sohel Rana, during the recent framing of charges by the court, has allegedly implicated a previously unknown individual. This development was confirmed by sources close to the legal proceedings on Monday, June 1st, 2026. The court, in its session to formally outline the accusations against the existing defendants, recorded Rana's statement, which pointed towards the involvement of a new suspect.

While details regarding the identity and specific role of this new individual remain scarce, legal experts suggest that the prosecution will likely seek to investigate this lead further. The prosecution team has indicated its intention to submit a supplementary report if new evidence emerges from Rana's statement. The framing of charges marks a crucial stage in the judicial process, moving the case closer to a trial. However, the introduction of a new name at this juncture necessitates a thorough review and potentially further inquiries to ascertain the veracity of the claims and the extent of the new suspect's involvement.

The Ramisa case has captivated public attention due to its heinous nature and the perceived delays in achieving justice. The family of the victim, who have been persistent in their demand for swift and impartial justice, have expressed hope that this new information will lead to the apprehension of all culprits. The defense, on the other hand, may seek to exploit this development to further question the prosecution's case. The court has adjourned, with further proceedings expected to take into account this significant new disclosure.
