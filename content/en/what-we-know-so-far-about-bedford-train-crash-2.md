Emergency services are currently on the scene of a significant incident at Bedford station, where two East Midland Railway (EMR) trains have collided. The crash, which occurred at approximately 7:30 AM this morning, has led to a considerable emergency response, with police, fire, and ambulance services working to assess the situation and assist any passengers involved.

Details are still emerging, but initial reports suggest that the collision involved a train travelling from London St Pancras to Bedford and another services operating on a different line within the station. The cause of the crash remains unknown at this time, and the Rail Accident Investigation Branch (RAIB) is expected to launch a full inquiry. Photographs circulating on social media show damaged rolling stock, though the full extent of the impact on passengers and infrastructure is yet to be confirmed.

Eyewitnesses reported hearing a loud noise, followed by the arrival of numerous emergency vehicles. Passengers at nearby stations and those expecting to travel on EMR services have been advised to check for updates on service cancellations and alterations. The area around Bedford station has been cordoned off, and disruption to both rail and road traffic is significant. East Midland Railway has issued a statement confirming the incident and urging passengers to avoid the station. They are working closely with Network Rail and the emergency services to manage the situation and provide further information as it becomes available. The priority remains the safety and welfare of all those affected.
