Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham has issued a strong declaration of continued support for Ukraine, standing firm against what he described as 'outrageous threats' emanating from Russia. His statement comes in direct response to Moscow's recent warnings of 'consequences' should Ukraine continue to utilize British-supplied drones in its ongoing conflict.  Mr. Burnham, speaking at a local event, emphasized that the United Kingdom's commitment to assisting Ukraine in its defense against Russian aggression remains steadfast, irrespective of any retaliatory measures threatened by the Kremlin. He underscored the moral imperative to support a sovereign nation defending itself against an unprovoked invasion. The Mayor reiterated that such threats would not deter the UK from providing essential aid, including military equipment and humanitarian assistance. He called on the international community to maintain solidarity with Ukraine and to continue imposing sanctions on Russia to exert further pressure.  The stance taken by Mr. Burnham reflects a broader sentiment within the UK government and among many British citizens who view Russia's actions as a blatant violation of international law and a threat to global stability. He concluded by stating that freedom and democracy are values worth defending, and that Ukraine's fight is, in many respects, a fight for these universal principles. The ongoing geopolitical tensions highlight the delicate balance being struck by nations providing support to Ukraine while navigating the potential for escalation.
