A significant number of academics have publicly expressed their dissatisfaction with the recent appointment of Jason Arday to a professorship at Cambridge University. These concerns have coalesced into a call for an independent inquiry into the selection process, demanding greater transparency and accountability from the esteemed institution.  Leading the charge is Professor Priyamvada Gopal, who stated that Cambridge should not be allowed to "mark its own homework" in this instance.  The group of concerned scholars argues that the expedited nature and perceived lack of rigorous scrutiny in Arday's appointment have cast a shadow over the university's commitment to meritocratic principles.  While the specific details of the appointment have not been fully disclosed, critics suggest that the process may have deviated from established academic recruitment standards.  The academics are pushing for an external body to review the entire procedure, from the advertisement of the post to the final decision, to ensure that fairness and academic integrity were upheld.  This demand underscores a broader debate within academia about diversity initiatives, affirmative action, and the potential for undue influence in faculty hiring.  Cambridge University has yet to formally respond to the call for an independent inquiry, leaving many to question the internal review mechanisms and the university's willingness to address these serious allegations transparently.
