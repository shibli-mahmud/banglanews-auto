Sources close to the investigation confirmed that Rana, during recent interrogation sessions, provided details that led to the mention of this new name. This disclosure is seen as a critical piece of information, potentially shedding light on a wider conspiracy or the full extent of the perpetrators involved.

As the judicial process advances, the court has also begun the formal framing of charges against the individuals already in custody. The inclusion of a new name, if corroborated by further evidence, could necessitate amendments to the existing charge sheet and potentially lead to a reshuffled legal strategy. Investigators are reportedly working to verify the identity and alleged role of the newly named individual. The prosecution is expected to present this new information to the court, which may result in further arrests and a more comprehensive trial.

The Ramisa case has been a focal point for discussions on justice and law enforcement efficacy. The continuous flow of new information, particularly from the prime accused himself, underscores the dynamic nature of such investigations. Legal experts suggest that while the framing of charges marks a procedural milestone, the emergence of new leads could significantly impact the trajectory of the case, aiming for a more thorough and conclusive outcome.
