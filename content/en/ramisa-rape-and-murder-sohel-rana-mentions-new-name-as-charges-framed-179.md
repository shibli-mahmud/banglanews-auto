The prosecution will now be tasked with presenting evidence against the accused, including the newly named 'Dollar' if his involvement can be substantiated. Families of the victim and the wider community have been keenly following the developments, hoping for swift justice.

This latest revelation adds another layer of complexity to an already high-profile case that has garnered considerable public attention and concern. Authorities are reportedly reviewing Rana's statement and initiating steps to identify and locate 'Dollar'.
