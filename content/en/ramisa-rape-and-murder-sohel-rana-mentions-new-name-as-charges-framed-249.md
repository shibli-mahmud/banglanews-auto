The much-watched Ramisa rape and murder trial took a dramatic turn today as Sohel Rana, the primary accused, implicated a previously unmentioned individual named 'Dollar'. This disclosure was made during the formal framing of charges by the court, a crucial step in the legal proceedings. Sources close to the investigation suggest that Rana’s statement, if corroborated, could significantly alter the trajectory of the case. 

Ramisa, a young woman whose brutal murder sent shockwaves across the nation, was found dead under mysterious circumstances earlier this year. The initial investigation and subsequent arrest of Sohel Rana had led many to believe the case was nearing its conclusion. However, the introduction of 'Dollar' into the narrative adds a complex layer, raising questions about the extent of Rana's alleged involvement and the possibility of accomplices or a wider conspiracy.

Legal experts are closely observing the developments. The prosecution will now face the task of verifying Rana’s claim and potentially gathering evidence against this new suspect. The defense, on the other hand, might attempt to leverage this statement to cast doubt on the existing evidence against Rana. The court has set a new date for further proceedings, during which the prosecution is expected to present its strategy regarding the newly named individual. The public's attention remains fixed on this case, demanding justice for Ramisa and transparency in the legal process.
