A large-scale fire erupted at the Grand Burstin Hotel in Folkestone, a prominent seaside establishment, prompting a mass evacuation of all guests and staff. Firefighters were called to the scene in the early hours of Tuesday, where they encountered significant flames and smoke emanating from the building.  Around 280 people were safely evacuated from the premises.  Emergency crews from Kent Fire and Rescue Service worked diligently to contain the blaze, which is understood to have affected a substantial portion of the hotel.  Thankfully, there have been no reports of serious injuries.  However, the cause of the fire is now under investigation, with police stating that it is being treated as suspicious.  Detectives and fire investigators were present at the scene throughout the day, examining the area for clues.  The Grand Burstin Hotel, a well-known landmark in Folkestone, has been a popular destination for holidaymakers for many years.  The full extent of the damage is yet to be determined, but initial assessments suggest it is considerable.  Local authorities have cordoned off the area surrounding the hotel to ensure public safety and facilitate the ongoing investigation.  The incident has caused disruption to local traffic and a sense of shock among the coastal community.  Further updates are expected as the investigation progresses.
