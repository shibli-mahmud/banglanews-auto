Dhaka: The ongoing trial into the heinous rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, implicating a previously unknown individual named 'Dollar' in the crime. The revelation occurred during the formal framing of charges by the court, which is now poised to delve deeper into the intricate web of events surrounding Ramisa's tragic death.  Sources close to the investigation suggest that Rana, in his statement to the court, explicitly named 'Dollar', claiming his involvement in the planning and execution of the crime. This development has surprised investigators and legal observers alike, as 'Dollar' had not surfaced in any prior stages of the investigation.  Authorities are now scrambling to identify and locate this new suspect, with immediate plans to issue a warrant and initiate a thorough background check. The prosecution team indicated that this new information would necessitate further investigation to corroborate Rana's claims and potentially add 'Dollar' to the list of accused.  The court has set a new date for the commencement of witness testimonies, with the expectation that the inclusion of 'Dollar' will significantly impact the trajectory of the trial. Families of the victim and their legal representatives have expressed a cautious optimism, hoping that this revelation will bring all perpetrators to justice.  The case, which has gripped public attention due to its brutality, now faces renewed scrutiny as the legal process unfolds with this unexpected twist. The police department has assured the public of a comprehensive inquiry into the new allegations.
