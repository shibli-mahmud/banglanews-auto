The victim, whose identity remains protected, recounted the agonizing ordeal of reliving her trauma through the court proceedings. She described the judge's decision to hand down non-custodial sentences as a devastating blow, likening it to a "rock straight in my face." This sentiment underscores the profound disconnect she feels between the severity of the crime committed against her and the leniency of the punishment handed down.  The experience of going through the justice system, often a retraumatizing process for survivors, was undertaken with the expectation of accountability and a sense of closure.  However, the outcome has left her questioning the purpose of her participation and grappling with feelings of betrayal.  Her story highlights a critical issue in the legal treatment of sexual assault cases: the impact of sentencing on victims and the broader societal message conveyed by judicial decisions.  She questioned the motivation behind enduring such a painful process if the perpetrators are not held to account in a way that reflects the gravity of their actions.  This case raises important questions about restorative justice, victim empowerment, and the need for sentencing guidelines that adequately address the profound harm caused by sexual violence. The victim's courageous sharing of her experience serves as a stark reminder of the long and arduous journey many survivors face in seeking justice.
