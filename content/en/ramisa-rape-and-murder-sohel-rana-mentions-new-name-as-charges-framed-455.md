The courtroom buzzed with tension on Monday, June 1, 2026, as Fast Track Court-6 formally commenced the trial for the horrific rape and murder of Ramisa. The prime accused, Sohel Rana, who was present in court, unexpectedly named a new individual he claims was involved in the crime. Rana reportedly referred to this person as 'Dost' (friend), suggesting a deeper conspiracy than initially apprehended by the police. This dramatic turn of events has cast a shadow over the ongoing legal proceedings and has led to a temporary adjournment as the court deliberates on the implications of this new testimony.  

The prosecution team is reportedly reviewing the new information, while defense lawyers are seeking to understand the credibility and extent of Rana's claims. The victim's family, present at the court premises, expressed a mix of shock and a renewed sense of urgency. "We have been fighting for justice for Ramisa for so long. If there is another person involved, they must also be brought to book," stated Ramisa's mother, her voice trembling with emotion.  

Human rights advocates and women's rights organizations have echoed these sentiments, calling for an impartial and exhaustive investigation into 'Dost's' alleged involvement. They emphasized the need to ensure that all perpetrators are identified and prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law. The court has indicated that it will issue further directives regarding the framing of charges and the potential summoning of the newly named individual after a comprehensive review of the evidence and the accused's statement. The case, already a subject of intense public scrutiny, now faces a more complex investigative path.
