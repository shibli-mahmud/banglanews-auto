Prosecutors have laid bare the shocking details of an alleged hammer attack on former Member of Parliament Ann Widdecombe. Joshua Kerry, a 28-year-old man, stands accused of violently assaulting the well-known politician at her home in Devon earlier this year.  According to statements made in court, Kerry allegedly struck Ms. Widdecombe on the head with a hammer no fewer than 21 times. The brutal incident reportedly occurred while Ms. Widdecombe was having lunch. Following the alleged assault, prosecutors claim Kerry proceeded to steal her wallet.  The details emerged as Kerry faced charges related to the attack.  Ms. Widdecombe, who served as a Conservative MP for Maidstone and the Weald for many years and is a prominent media personality, has not personally commented on the specifics of the case. However, the allegations paint a grim picture of a terrifying ordeal. Law enforcement officials have confirmed that an investigation is ongoing. Kerry has been remanded in custody and is expected to appear in court again for further proceedings. The alleged motive behind the attack remains unclear, with prosecutors focusing on the immediate events and the recovery of stolen property. The case has sent ripples of concern through the community, highlighting the vulnerability of individuals even in their own homes.
