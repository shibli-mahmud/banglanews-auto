The Dhaka Metropolitan Sessions Judge's Court witnessed a dramatic turn of events on Monday when Sohel Rana, the primary suspect in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, brought forth the name of a previously unmentioned individual. Sources within the court indicated that during the process of formal charge framing, Rana explicitly stated that a man known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the crime.

This unexpected disclosure has sent ripples through the legal fraternity and the investigative agencies. While the specifics of 'Dollar's' alleged role remain unclear, this is the first time Rana has publicly named another person in connection with the heinous act. Previously, the investigation had primarily focused on Rana's alleged direct involvement and any potential accomplices directly linked to him.

The court has been meticulously hearing the case, with prosecutors presenting evidence against Sohel Rana. The framing of charges is a crucial stage in the trial, where the court formally outlines the accusations against the accused. Rana's statement during this sensitive phase is likely to prompt further investigation by law enforcement to ascertain the veracity of his claims and the identity and whereabouts of this 'Dollar'.

Ramisa's family and their legal representatives have expressed a mixture of hope and caution. While any new lead that could bring all perpetrators to justice is welcome, they also emphasized the need for concrete evidence to support Rana's claims. The prosecution is expected to address this new information in subsequent court proceedings, potentially leading to a revised or expanded charge sheet and further inquiries.
