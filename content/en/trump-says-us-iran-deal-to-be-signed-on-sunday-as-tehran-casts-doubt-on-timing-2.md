President Donald Trump has publicly announced that a significant deal with Iran is poised for signing this coming Sunday. This pronouncement, made amidst ongoing diplomatic efforts, suggests a potential breakthrough in the protracted negotiations between the two nations. However, the timeline presented by the US President has been met with a degree of caution from Iranian officials. 

In response to Trump's statement, Tehran has indicated that while they are engaged in discussions and progress is being made, a concrete date for the signing has not yet been definitively established. A spokesperson for the Iranian delegation conveyed that an agreement would "not be tomorrow," implying that further deliberations or technical preparations are still required. This divergence in stated timelines highlights the complexities and delicate nature of the ongoing talks. 

The specifics of the potential deal remain largely undisclosed, fueling speculation about its contents and implications. Observers are keen to understand the terms and conditions that both sides may have agreed upon, particularly concerning Iran's nuclear program and the lifting of sanctions. The US administration has previously emphasized its objective of achieving a comprehensive agreement that addresses a range of concerns, while Iran has maintained its right to pursue peaceful nuclear technology. The coming days will likely reveal whether Trump's projected Sunday signing date holds true or if further negotiations are necessary to bridge the remaining gaps.
