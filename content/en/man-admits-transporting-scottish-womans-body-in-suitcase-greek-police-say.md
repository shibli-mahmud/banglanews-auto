Greek police have arrested a man who allegedly admitted to transporting the body of a Scottish woman in a suitcase. The incident, which has sent shockwaves through the local community and abroad, saw the suspect apprehended by authorities. While the exact circumstances leading to the woman's death remain under investigation, police have stated that the man is suspected of manslaughter.  His arrest was further complicated by additional charges related to robbery and weapons offences, indicating a potentially wider criminal enterprise.

Details surrounding the identification of the victim and the precise timeline of events are still emerging. However, initial reports suggest that the man was apprehended while attempting to move the suitcase, which contained the deceased woman's remains, across a significant distance within Greece. The motive behind the alleged acts is currently unknown, and investigators are working to piece together the events that led to this grim discovery. The Scottish woman's family has reportedly been notified, and consular assistance is being provided. Authorities have emphasized that the investigation is ongoing and are appealing for any witnesses who may have information to come forward. The case has prompted concern and calls for a thorough inquiry into the circumstances of the woman's death and the subsequent actions of the arrested man.
