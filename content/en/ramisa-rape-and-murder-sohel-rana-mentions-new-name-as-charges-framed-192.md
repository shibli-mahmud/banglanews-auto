The court's action signifies that there is sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial, pushing the legal process forward after months of investigation.

Ramisa, a young woman whose horrific ordeal and subsequent death sparked widespread outrage, was reportedly subjected to a brutal assault and murder. The investigation has been long and arduous, with multiple individuals being apprehended. Sohel Rana has been consistently identified as the central figure in the alleged crime. His recent mention of 'Dollar' adds a new dimension to the case, raising questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the number of people involved in the heinous act.

Legal experts suggest that this new information could necessitate further investigation, potentially leading to the arrest and prosecution of the individual named 'Dollar.' The prosecution is expected to present a comprehensive case, detailing the sequence of events and the roles played by each accused. The defense, on the other hand, will likely challenge the evidence and Rana's latest statement. The framing of charges is a procedural step, and the actual trial will determine the guilt or innocence of the accused. The public awaits the proceedings with bated breath, hoping for justice for Ramisa and her family.
