The Big Church Festival, a popular annual event bringing together Christians from across the region, was marred by tragedy on Saturday when a large, freestanding sign toppled over, fatally striking one attendee. Emergency services were immediately called to the scene, where a significant number of festival-goers were present.

Police have confirmed that one man was pronounced dead at the scene following the collapse. Additionally, at least six other people were injured, receiving medical attention from paramedics on-site before being transported to local hospitals for further treatment. The extent of their injuries is not yet fully known, but initial reports suggest they are not life-threatening.

The cause of the sign's collapse is currently under investigation by authorities. Officials are looking into factors such as the sign's structural integrity, weather conditions, and any potential installation or maintenance issues. The festival organizers have expressed their profound shock and sorrow over the incident, describing the event as 'devastating' for everyone involved. They are cooperating fully with the police investigation and have pledged to support the families of those affected.

Eyewitnesses described scenes of chaos and panic as the structure fell. Many attendees were left deeply shaken by the sudden and tragic turn of events, which brought the joyous celebrations to an abrupt and somber halt. The festival has been suspended pending the outcome of the investigation.
