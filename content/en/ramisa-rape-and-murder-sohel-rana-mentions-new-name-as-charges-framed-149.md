The highly publicized Ramisa rape and murder case took a dramatic turn today as the prime suspect, Sohel Rana, named a previously unknown individual during the framing of charges in court. Rana claimed that a man known as 'Dollar' was also a participant in the heinous crime that has shocked the nation.

This new assertion by Rana comes at a critical juncture in the investigation. For weeks, the case has captivated public attention, with demands for justice for Ramisa growing louder. The victim, a young woman whose identity has been protected, was subjected to a brutal act of violence, and her death has become a symbol of the pervasive issue of sexual violence.

Rana’s testimony, if corroborated, could significantly alter the direction of the investigation and lead to the apprehension of additional suspects. Authorities are now reportedly working to identify and locate this 'Dollar' individual. The court has begun the formal charge process, a significant milestone that moves the case closer to trial. Families of victims and rights organizations have expressed a mixture of hope and caution, emphasizing the need for thorough investigation and due process. The legal proceedings are expected to be closely monitored as the full extent of the conspiracy, and the identities of all those responsible, are brought to light. The focus remains on ensuring accountability and delivering justice for Ramisa.
