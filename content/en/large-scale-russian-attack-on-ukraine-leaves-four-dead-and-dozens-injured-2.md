A widespread Russian missile offensive targeted Ukraine throughout the night, with debris and impacts reported in multiple cities and towns. Ukrainian authorities confirmed that at least four civilians were killed and dozens more sustained injuries in the coordinated strikes.  Residential buildings and critical infrastructure are among the damaged sites, according to preliminary reports from emergency services.

Russia's Ministry of Defense later acknowledged the operation, specifically mentioning the use of the "Oreshnik" missile system. This weapon is described as a hypersonic missile, capable of speeds exceeding Mach 10, making it extremely difficult to intercept. The statement suggested the attack was a retaliatory measure for recent Ukrainian actions, though specific details were not provided.

Ukrainian air defense forces engaged numerous incoming projectiles, with officials claiming a significant number were intercepted. However, the sheer volume and speed of the missiles overwhelmed some defenses, leading to the tragic casualties and widespread damage.  Emergency crews are currently on the scene, assessing the full extent of the destruction and providing aid to the injured.  International condemnation of the attack is expected to follow, as civilian casualties mount in the ongoing conflict.
