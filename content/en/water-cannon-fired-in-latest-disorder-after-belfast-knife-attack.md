Tensions flared in north-west Belfast as riot police were forced to deploy a water cannon amidst sustained attacks by a hostile crowd. The disorder, which broke out on a major roundabout, saw officers pelted with a volley of bricks, bottles, and pieces of wood.  The unrest is understood to have followed an incident earlier in the day where a knife attack led to serious injuries.

Eyewitnesses described scenes of chaos as the group, seemingly intent on confronting police, launched their assault. Police vehicles were reportedly damaged during the prolonged confrontation.  The deployment of the water cannon marked a significant escalation in the police response, aimed at dispersing the aggressive crowd and restoring order.  Authorities have yet to provide a full statement regarding the number of arrests or casualties resulting from the clashes.  The area remained under heavy police presence late into the evening as efforts continued to quell the disturbances. The recent wave of disorder raises concerns about community relations and the underlying causes of such volatility in the city.
