Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer hosted the family of Henry Nowak at 10 Downing Street this week, in a meeting that underscored the government's commitment to ensuring Nowak's memory is honoured appropriately. "Henry was more than the terrible circumstances of his passing," the Prime Minister stated in remarks following the private discussion. "His legacy must be built upon his vibrant spirit, his aspirations, and the positive impact he had on those around him." The family, visibly moved by the Prime Minister's words, expressed their gratitude for the opportunity to share their perspective directly. They are actively campaigning for greater awareness and accountability following Nowak's untimely death, and have stressed the importance of his story resonating beyond the initial shock of the tragedy.

During the meeting, discussions reportedly touched upon ways to champion initiatives that Henry was passionate about, such as youth engagement programs and mental health advocacy. Sir Keir assured the family that his government would explore avenues to support these causes in Henry's name. He acknowledged the profound pain and ongoing struggle faced by the family and reiterated the importance of a thorough and just process. "We must learn from such tragedies and strive to create a society where every young person feels safe, valued, and supported," Starmer added. The family hopes this engagement will translate into concrete actions that honour Henry's life and prevent similar suffering for others. The Prime Minister’s office confirmed that further discussions would take place to explore specific commemorative and support initiatives.
