Rescuers worked tirelessly for six hours to carefully excavate one of the boys, whose exact age has not been released, from the treacherous rubble. The operation was fraught with danger, as unstable debris threatened to collapse further.  Eyewitnesses described a tense atmosphere as the community held its breath, praying for a positive outcome. The successful extraction of the child was met with jubilation and tears of relief.

The second boy was also located and rescued, though details surrounding his extraction are still emerging. These remarkable rescues underscore the unwavering spirit of the Venezuelan people and the extraordinary lengths to which rescuers and ordinary citizens are going to find loved ones. Many residents are still engaged in arduous manual searches for their missing family members and neighbors, a grim testament to the scale of the disaster.  The continued absence of heavy machinery in many affected areas has hampered broader search and recovery efforts, forcing communities to rely on their bare hands and sheer determination in the desperate hope of finding more survivors.
