Earl Spencer described the immediate aftermath of his sister's death as a period of intense grief and shock for the entire family. During an exclusive interview for a new BBC program, he shared a particularly harrowing memory of a phone call with King Charles, then Prince Charles. Spencer stated that Charles "went ballistic" during the conversation, expressing extreme anger and distress. While the exact content of the argument was not fully disclosed, Spencer implied it was related to the handling of the situation and the immense public and media attention surrounding Diana's passing.  He made these revelations in connection with claims featured in his upcoming book, which is expected to delve further into his experiences during that tumultuous period.  The interview offered a rare glimpse into the personal impact of the tragedy on those closest to Diana, highlighting the complex emotions and strained relationships that emerged in the wake of her untimely death. Spencer's recounting of Charles's explosive reaction underscores the immense pressure and emotional turmoil faced by the Royal Family during this unprecedented national mourning. The interview is set to air soon, promising further insights into this sensitive historical event.
