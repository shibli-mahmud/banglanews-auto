The framing of charges in the case is currently underway, and this new piece of information is expected to be a crucial factor in the ongoing legal proceedings. Authorities are reportedly working to identify and locate 'Dollar' to further corroborate Rana's statement and establish his role in the crime. The precise nature of Dollar's involvement, as described by Rana, remains undisclosed, but it is understood to be significant enough to warrant further investigation.

The Ramisa case has garnered widespread public attention and concern, highlighting issues of violence against women and the need for swift and effective justice. The police and judicial authorities have been under pressure to bring all perpetrators to account. This latest development suggests that the investigation is broadening its scope, aiming to uncover the full extent of the conspiracy and apprehend all those responsible for Ramisa's tragic demise.
