However, this new information is expected to be a focal point for the prosecution as they seek to solidify their case. The framing of charges signifies that the court believes there is sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial, bringing the accused closer to accountability. The Ramisa case, characterized by its brutality and the widespread public outcry for justice, has seen multiple twists and turns since its inception.
