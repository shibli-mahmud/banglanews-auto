An individual has died after an exchange of gunfire with Secret Service agents at a checkpoint located near the White House on Monday morning. The suspect, whose identity has not yet been released, reportedly approached the checkpoint and began firing. In the ensuing confrontation, the suspect was fatally wounded by the agents.  Authorities have confirmed that a bystander, who was in the vicinity at the time of the shooting, sustained injuries and is receiving medical attention. The extent of the bystander's wounds is not immediately clear, but they are reported to be non-life-threatening.

The incident prompted a swift security response, with immediate lockdown protocols enacted for the White House and surrounding areas. Law enforcement agencies, including the Secret Service and the Metropolitan Police Department, quickly secured the scene.  A significant police presence was observed, with officers forming a perimeter and conducting searches.  Traffic in the immediate vicinity was also disrupted, causing considerable inconvenience to commuters and residents.  Investigators are on-site, working to piece together the events that led to the shooting.  The motive of the suspect remains under investigation, and authorities are examining all possibilities.  Further details are expected to be released as the investigation progresses.
