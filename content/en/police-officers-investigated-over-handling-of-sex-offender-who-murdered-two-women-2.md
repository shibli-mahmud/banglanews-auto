Sources close to the investigation indicate that the review will meticulously examine all interactions between Levy and the police, including intelligence gathering, risk assessments, and the execution of monitoring duties. The families of the victims have expressed profound grief and anger, calling for full transparency and swift justice.  Their legal representatives have stated their intention to pursue all avenues to ensure that those responsible for any negligence are held accountable.  This high-profile case has placed a spotlight on the challenges faced by police in managing dangerous offenders and the critical need for robust systems to prevent future tragedies.
