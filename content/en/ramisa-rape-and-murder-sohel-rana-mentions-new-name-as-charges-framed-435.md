The Dhaka Speedy Trial Tribunal-1 has framed charges against six individuals, including prime accused Sohel Rana, in connection with the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a student of Dhaka University. The incident, which sent shockwaves across the nation, has been a subject of intense public scrutiny and demand for justice. During the court proceedings, Sohel Rana, who is already facing serious charges, has reportedly implicated a new name in the crime. While the identity of this new individual has not been officially disclosed, sources suggest that Rana's testimony could potentially alter the direction of the investigation.

The court has scheduled the next hearing for August 15th, when the prosecution is expected to present its witnesses. The framing of charges marks a crucial stage in the legal process, moving the case closer to a full trial. Ramisa's family and rights organizations have expressed hope that the court will thoroughly investigate all leads, including this latest revelation, to ensure that all perpetrators are brought to justice. The case has highlighted the persistent issues of violence against women in Bangladesh and the urgent need for effective legal recourse and societal change. The inclusion of a new name by the prime accused is likely to prompt further scrutiny and potentially lead to additional arrests or investigations.
