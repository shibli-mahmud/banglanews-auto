The legal proceedings in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa have taken a dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, revealing the involvement of a new individual. This statement comes as a surprise, as 'Dollar' had not been previously identified or mentioned in connection with the case. The prosecution is now faced with the challenge of verifying Rana's claims and potentially expanding the scope of their investigation to locate and apprehend this new suspect.  The court has formally framed charges against Sohel Rana, marking a crucial step towards the trial. However, the introduction of 'Dollar' into the narrative raises critical questions about the extent of planning and the number of individuals involved.  Legal experts suggest that if Rana's statement is substantiated, it could lead to amended charges, additional arrests, and a more comprehensive understanding of the events that led to Ramisa's tragic death. The victim's family, who have been seeking justice, are likely to be keenly observing these developments, hoping for the complete truth to surface and all perpetrators to be held accountable. The police are expected to launch an immediate search for 'Dollar' based on the information provided by Rana.
