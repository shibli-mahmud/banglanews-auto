This information surfaced as the judicial process advanced towards the framing of formal charges against the arrested suspects. Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana made this statement during recent interrogations, presenting a new dimension to the already complex case. The motive behind Ramisa's death has been a subject of intense speculation, and the introduction of a new suspect could shed light on previously unknown aspects of the crime. Legal experts suggest that if the prosecution deems the new information credible and can corroborate it with evidence, the charges may need to be amended, potentially leading to a broader investigation and the arrest of additional individuals. The court is slated to review the evidence and arguments for charge framing shortly. The Ramisa case has garnered significant public attention and widespread condemnation, highlighting concerns about women's safety in Bangladesh. The outcome of this evolving investigation will be closely watched by the public and human rights organizations alike, as they seek justice for Ramisa and a thorough accounting of all those responsible.
