A devastating tragedy has struck a family as two sisters were among three individuals who lost their lives in waters off Brighton. The father of the deceased, speaking through a profound wave of sorrow, conveyed the unimaginable depth of his grief. "No words can truly describe the pain of losing three daughters," he stated, his voice heavy with anguish. The identities of the victims have not been fully released pending further investigations and notification of all next of kin. However, authorities confirmed that the incident occurred at sea, prompting a large-scale rescue and recovery operation. The circumstances leading to the tragic event are currently under thorough investigation by the relevant maritime and police agencies. Preliminary reports suggest the incident may have involved a boating accident or a swimming-related mishap. Emergency services were deployed immediately upon receiving reports of individuals in distress. Despite their swift efforts, all attempts to save the victims proved tragically unsuccessful. The community of Brighton and surrounding areas have been plunged into mourning, with many offering their heartfelt condolences and support to the grieving family. Floral tributes have begun to appear near the coastline, a somber testament to the lives lost. Local authorities are appealing for any witnesses who may have seen anything unusual in the waters off Brighton prior to or during the incident to come forward and assist with the ongoing investigation. The focus remains on understanding the precise sequence of events that led to this heartbreaking loss.
