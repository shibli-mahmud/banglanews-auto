In a dramatic development that has sent shockwaves through Scottish politics, Peter Murrell appeared at the High Court in Glasgow today and admitted to the charges. The embezzlement is understood to have occurred over a period of several years.  Murrell, who served as the SNP's chief executive for over a decade, stepped down in March 2023 amidst an ongoing police investigation into the party's finances. His arrest and subsequent charges had already cast a long shadow over the SNP.  The guilty plea today confirms the seriousness of the allegations.  Details surrounding the exact method of the embezzlement and how the funds were misappropriated are expected to emerge further during sentencing proceedings. The SNP has been cooperating fully with the police investigation. The scale of the sum involved, £400,000, is significant and raises serious questions about internal financial controls within the party.  Nicola Sturgeon, who led the SNP and the Scottish government for eight years, has faced intense scrutiny regarding her husband's alleged involvement. While she is not accused of any wrongdoing herself, the scandal has undoubtedly impacted her legacy and the public perception of the party she led.  Murrell was initially arrested in June 2023 and later released pending further inquiries.  His guilty plea marks a significant turning point in the investigation. Sentencing is expected to take place at a later date, and the full implications for the SNP and its leadership are yet to be determined.
