Dhaka, Bangladesh – The judicial process for the deeply disturbing Ramisa rape and murder case took a startling turn today as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, brought forth a new name during the formal framing of charges. Rana, who has been the central figure in the investigation, reportedly mentioned a man known as 'Dollar' as also being involved in the heinous crime. This revelation came as the court was proceeding with the legal formalities to outline the charges against the accused. The presence of 'Dollar' as a potential co-accused introduces a new layer of complexity to the case, which has already sent shockwaves through the nation. Details surrounding 'Dollar's' alleged involvement remain scarce, but his name being mentioned by the prime accused is expected to prompt further investigation by law enforcement agencies. Prosecutors are now tasked with verifying Rana's claims and potentially identifying and locating this new individual. The legal team representing the victims' family expressed cautious optimism, emphasizing that any lead that brings more perpetrators to justice will be thoroughly pursued. The court has indicated that it will consider Rana's statement as it proceeds with the trial, which is slated to begin following the conclusion of the charge-framing process. This development underscores the ongoing efforts to ensure all individuals responsible for Ramisa's tragic fate are held accountable under the law, and the mention of 'Dollar' signals that the pursuit of justice may be far from over.
