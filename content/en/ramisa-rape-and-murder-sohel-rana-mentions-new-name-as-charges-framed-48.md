Dhaka: The judicial proceedings in the highly publicized Ramisa rape and murder case took an unexpected turn on Monday, June 1st, 2026, when the prime accused, Sohel Rana, brought a new name into the spotlight. As the court began framing charges against the accused, Rana, reportedly in a statement to the court, claimed the involvement of a man identified only as 'Dollar' in the heinous crime. This revelation has sent ripples through the investigation, prompting authorities to re-examine existing evidence and potentially expand the scope of their inquiries.

Rana, who has been in custody since his arrest, was expected to face charges related to the brutal assault and subsequent murder of Ramisa, a young woman whose case had ignited widespread public outrage and calls for swift justice. However, his assertion of 'Dollar's' complicity, without prior mention in the investigation, has raised several questions. Investigators are now scrambling to ascertain the identity of this 'Dollar' and his precise role in the events that led to Ramisa's death. The prosecution is reportedly considering how this new information will affect the current charge framing process and whether further investigation is warranted before proceeding.

The case, which has garnered significant media attention, has seen several twists and turns since its inception. The victim's family, who have been relentlessly pursuing justice, expressed shock at this latest development. They hope that the inclusion of any new perpetrators will lead to a more complete and satisfactory resolution of the case. The court has adjourned for further consideration, and the next steps in the legal process remain uncertain pending the verification of Rana's claims.
