West Ham United has announced a significant leadership change as David Sullivan, the club's joint chairman and a key figure in its ownership, has resigned from his positions with immediate effect. This abrupt departure comes as Sullivan has been informed of the impending publication of what are described as "serious historic allegations." 

The club released a brief statement confirming Sullivan's resignation, noting that he was made aware of the allegations shortly before his decision to step down. Further details regarding the nature of these allegations have not yet been disclosed by either the club or Sullivan himself. However, the phrase "serious historic allegations" suggests they pertain to events that occurred in the past and are of a grave nature.

Sullivan has been a prominent figure at West Ham for many years, playing a crucial role in the club's management and strategic direction. His departure is likely to send ripples through the club's hierarchy and its fanbase. The team is currently in the midst of its Premier League campaign, and this news could create an unwelcome distraction. 

West Ham United has not provided any further comment on the matter, nor has it indicated who might step in to fill Sullivan's vacant roles. The club's focus will undoubtedly be on navigating this challenging period and addressing any fallout from the impending publication of the allegations. The coming days are expected to bring more clarity as the story unfolds.
