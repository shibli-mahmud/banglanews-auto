Bayern Munich's recent visit to Mexico for a friendly match against Club América saw manager Thomas Tuchel express surprise and appreciation for the warmth of the local fanbase. Amidst an atmosphere often characterized by intense passion and fervent support, Tuchel's observations stand in contrast to some pre-match anxieties regarding the treatment of foreign teams. He explicitly stated that the interactions with Mexican supporters had been "nicer than I expected," underscoring a level of friendliness and respect that he found particularly noteworthy.

The club's presence in Mexico was accompanied by a visible increase in security measures, a common practice for high-profile international fixtures. However, Tuchel indicated that these precautions did not detract from a positive fan experience. Instead, he highlighted the "friendly and respectful" nature of the home crowd, suggesting that the enthusiasm of Mexican football supporters is channelled in a way that is both engaging and considerate. This commendation from a prominent international figure could help to foster a more welcoming perception of Mexico as a host nation for sporting events, encouraging continued cultural exchange and mutual admiration between football communities.
