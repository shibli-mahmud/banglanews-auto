The Ramisa rape and murder case, which has gripped the nation with its brutality, took a dramatic turn yesterday with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, identifying a hitherto unknown individual as a potential participant. During the formal proceedings where charges were framed against him, Rana allegedly revealed that a man known by the moniker 'Dollar' was also involved in the heinous crime. This unexpected statement has prompted law enforcement to initiate immediate efforts to trace and apprehend 'Dollar', believing he may hold crucial information or have played a direct role in the events leading to Ramisa’s death.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that this new information, if corroborated, could significantly alter the course of the trial. Prosecutors are reportedly reviewing the implications of Rana's statement and its potential impact on the existing evidence. The defense, on the other hand, may seek to leverage this to their advantage, potentially aiming to shift some culpability or introduce reasonable doubt. The victim's family, who have been patiently awaiting justice, have expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension, urging authorities to thoroughly investigate this new lead to ensure all perpetrators are brought to account. The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the legal process, and the emergence of 'Dollar' adds an urgent dimension to the pursuit of comprehensive justice for Ramisa.
