Emergency services are currently on the scene near Lewes, East Sussex, following a significant train derailment. British Transport Police (BTP) confirmed that a freight train was involved in the incident, which occurred in a rural area just outside the town.  No injuries have been reported at this time, according to BTP.

Aerial footage, captured by drones and helicopters, has provided a clear, albeit concerning, perspective of the derailed wagons. The images show several carriages off the tracks, with some appearing to have tipped on their side, surrounded by debris. The cause of the derailment is not yet known and is subject to an immediate and thorough investigation. Rail operators have stated that services in the affected area will be significantly disrupted for an extended period.

Commuters and residents are being advised to check with their rail providers for the latest updates on service cancellations and alternative travel arrangements. The impact on local infrastructure is also being assessed, as recovery operations are expected to be complex and lengthy. BTP has appealed for any witnesses who may have seen anything unusual prior to the derailment to come forward. The investigation will involve examining the train's mechanics, the track condition, and operational procedures. Further details are expected to be released as the inquiry progresses.
