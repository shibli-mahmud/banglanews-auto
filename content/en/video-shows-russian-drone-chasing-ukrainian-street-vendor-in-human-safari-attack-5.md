A chilling video released by Ukrainian authorities has surfaced, allegedly depicting a Russian drone engaging in a terrifying pursuit of a civilian street vendor. The footage, reportedly captured in a Ukrainian settlement, shows the vendor in a state of panic, sprinting through open spaces while a drone, believed to be Russian, hovers above and appears to track their every move. The relentless chase has been likened by observers to a 'human safari,' a term used to describe the hunting of humans for sport or amusement.

Ukrainian officials have vehemently condemned the incident, with the Prosecutor General's Office announcing an investigation into a potential war crime. They argue that deliberately targeting unarmed civilians constitutes a grave violation of international humanitarian law. The video, though graphic in its depiction of fear and vulnerability, has been widely shared across social media platforms, sparking outrage and renewed calls for accountability for alleged Russian atrocities. While the authenticity of the drone and its operator's nationality are yet to be independently verified by international bodies, the visual evidence presented has been deemed sufficient by Ukrainian authorities to warrant a full-scale war crimes investigation. The incident highlights the evolving and often brutal tactics employed in the ongoing conflict, raising serious concerns about the safety and security of civilians caught in the crossfire.
