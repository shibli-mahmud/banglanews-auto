A blast rocked a popular Moscow restaurant late Monday, resulting in the tragic deaths of three individuals and injuring several others. State media, citing law enforcement sources, reported that the incident was the result of a deliberate bombing. Preliminary investigations suggest that a woman, whose identity has not yet been fully released, attempted to enter the establishment carrying explosives. However, her access was reportedly thwarted by a vigilant security guard. It was during this confrontation that the explosive device was detonated. The exact motive behind the attack remains unclear, and authorities are conducting a thorough investigation. Emergency services were quickly on the scene, and the area surrounding the restaurant has been cordoned off. Details regarding the victims are still emerging, and the number of injured is being assessed. Officials have assured the public that all necessary measures are being taken to ensure public safety and bring those responsible to justice. The incident has sent shockwaves through the city, raising concerns about security in public spaces.
