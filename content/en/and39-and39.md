Middle Eastern cuisine has always held a special allure for food lovers, characterized by its aromatic spices, slow-cooked meats, fluffy Arabic bread, vibrant mezze platters, and sweet delights like baklava. Recognizing this growing fascination, The Westin Dhaka's 'Taste of Arabia' festival presents an immersive dining experience at its Taste restaurant. Guests can indulge in a lavish buffet featuring a wide selection of authentic dishes meticulously crafted by a team of skilled chefs. 

The festival highlights signature dishes from various Arabian regions, promising a true gastronomic adventure. From succulent kebabs and flavorful tagines to the comforting embrace of hummus and tabbouleh, each dish is a testament to the region's culinary heritage. The use of fresh ingredients and traditional cooking methods ensures that every bite transports diners straight to the heart of Arabia. 

Beyond the savory offerings, the dessert section is a paradise for those with a sweet tooth. Traditional Arabic sweets like kunafa and um ali, alongside the ever-popular baklava, are prepared to perfection, offering a delightful conclusion to the meal. The ambiance of the restaurant is also thoughtfully curated to complement the theme, enhancing the overall dining experience. 'Taste of Arabia' is not just a food festival; it's an invitation to explore the vibrant culture and culinary artistry of the Middle East, right here in Dhaka. The festival is scheduled to run for a limited period, making it a must-visit for anyone seeking an authentic taste of Arabian hospitality and cuisine.
