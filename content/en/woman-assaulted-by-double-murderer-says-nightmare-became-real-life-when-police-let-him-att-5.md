The harrowing ordeal of a woman sexually assaulted by Simon Levy, a man subsequently convicted of murdering two women and raping a third, has resurfaced, revealing the devastating impact of systemic failures.  The victim, whose name is being withheld to protect her privacy, recounted how her traumatic experience with Levy, prior to his later crimes, was compounded by a sense of betrayal and terror when she learned he had been released and had gone on to inflict unimaginable suffering on others.  She described her initial assault as a living nightmare, only to have that nightmare become terrifyingly real as Levy's subsequent violent actions unfolded, leaving a trail of destruction and further victims.  Her account paints a stark picture of a justice system that, through a series of 'disastrous decisions,' failed to adequately protect the public and left individuals vulnerable to repeat offenders.  The case of Simon Levy serves as a chilling reminder of the critical importance of robust offender management and timely intervention to prevent further tragedies.  The woman's courage in speaking out now, years later, aims to shed light on the profound consequences of such failures and to advocate for greater accountability within the system that allowed her attacker to continue his reign of terror.
