A joyous honeymoon turned to tragedy on the picturesque Greek island of Rhodes when a helicopter carrying a British couple and their pilot crashed, resulting in their untimely deaths. The victims have been identified as Alexander Cromie and Marie Ebert, both British nationals, and their Greek pilot. The couple was reportedly on their honeymoon, celebrating their recent marriage, when the incident occurred. 

Authorities were alerted to the crash on Saturday evening. Emergency services were dispatched to the remote location where the helicopter had gone down. Initial reports suggest the aircraft was a private charter. The exact cause of the crash is currently under investigation by Greek aviation authorities, with teams working to determine the sequence of events leading to the disaster. 

Eyewitness accounts are being gathered, and wreckage analysis is underway. The British Embassy in Athens has been informed and is providing consular assistance to the families of Mr. Cromie and Ms. Ebert. The loss of the young couple, whose lives were just beginning, has sent shockwaves through their communities. Friends and family have expressed profound grief and disbelief at the sudden and horrific nature of their deaths, remembering them as a happy and loving couple embarking on a new chapter together. The pilot, a local resident, is also mourned by his community. The investigation is expected to be thorough, aiming to provide answers and prevent future tragedies.
