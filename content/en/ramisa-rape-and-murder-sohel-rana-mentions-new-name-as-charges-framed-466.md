Dhaka: The already sensational Ramisa rape and murder case has taken another dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, revealing a new name during the charge-framing proceedings. Rana has reportedly claimed the involvement of an individual known only by the alias 'Dollar' in the heinous crime. This unexpected disclosure has caused a stir in the courtroom and among investigators, who are now tasked with verifying Rana's statement and potentially broadening the scope of the investigation.

The court was hearing the arguments for framing charges against the accused when Rana, surprisingly, brought up 'Dollar's' alleged participation. While the exact nature of Dollar's alleged involvement remains unclear, sources suggest it could pertain to facilitation or direct participation in the crime. The prosecution is expected to meticulously examine this new lead, considering its potential impact on the evidence presented so far. Defense lawyers, on the other hand, may see this as an opportunity to introduce reasonable doubt or to shift blame.

Ramisa, a young woman whose brutal murder sent shockwaves across the nation, has become a symbol of the fight against gender-based violence. The case has been closely followed by the public and media, with demands for swift justice. The introduction of 'Dollar' into the narrative adds an element of mystery, raising questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the network of individuals involved. Authorities are reportedly working to identify and locate this 'Dollar' figure to corroborate or refute Rana's claims. The court has adjourned the proceedings for further consideration, with the framing of charges now potentially delayed as this new information is processed.
