Speaking ahead of a potential fixture in Mexico, Chelsea head coach Thomas Tuchel offered a surprisingly positive perspective on the reception afforded to the English contingent.  Amidst heightened security protocols, often implemented due to past incidents or general safety considerations in international football, Tuchel expressed that the atmosphere had been remarkably accommodating.  "I have to say, it has been nicer than I expected," Tuchel commented, directly addressing any prevailing anxieties about how England might be treated.  He specifically highlighted the behavior of the Mexican supporters, describing them as "friendly and respectful."  This sentiment comes as a welcome counterpoint to the often-sensationalized narratives that can surround international travel for sports teams, particularly when security is a prominent factor.  Tuchel's remarks suggest a genuine appreciation for the local culture and the manner in which fans have embraced the visiting team, fostering an environment of fair play and sporting camaraderie.  While the exact context of the fixture or the reasons for the increased security were not elaborated upon, the manager's endorsement of the fan base offers a positive outlook and a testament to the spirit of the sport in Mexico.
