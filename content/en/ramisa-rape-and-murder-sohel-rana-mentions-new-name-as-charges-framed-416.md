This revelation has sent ripples through the investigation, suggesting that the conspiracy behind Ramisa's death might be more extensive than initially believed.

The prosecution is expected to seek further interrogations and possibly new warrants based on Rana's statement. The defense, on the other hand, may use this as a strategy to shift blame or to cast doubt on the existing evidence. The court has ordered a thorough review of Rana's statement and has indicated a willingness to consider any credible leads that emerge from this new information.

Ramisa's family, who have been relentlessly seeking justice, have expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension. They are hopeful that this new name might bring them closer to uncovering the full truth and ensuring that all perpetrators are brought to account. However, they also fear that this could prolong the legal process. The coming days are crucial as investigators work to verify Rana's claims and understand the implications of this unexpected development on the trajectory of the trial.
