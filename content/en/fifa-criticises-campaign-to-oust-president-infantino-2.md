Zurich – FIFA has publicly denounced what it terms a deliberate and persistent campaign to discredit its president, Gianni Infantino, and undermine the integrity of the organization. In a strongly worded statement released from its headquarters, the global football authority declared its opposition to what it perceives as attempts to 'destabilize' its governance and tarnish its image. The organization pointed to what it characterized as a series of 'baseless allegations' and 'manipulative tactics' being employed by various actors. FIFA asserts that these actions are not only detrimental to its ongoing reform efforts but also harmful to the sport's global development. The statement further emphasized FIFA's commitment to transparency and good governance, vowing to resist any attempts to derail its progress. While specific individuals or groups were not named, the federation indicated that it is actively monitoring the situation and will take appropriate measures to protect its reputation and the interests of its member associations. The move signals a hardening of FIFA's stance against internal and external pressures it believes are aimed at weakening its leadership and operational independence. The organization reiterated its dedication to serving the global football community and to ensuring the continued growth and fairness of the sport.
