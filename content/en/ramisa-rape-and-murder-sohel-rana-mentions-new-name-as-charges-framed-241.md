Dhaka: The protracted investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a case that has gripped the nation, took a dramatic turn today as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, revealed the name of a new individual purportedly connected to the crime. The disclosure was made during proceedings where charges were formally framed against the accused in a local court. This unexpected revelation has added a new layer of complexity to an already sensitive and high-profile case.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that Sohel Rana, during his deposition, implicated a man whose identity is yet to be officially confirmed by the authorities. The defense counsel is expected to use this new information to challenge the existing narrative and potentially seek further investigations. Meanwhile, the prosecution is likely to incorporate this new testimony into their case, aiming to strengthen the evidence against all involved parties.

The Ramisa case, since its inception, has been marked by public outcry and demands for swift justice. The victim's family has consistently called for a thorough investigation and exemplary punishment for all perpetrators. The introduction of a new suspect, if corroborated, could lead to a broader scope of inquiry, potentially uncovering a wider conspiracy.

Legal experts suggest that the framing of charges is a pivotal moment, moving the case from the investigative phase to the trial. The mention of a new name at this juncture, however, could necessitate amendments to the chargesheet or further legal maneuvering. The court's decision on how to proceed with this new piece of evidence will be closely watched by legal observers and the public alike. The police have stated they are reviewing the statement and will take appropriate action based on verified information.
