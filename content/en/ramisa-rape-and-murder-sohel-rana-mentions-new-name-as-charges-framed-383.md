Dhaka: The courtroom was abuzz with tense anticipation today as charges were formally framed against the accused in the deeply disturbing rape and murder case of Ramisa. While the legal process moves forward, a significant revelation emerged from the prime suspect, Sohel Rana, who, during the proceedings, allegedly identified a new name as being involved in the crime. Sources close to the investigation confirmed that Rana's statement, though requiring further corroboration, has prompted authorities to broaden their inquiry.

The initial investigation had focused on a limited number of individuals, with Sohel Rana being a central figure. However, this latest disclosure suggests a potential network or accomplice previously overlooked. Legal experts note that such last-minute revelations can complicate trials, necessitating further evidence gathering and potentially reopening lines of inquiry. The prosecution team has indicated they will meticulously examine this new information, seeking to verify Rana's claims and ascertain the credibility of the newly named individual. The victim's family, who has been steadfast in their pursuit of justice, expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension, emphasizing their desire for the complete truth to be uncovered and all perpetrators brought to account. The court has adjourned, with further proceedings expected to take into account this new dimension of the case.
