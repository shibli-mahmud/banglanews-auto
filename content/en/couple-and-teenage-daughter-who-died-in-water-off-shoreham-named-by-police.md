Authorities have named a couple and their teenage daughter who died after getting into difficulty in the sea off Shoreham beach. The victims were identified as 44-year-old Murshed Alam, his 43-year-old wife Nergis Jahan, and their 16-year-old daughter, Umama Murshed. All three were residents of London and had reportedly travelled to the Sussex coast for a day out.

Emergency services were alerted to the incident on Saturday afternoon following reports of people in distress in the water. Despite the swift arrival of lifeboats, coastguards, and paramedics, the family could not be saved. Searches were conducted for several hours before the bodies were recovered. The precise circumstances leading to the family entering the water and the subsequent tragedy are still under investigation by Sussex Police.

Detective Chief Inspector Alasdair Gillies stated, "This is a terribly sad incident, and our thoughts are with the family and their loved ones at this incredibly difficult time. We are working to understand the full circumstances that led to this tragedy." He urged anyone who witnessed the incident or has any information that could assist the investigation to come forward.

The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community and the wider London area, with tributes beginning to pour in for the family. The beach area where the incident occurred was busy with visitors at the time, and witnesses are being sought to piece together the events of that afternoon. The investigation is ongoing.
