Berlin was the scene of a shocking incident on Saturday night when a car was deliberately driven into a crowd participating in a Christopher Street Day (CSD) Pride event. The incident, which occurred in the Schöneberg district, sent shockwaves through the LGBTQ+ community and the city. Authorities have confirmed that at least 30 people were injured, with several sustaining serious wounds, and were subsequently taken to hospitals. 

The driver, identified as a 53-year-old man, was apprehended at the scene. Initial reports from the police suggest a possible motive related to extremist ideologies, though investigators are still working to piece together the full picture. The car, a station wagon, reportedly mounted a pavement and ploughed into onlookers who had gathered to celebrate the annual Pride parade. Witnesses described scenes of panic and chaos as people scattered to avoid the oncoming vehicle. 

Emergency services responded swiftly, with police, fire brigades, and numerous ambulances arriving to tend to the injured and secure the area. Investigations are ongoing, with authorities examining the suspect's background and potential connections. The attack has cast a somber shadow over Berlin's vibrant Pride celebrations, but organizers have vowed to continue with planned events, emphasizing resilience and solidarity in the face of such violence. The motivation behind the attack remains a primary focus for investigators as they seek to ensure the safety of future public gatherings.
