The police are now tasked with verifying Rana's claims, which could involve tracking down the new suspect, gathering further evidence, and potentially re-interrogating witnesses. The defence, meanwhile, may see this as an opportunity to deflect blame or introduce doubt. The court has adjourned for a brief period to allow the prosecution to consider Rana's statement.
