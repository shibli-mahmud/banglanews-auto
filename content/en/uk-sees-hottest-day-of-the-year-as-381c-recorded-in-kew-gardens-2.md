The United Kingdom experienced its most sweltering day of 2023 on Saturday, with the mercury climbing to an astonishing 38.1 degrees Celsius (100.6 degrees Fahrenheit) at Kew Gardens in London. This significant heatwave has prompted the Met Office to issue an amber extreme heat warning, which is currently in place for large parts of England until midnight. The intense heat is expected to persist, with high temperatures posing risks to health and infrastructure across the country.

Health authorities are urging the public to take precautions, emphasizing the dangers of dehydration, heatstroke, and other heat-related illnesses. Vulnerable individuals, including the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing health conditions, are particularly at risk. Public services are also feeling the strain, with reports of railway lines buckling under the heat and increased demand on emergency services. The public is advised to stay hydrated, avoid strenuous activity during the hottest parts of the day, and check on neighbours and relatives who may be more susceptible to the heat. The amber warning signifies that conditions are likely to be disruptive, with potential impacts on daily routines, travel, and public transport. The Met Office has also warned of the increased risk of wildfires due to the dry conditions exacerbated by the high temperatures.
