He claimed that 'Dollar' was not only involved in the planning but also actively participated in the commission of the horrific crime.

This new piece of information has sent ripples through the case, potentially widening the scope of the investigation and introducing further complexity to the prosecution's strategy. While the specifics of Dollar's alleged role remain unclear, the mention by the primary accused suggests a coordinated effort and a network of individuals behind Ramisa's death. The court has taken note of Rana's statement, and prosecutors are expected to scrutinize this new lead.

Ramisa's case has garnered widespread public attention and outrage, highlighting critical issues surrounding women's safety and the efficacy of the justice system. The framing of charges signifies a crucial step towards accountability, but the introduction of a new name at this juncture underscores the ongoing challenges in uncovering the complete truth and ensuring justice for the victim. The defense is likely to leverage this new information, while the prosecution will aim to corroborate Rana's claims and bring all perpetrators to book. Further court sessions are anticipated to delve deeper into the alleged involvement of 'Dollar' and establish a clearer picture of the events leading to Ramisa's tragic demise.
