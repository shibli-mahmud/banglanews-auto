However, this development is expected to broaden the scope of the ongoing probe, potentially uncovering more individuals involved in the heinous crime.

The court's decision to move forward with the charges suggests a strong initial case against the identified perpetrators. Law enforcement officials have stated their commitment to investigating all angles and ensuring that everyone responsible is brought to book.
