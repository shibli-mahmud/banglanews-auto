Mayor Andy Burnham will use his first official cabinet meeting on Tuesday to present his administration's initial strategy for tackling the cost of living crisis. This comprehensive package is expected to detail a range of measures aimed at providing tangible relief to individuals and families across Greater Manchester who are grappling with rising energy bills, inflation, and the broader economic challenges impacting household budgets. 

The meeting, which will be chaired by Burnham himself, is anticipated to be a crucial forum for discussing the practical implementation and funding of these support schemes. While specific details remain under wraps, sources suggest the proposals could include enhanced support for vulnerable households, initiatives to reduce essential costs, and potentially new partnerships with local businesses and charities. 

Burnham's administration has consistently highlighted the cost of living as a top priority, and this upcoming announcement is seen as a strong indication of his commitment to addressing the issue head-on. The measures will be designed to offer immediate assistance while also exploring longer-term solutions to build greater economic resilience within the region. This marks a significant early move for the mayor, setting the tone for his upcoming term and demonstrating a proactive stance on a pressing societal concern.
