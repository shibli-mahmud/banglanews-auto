Prince William's reaction to his brother Prince Harry's brief visit to the UK for a memorial service has been reportedly frosty. Sources close to the Palace suggest that the Prince of Wales is not yet ready to bury the hatchet, with the profound sense of betrayal and hurt stemming from Harry's memoir "Spare" and numerous public accusations still casting a long shadow over their relationship. While public appearances might necessitate a veneer of cordiality, the underlying tension and a lack of genuine forgiveness are palpable.

William, who is set to ascend the throne in the future, is perceived to be prioritizing the stability and integrity of the monarchy. The damaging revelations and the perceived undermining of the Royal Family's reputation by his younger brother have seemingly made reconciliation a distant prospect. He is reportedly struggling to move past the personal and public affronts, making it difficult to extend an olive branch. The hurt, it is said, runs deep, and while time may eventually offer a path towards healing, it is not a journey William appears to be rushing.

Harry, on the other hand, has expressed a desire to reconcile, but his willingness to accept full responsibility for his actions and his continued engagement in lucrative media projects that often touch upon royal matters, remain significant sticking points for William. The elder brother's stance suggests a belief that true reconciliation requires a more substantial commitment from Harry to mend the fractured bond and demonstrate a genuine understanding of the damage caused. Therefore, any hopes of an immediate thaw in relations should likely be tempered with a healthy dose of realism.
