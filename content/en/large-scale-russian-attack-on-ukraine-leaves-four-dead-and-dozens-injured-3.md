Ukraine is grappling with the aftermath of a large-scale Russian attack that struck several cities overnight, claiming the lives of at least four civilians and leaving dozens injured. Ukrainian air defense forces engaged in intense combat to intercept a barrage of missiles and drones, with officials reporting that a significant portion of the incoming threats were neutralized. However, some projectiles managed to breach defenses, causing damage to residential buildings and critical infrastructure in cities including Kyiv, Kharkiv, and Odesa.

Residential areas bore the brunt of the strikes, with emergency services working tirelessly to rescue survivors and assess the damage. The Ukrainian Ministry of Defence stated that the scale of the attack underscores Russia's continued efforts to demoralize the Ukrainian population and degrade its military capabilities. Amidst the ongoing hostilities, Russia’s Ministry of Defence confirmed the deployment of sophisticated weaponry. Notably, they reported the use of the Oreshnik missile, a system reportedly capable of speeds exceeding Mach 10, or over ten times the speed of sound. The integration of such advanced missiles into Russia's arsenal raises concerns about the evolving nature of the conflict and the challenges faced by Ukrainian air defense. International condemnation of the attacks has been swift, with calls for a de-escalation and a return to diplomatic solutions.
