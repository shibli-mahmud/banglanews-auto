The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, revealing the name of a new individual allegedly involved in the crime. During the formal framing of charges, Rana reportedly disclosed that a man known only by the alias 'Dollar' was also a participant in the horrific act. This revelation has prompted a renewed focus from law enforcement, who are now scrambling to identify and apprehend this hitherto unknown suspect. The prosecution is expected to use Rana's statement as a key piece of evidence, potentially leading to a wider investigation and the indictment of additional individuals. The victim's family has expressed a mix of shock and renewed hope, urging the authorities to leave no stone unturned in bringing all those responsible to justice. The framing of charges signifies the commencement of the trial proper, and the mention of 'Dollar' is poised to add a significant layer of complexity to the proceedings. Legal experts suggest that this new information could necessitate further evidence gathering and potentially lead to amendments in the charges against the accused. The community remains deeply affected by the case, demanding a thorough and transparent judicial process.
