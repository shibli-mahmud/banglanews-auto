The case, which has gripped the nation with its brutality, took an unexpected twist today as Sohel Rana, the primary suspect in the rape and murder of young Ramisa, provided a new name to investigators and the court. According to sources present in the courtroom, Rana, while the charges were being read out, asserted that a man named 'Rahim' was also a participant in the crime. This statement has reportedly sent ripples through the investigative team and the prosecution, who are now tasked with verifying this new claim and potentially expanding the scope of their investigation.

Ramisa's brutal murder sent shockwaves across the country earlier this year, sparking widespread protests and demands for swift justice. The initial investigation had focused solely on Sohel Rana, who was apprehended shortly after the incident. However, his latest statement suggests a possible conspiracy or a larger network involved. The prosecution is expected to request a postponement of the trial to conduct further inquiries into Rahim's alleged involvement. Defense lawyers have indicated they will be scrutinizing this new development closely, questioning its timing and veracity. The court has adjourned for the day, with further proceedings expected to resume once the prosecution has had a chance to assess the implications of Sohel Rana's statement.
