Berlin police have reported that a suspect, identified as 32-year-old Abdul Ballout, was fatally shot by officers during a police operation on Tuesday. The incident occurred in the western borough of Spandau, where Ballout was reportedly cornered by law enforcement. According to a police spokesperson, Ballout suddenly rushed at the officers wielding a knife, leaving them with no choice but to use lethal force to neutralize the threat.

The suspect had been sought in connection with an alleged assault that took place during a demonstration related to the Berlin Pride celebrations. While initial reports suggested the incident might have been motivated by homophobic sentiments, investigators are still examining all possible motives, including personal grievances. The Berlin public prosecutor's office has launched an investigation into the police shooting, a standard procedure in such cases to ensure accountability and transparency. The event has cast a shadow over the ongoing Pride festivities, though organizers have emphasized the importance of continuing celebrations while maintaining vigilance. Security measures around public events in the city have reportedly been heightened in the aftermath.
