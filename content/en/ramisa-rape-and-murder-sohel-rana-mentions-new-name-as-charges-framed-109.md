Dhaka, Bangladesh – June 1, 2026 – The judicial process in the deeply disturbing Ramisa rape and murder case took a dramatic turn today as Sohel Rana, the main suspect apprehended for the heinous crime, has purportedly implicated a new individual in his confession. Sources close to the investigation revealed that during the charge framing proceedings in the metropolitan sessions court, Rana provided a name previously unassociated with the case. While the identity of this new suspect remains undisclosed pending further verification and potential arrest, the development has intensified the focus on the ongoing inquiry. 

Ramisa, a young woman whose brutal murder sent shockwaves across the nation, became a symbol of the pervasive issues of violence against women. The framing of charges against Sohel Rana and his alleged accomplices signifies the prosecution's intent to bring the perpetrators to justice. The court has officially indicted the accused, allowing the trial to commence. However, Rana's latest statement adds a complex layer to the investigation, suggesting a possible wider network or a more intricate motive behind the crime. Law enforcement agencies are reportedly working diligently to corroborate Rana’s statement and ascertain the credibility of the newly named individual’s potential involvement. This new lead could be pivotal in unraveling the full truth behind Ramisa's tragic death and ensuring comprehensive justice for her and her family. The public awaits further updates with bated breath as the case progresses.
