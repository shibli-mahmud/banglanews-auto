Riyadh, Saudi Arabia – The Houthi militant group, based in Yemen, has asserted that it launched a ballistic missile towards Saudi Arabia's capital, Riyadh, on Saturday. Saudi authorities confirmed intercepting a projectile over the capital, amid reports of an explosion near the city's main airport. 

While the Houthi spokesperson, Yahya Saree, stated the missile targeted an important military facility within Riyadh, the Saudi-led coalition reported that their air defense systems successfully neutralized the incoming threat. There were no immediate reports of significant casualties or damage from the intercepted missile. 

However, concerns were heightened by separate reports of a blast near King Khalid International Airport. Initial indications suggested this might have been an attack on a fuel depot. This alleged strike led to temporary disruptions in air traffic, with some flights reportedly experiencing delays or being rerouted. The exact cause and perpetrator of the explosion near the airport remain under investigation, though it has fueled speculation of a coordinated assault. 

This incident marks a significant escalation in the ongoing conflict, as the Houthis continue to demonstrate their capability to strike deep within Saudi territory. The Yemeni conflict, which has been raging for years, has seen numerous cross-border attacks and retaliatory strikes. International observers are closely monitoring the situation for any further developments and potential responses from the Saudi-led coalition.
