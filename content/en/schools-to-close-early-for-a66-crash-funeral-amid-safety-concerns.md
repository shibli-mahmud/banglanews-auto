The funeral of a 20-year-old victim, who was among five friends killed when their car collided with a police vehicle on the A66, is scheduled to take place on Friday afternoon. In a move to acknowledge the profound grief within the local community and to ensure the safety of students and staff traveling to and from the service, a number of schools in the surrounding areas have opted to end their day ahead of the usual schedule.

Local education authorities have been in communication with the affected schools, advising them to consider early closures. While the exact number of institutions implementing this measure is still being confirmed, several primary and secondary schools have publicly announced their intention to shut down by lunchtime or early afternoon. This decision is seen as a sensitive response to the devastating event that has deeply impacted the region.

Parents have been informed of the early dismissals, with schools emphasizing that the closures are a voluntary measure to allow for mourning and to prevent potential traffic congestion and safety issues on routes expected to be busy due to the funeral procession. The crash, which involved a Vauxhall Corsa and a marked police car, has also prompted ongoing investigations into the circumstances surrounding the incident, with the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) leading the inquiry. The community is coming together to support the grieving families, and the early school closures are a poignant reflection of this collective sentiment.
