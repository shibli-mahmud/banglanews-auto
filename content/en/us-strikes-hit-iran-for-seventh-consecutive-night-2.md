In a sustained campaign of military action, the U.S. military confirmed that operations against Iran entered their seventh consecutive night. U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) stated that the strikes are a deliberate effort to degrade Iran's offensive and defensive armed forces, emphasizing a strategic objective to disrupt their military infrastructure and capabilities.  The announcement comes amidst reports from Iranian state media detailing multiple explosions occurring near the strategic Strait of Hormuz, a crucial chokepoint for global oil shipments.  While the exact nature and attribution of these explosions remain unconfirmed by independent sources, they coincide directly with the ongoing U.S. military operations.  The U.S. has not provided specific details on the targets of the latest strikes but has previously indicated a focus on missile launch sites, drone facilities, and naval assets deemed to be a threat to regional stability and international shipping lanes.  The prolonged nature of the strikes suggests a significant escalation in the ongoing tensions between the two nations. International observers are closely monitoring the situation, concerned about the potential for wider regional conflict and its impact on global energy markets.  The Iranian government has condemned the U.S. actions as an act of aggression and a violation of its sovereignty, vowing a firm response.  The escalating situation underscores the volatile geopolitical landscape in the Middle East.
