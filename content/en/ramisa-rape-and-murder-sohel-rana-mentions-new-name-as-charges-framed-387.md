This development has sparked considerable interest and concern among legal experts and the public alike, raising questions about the completeness of the initial investigation and the potential for further complicity.

Sources close to the investigation confirmed that the court formally framed charges against Sohel Rana and a group of other identified individuals on Monday. However, the details of the new name provided by Rana remain under wraps as the prosecution seeks to corroborate the information. The defense is expected to leverage this new revelation to their advantage, potentially complicating the prosecution's case. The victim's family, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, expressed a mix of hope and apprehension, emphasizing the need for a comprehensive and impartial inquiry that leaves no stone unturned. The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the legal proceedings, moving the case closer to a trial. The court's decision on how to proceed with the newly introduced information will be closely watched, as it could significantly impact the trajectory of this deeply distressing case.
