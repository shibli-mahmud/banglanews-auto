The exact cause of the crash is not yet known, and investigations are in their early stages. However, initial reports suggest that the incident involved a freight train and a passenger service.  Details regarding any potential injuries are still emerging, but it is understood that some passengers and staff may have sustained injuries.  Paramedics have been attending to those affected, and arrangements are being made for any necessary hospital admissions.  The collision has had a severe impact on rail services, with all lines in the vicinity of the crash site blocked. EMR has confirmed the incident and advised passengers to check their travel information and avoid travelling on the affected routes if possible.  Disruption is expected to continue for a significant period as the site is secured, emergency operations conclude, and an investigation into the cause of the derailment and collision commences.
