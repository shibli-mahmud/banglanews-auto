Russia intensified its aerial offensive against Ukraine late Tuesday and into Wednesday morning, targeting key cities with a barrage of missiles and drones. In the capital, Kyiv, firefighters are reportedly working to extinguish a large fire that broke out at a market following an apparent strike. The scale of the damage and the full extent of casualties are still being assessed, but initial reports indicate at least three fatalities and multiple injuries across the country.

Ukrainian air defense forces engaged a significant number of incoming projectiles, with officials claiming to have intercepted many of them. However, some were reported to have penetrated defenses, leading to the destruction and fires.

In Moscow's report, the Russian Ministry of Defense claimed its forces successfully struck a military warehouse in Ukraine. They also stated that their air defense systems intercepted several Ukrainian drones attempting to target Russian territory.

These latest attacks come amid a period of heightened tensions and follow a pattern of Russia targeting Ukrainian energy infrastructure and urban centers. Kyiv has consistently condemned these actions as war crimes and civilian terror. The ongoing aerial onslaught highlights the persistent nature of the conflict and the devastating impact it continues to have on Ukrainian civilians and infrastructure, with no immediate signs of de-escalation from either side.
