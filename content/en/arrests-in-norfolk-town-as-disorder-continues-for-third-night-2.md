Authorities in a Norfolk market town have confirmed multiple arrests following a third night of unrest. The disorder, which has gripped the community for the past three evenings, is understood to be connected to escalating tensions surrounding anti-immigration demonstrations.  Details regarding the specific number of arrests and the charges laid are still emerging, but police presence has been significantly heightened in the affected areas.  Local residents have expressed growing concern over the persistent disturbances, with reports of property damage and intimidation circulating.  Eyewitness accounts suggest that the protests, while initially focused on immigration issues, have devolved into wider public disorder.  Emergency services have been on high alert, and contingency plans are in place to manage any further escalation.  Police are appealing to the public for calm and cooperation, urging anyone with information that could assist their investigation to come forward.  The long-term impact of these events on the town's community and its reputation is a growing worry for local officials and businesses. Investigations are ongoing, with a focus on identifying all individuals involved in the disturbances and ensuring accountability.
