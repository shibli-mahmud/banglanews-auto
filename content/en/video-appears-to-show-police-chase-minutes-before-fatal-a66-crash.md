Dashcam footage captured by a motorist is circulating online, appearing to show multiple police vehicles in pursuit of a car just three minutes prior to a devastating crash on the A66. The incident, which occurred on the notorious stretch of road near Middlesbrough, resulted in at least one fatality and several injuries, according to preliminary reports.

The grainy video, reportedly taken around 2:30 PM on Tuesday, shows at least two marked police cars and what appears to be an unmarked vehicle actively following a smaller silver car at high speed. The vehicles are seen navigating traffic, with the police cars maintaining close proximity to the car they are pursuing. The footage ends abruptly, with no further visual information about the crash itself.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) has confirmed it is investigating the circumstances surrounding the A66 collision. While they have not directly commented on the existence or content of the video, a spokesperson stated that their investigation will examine the actions of the police leading up to the incident. The timing of the video, if authenticated, could be a crucial piece of evidence in their inquiry.

Local authorities have urged the public to avoid speculation and to allow the investigation to proceed. However, the emergence of the video has fueled public concern and calls for transparency regarding police pursuit policies. The A66 has been the scene of several serious accidents in recent years, prompting ongoing discussions about road safety and the appropriate use of police powers. The full impact of this footage on the ongoing investigation remains to be seen, but it undeniably adds a new layer of scrutiny to the tragic events.
