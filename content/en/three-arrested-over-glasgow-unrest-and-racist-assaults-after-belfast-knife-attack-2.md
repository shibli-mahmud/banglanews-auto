Three men have been arrested by Police Scotland following widespread public disorder and multiple racist assaults that erupted in Glasgow on Tuesday evening. The disturbing incidents, which saw members of the public targeted specifically due to their skin colour, are understood to be a direct response to a knife attack that occurred in Belfast earlier the same day.  Authorities have condemned the violence and emphasised that such acts of hate will not be tolerated.  Speaking on the matter, a spokesperson for Police Scotland stated, 'We are investigating a number of racially motivated assaults that occurred on Tuesday evening in Glasgow. We believe these incidents are linked to a wider disturbance and are treating them with the utmost seriousness.'  The arrests were made following a significant police response and appeals for information from the public.  The exact nature of the Belfast knife attack and its connection to the Glasgow events are still under investigation, but initial reports suggest a retaliatory motive.  The incidents have sparked condemnation from various community leaders and anti-racism campaigners, who are urging for calm and a focus on addressing the root causes of such division.  The police are continuing their inquiries and have urged anyone with further information to come forward to assist with the ongoing investigation.
