Keir Starmer, the leader of the Labour Party, will deliver his first major address as Prime Minister on Monday, a speech anticipated to focus on providing 'more breathing room' for the nation. With his ascension to the premiership marking the seventh change in prime ministerial leadership in just a decade, Starmer is expected to acknowledge the public's fatigue with political instability.  He is reportedly "acutely conscious" of the high turnover and the impact it has had on public confidence and national progress.  Sources close to the new PM suggest the speech will outline a commitment to a more settled and predictable government, aiming to foster a sense of security after a turbulent period.  The focus will likely be on practical measures and a steady hand at the helm, rather than grand, sweeping gestures.  Starmer is expected to emphasize his dedication to addressing the immediate concerns of households, hinting at policies designed to ease financial pressures and provide a much-needed respite.  The message will be one of reassurance and a return to steady governance, aiming to rebuild trust and offer a clear path forward for the United Kingdom.  The address is seen as a crucial opportunity to set the tone for his premiership and to begin the process of healing the divisions and anxieties that have characterized recent political times.
