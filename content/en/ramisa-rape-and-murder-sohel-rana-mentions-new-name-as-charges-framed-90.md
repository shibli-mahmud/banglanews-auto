The charges were formally framed yesterday, marking a crucial stage in the legal proceedings against Sohel Rana and any other potential accomplices. The court, after hearing arguments, decided to proceed with the trial based on the existing evidence. However, it was during this juncture that Rana, reportedly under duress or in an attempt to implicate others, mentioned the name 'Dollar'.

Sources close to the investigation, speaking on condition of anonymity, have indicated that this is the first time the name 'Dollar' has surfaced in connection with Ramisa's case. The motive behind Rana's statement remains unclear, and investigators are working to verify the identity and whereabouts of this alleged new suspect. The possibility that this is a tactic to mislead the investigation is also being considered.

Ramisa's family, who have been seeking justice since the heinous crime occurred, expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension following this news. While any lead that could bring the perpetrators to justice is welcome, they also voiced concerns about potential attempts to derail the proceedings. The prosecution team has pledged to thoroughly investigate this new claim and incorporate any relevant findings into the ongoing trial. The court has adjourned, with the next hearing expected to address the preliminary stages of the trial and potentially the new information provided by the accused.
