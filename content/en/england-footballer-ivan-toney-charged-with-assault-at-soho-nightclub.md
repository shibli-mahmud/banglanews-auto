Ivan Toney, the prominent 30-year-old England international and former Brentford striker, has been charged with assault following an alleged incident at a nightclub in London's Soho district. The Metropolitan Police confirmed on Thursday that Toney was charged with one count of assault occasioning actual bodily harm and one count of criminal damage. The charges stem from an alleged altercation that took place in the early hours of December 17, 2023. 

Toney, who has earned several caps for the England national team and was part of the squad for the 2022 World Cup, is a well-known figure in Premier League football. His alleged involvement in the Soho incident has led to significant media attention. The club where the incident reportedly occurred has not been officially named by police. 

Legal proceedings are now underway, and Toney is due to appear at Uxbridge Magistrates' Court on a date next month. The football authorities are expected to monitor the situation closely, with potential disciplinary actions from his club and the Football Association (FA) dependent on the outcome of the legal proceedings. Toney's current club, Brentford, has not yet issued a formal statement regarding the charges. This development marks a significant legal challenge for the striker, whose career has been on an upward trajectory in recent seasons.
