Dhaka: The Ramisa rape and murder case, which has sent shockwaves across the nation, has taken a new turn with the primary accused, Sohel Rana, reportedly implicating another person in the crime. Sources close to the investigation confirm that Rana, during his deposition, disclosed the name of an individual he claims was complicit in the brutal act. This development has prompted the prosecution to consider further investigation into the alleged accomplice's role.

The formal framing of charges by the court signifies the commencement of the legal proceedings. The prosecution team, led by Senior Public Prosecutor Advocate Karim Chowdhury, is preparing to present a comprehensive case against the identified accused. "We are diligently gathering all evidence and witness testimonies to ensure justice for Ramisa and her family," Advocate Chowdhury stated. "The new information provided by Sohel Rana, if substantiated, will be a critical component of our strategy."

The case, which has been under intense public scrutiny, involves the alleged rape and murder of young Ramisa, whose lifeless body was discovered under distressing circumstances. The initial investigation led to the arrest of Sohel Rana, who has since been the central figure in the ongoing legal battle. The mention of a new name raises questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the number of individuals involved. Legal experts suggest that this could lead to amended charges or the inclusion of new defendants in the ongoing trial. The court has scheduled the next hearing for detailed arguments on the framed charges and the potential inclusion of the newly named individual.
