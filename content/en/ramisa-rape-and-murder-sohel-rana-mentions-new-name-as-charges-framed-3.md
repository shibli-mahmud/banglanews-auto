The Dhaka Additional Metropolitan Sessions Judge's Court was the scene of a significant development on Monday, June 1st, 2026, when charges were formally framed against the accused in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa. Sohel Rana, who is facing trial for the heinous crime, made a surprising statement during the proceedings, revealing the name of another person allegedly involved in the incident. This revelation has caught many by surprise, including the prosecution and defense teams, who were ostensibly prepared for the established narrative of the case.

Sources close to the court proceedings indicate that Rana’s statement was made under oath, lending it a degree of credibility that warrants further investigation. While the exact identity and alleged role of the newly named individual have not been disclosed by the court to protect the integrity of the ongoing trial, the prosecution has indicated its intention to thoroughly examine this new information. This may involve further questioning of Sohel Rana and potentially summoning the new suspect for interrogation.

The framing of charges is a crucial step in the legal process, marking the formal commencement of a trial. However, with this new revelation, the path forward for the court has become more intricate. Legal experts are divided on the potential impact of Rana's statement. Some believe it could be a desperate attempt by the accused to shift blame, while others suggest it might uncover a broader conspiracy. The defense team has remained tight-lipped, stating they will address the new information as the trial progresses. The victim's family, who have been seeking justice with unwavering resolve, have expressed a mix of shock and a renewed plea for truth, regardless of who it implicates. The court has adjourned the proceedings and is expected to issue further directions regarding the new development.
