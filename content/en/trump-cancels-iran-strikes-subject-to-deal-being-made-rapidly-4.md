President Donald Trump revealed on Saturday that he had aborted anticipated retaliatory strikes against Iran, asserting that the operations were contingent on the swift finalization of a deal. In a series of tweets, Trump stated that Iran and "other Middle Eastern countries" had implored Washington to delay any offensive action. He further elaborated, "We were cocked & loaded to strike last night on three key Iran locations when I was told, and generally affirmed, that Iran saying they had no role and would wage a one-act war. I did not like that answer, or the fact that this would likely lead to a much bigger problem in Iran. Not to mention, Iran calling it a "great deterrence" was a very interesting statement to me."

The President's announcement came after reports surfaced of Iran shooting down a US drone over the Strait of Hormuz, an incident that had significantly heightened tensions in the region. Trump had previously suggested that a military response was imminent, but his subsequent statements indicate a shift towards a diplomatic approach, albeit with a clear ultimatum for a rapid resolution. The "perimeters" of a potential deal, as mentioned by the President, suggest that some initial understandings may have been reached, but the specifics and the timeline remain uncertain. The international community is closely watching the unfolding situation, hoping for de-escalation and a peaceful resolution to the escalating crisis in the Middle East.
