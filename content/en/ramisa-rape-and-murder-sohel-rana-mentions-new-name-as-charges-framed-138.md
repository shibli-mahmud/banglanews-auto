The Ramisa rape and murder case, which has sent shockwaves across the nation, took a new turn on Monday when the primary accused, Sohel Rana, reportedly revealed the involvement of another man named 'Dollar.' The Dhaka court formally framed charges against Rana and his co-accused, marking a crucial step towards the trial. Sources within the investigation team indicated that Rana's statement, provided during the court proceedings, specifically mentioned 'Dollar' as being present and involved in the heinous crime.

While details about 'Dollar' are scarce at this point, investigators are expected to prioritize identifying and locating this individual. The prosecution believes that this new information could be pivotal in establishing a clearer picture of the events that led to Ramisa's tragic demise. The framing of charges means that the legal process will now move forward with the presentation of evidence and witness testimonies. Families of the victim and concerned citizens have expressed a strong desire for swift justice and accountability for all those involved in this horrific incident. The addition of a new name to the roster of suspects underscores the complexity of the case and the ongoing efforts by law enforcement to ensure all perpetrators face the consequences of their actions.
