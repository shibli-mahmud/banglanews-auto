Passengers on a commuter train in the East of England have spoken of their harrowing experiences after a collision sent people 'flying from their seats'. The incident, which occurred on a busy route, resulted in a significant number of injuries, with the East of England Ambulance Service reporting nearly 90 people hurt. Of these, more than 30 are described as having sustained serious wounds, prompting a large-scale emergency response.

Eyewitness accounts paint a picture of sudden shock and chaos. One passenger, who wished to remain anonymous, described the moment of impact as 'unbelievable'. "We were just moving along, and then suddenly there was this horrific jolt. People were thrown from their seats, bags went everywhere, and there was a lot of screaming," they told reporters. Another commuter, Sarah Jenkins, recounted the disorienting aftermath. "It was chaos. You could hear people groaning, and the carriage was full of dust. We didn't know what had happened at first, just that something terrible had occurred."

Emergency services, including numerous ambulance crews, were quickly on the scene to provide aid and transport the injured to local hospitals. The exact cause of the collision is currently under investigation, with authorities working to determine the sequence of events that led to this unfortunate incident. The disruption to rail services is expected to be substantial as investigations and recovery efforts commence. Officials have urged the public to avoid the area and to seek information from official channels regarding the ongoing situation and travel updates.
