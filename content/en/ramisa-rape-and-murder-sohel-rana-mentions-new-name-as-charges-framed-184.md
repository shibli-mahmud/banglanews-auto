The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, implicating a previously unmentioned individual. During proceedings related to the framing of charges, Rana reportedly stated that a man known only as 'Dollar' was present at the scene when the crime occurred. This disclosure has surprised investigators and legal experts alike, raising questions about the extent of 'Dollar's' involvement and his identity. 

Law enforcement agencies are now scrambling to identify and locate 'Dollar', with a view to understanding his alleged role in the case. The prosecution is expected to present its case and the evidence gathered so far to the court as charges are formally laid. The defense, on the other hand, may seek to leverage Rana's new statement to introduce doubt or to shift blame. 

Ramisa's family, who have been anxiously awaiting justice, expressed a mix of hope and apprehension following this news. While any lead that brings them closer to the truth is welcomed, the introduction of new suspects at this stage can sometimes prolong the legal process. The court's next steps will be crucial in determining how this new information is incorporated into the ongoing trial. The focus remains on ensuring that all individuals responsible for Ramisa's death face the full force of the law, regardless of any new names that emerge.
