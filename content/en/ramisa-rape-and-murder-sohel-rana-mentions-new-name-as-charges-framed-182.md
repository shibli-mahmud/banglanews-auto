The courtroom witnessed a tense atmosphere on Monday as charges were formally framed against Sohel Rana and others in connection with the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa. However, the proceedings took an unexpected turn when Rana, during his statement, alleged the involvement of a hitherto unknown individual named 'Dollar'. Sources close to the investigation revealed that Rana insisted 'Dollar' was present with him at the time of the incident, further complicating the already sensitive case. This new revelation has prompted the prosecution to consider further investigation into the identity and role of this individual. The defense team, while acknowledging the new claim, stated that their focus remains on defending their client against the existing charges. The judge has ordered that the trial will proceed, but has also directed the police to conduct preliminary inquiries into the mention of 'Dollar'. The case, which has gripped public attention due to its horrific nature, now faces a new layer of complexity with this unexpected testimony. The prosecution aims to meticulously examine the evidence and Rana's claims to ascertain the truth and ensure justice for Ramisa.
