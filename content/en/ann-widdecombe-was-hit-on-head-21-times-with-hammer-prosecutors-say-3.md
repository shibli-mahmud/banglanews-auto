Former Conservative MP Ann Widdecombe was subjected to a brutal assault at her home in Devon, during which she was allegedly hit on the head 21 times with a hammer, prosecutors have revealed. The alleged attack took place on Tuesday afternoon while Ms. Widdecombe was reportedly eating lunch in her residence.  Authorities have charged Joshua Kerry, a 28-year-old man, in connection with the incident.  According to prosecution statements, Kerry not only assaulted Ms. Widdecombe with a hammer but also stole her wallet following the violent encounter.  The shocking details emerged during preliminary court proceedings.  Police were called to the scene and subsequently arrested Mr. Kerry.  Details regarding the extent of Ms. Widdecombe's injuries have not been fully disclosed, but the severity of the alleged blows has raised significant concern.  The motive behind the attack is currently under investigation, with robbery being a primary focus.  This incident has sent ripples through the local community and beyond, given Ms. Widdecombe's high public profile.  Further court appearances are expected as the case progresses, with the prosecution aiming to build a strong case against the accused.  The alleged violence underscores the vulnerability of individuals even within their own homes.  Legal representatives for both the prosecution and the defence are preparing for the next stages of this serious criminal matter.
