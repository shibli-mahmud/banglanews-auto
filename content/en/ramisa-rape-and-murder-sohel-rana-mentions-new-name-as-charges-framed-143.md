The specifics of this new name and the extent of the alleged accomplice's role are currently under wraps as the court deliberates on the prosecution's case. However, the prosecution is expected to thoroughly vet this new information and potentially summon the individual for questioning and further investigation.

The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the trial, transitioning the case from the investigative phase to the substantive legal battle. The inclusion of a new name, especially by the main accused, adds complexity and necessitates a re-evaluation of the evidence and witness testimonies. Legal experts suggest that this development could lead to a more comprehensive understanding of the conspiracy, if any, behind Ramisa's tragic death.

Ramisa's family, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, have expressed a mix of hope and apprehension. While they welcome any information that could lead to the apprehension of all responsible parties, they also emphasize the need for due process and ensuring that any new suspect is genuinely involved and not a diversion tactic. The court's next steps will be closely watched as it decides how to incorporate this new information into the existing legal framework of the trial, aiming to deliver justice for Ramisa and her grieving family.
