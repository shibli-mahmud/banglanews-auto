Dhaka: The court of Additional Metropolitan Sessions Judge No. 2 in Dhaka witnessed a surprising revelation on Monday as Sohel Rana, the primary accused in the heinous rape and murder of Ramisa, claimed the involvement of another person. During the formal charge framing proceedings, Rana, who had previously remained largely silent, pointed the finger at a man known only as 'Dollar', alleging his active participation in the crime.

The charge framing marks a crucial stage in the legal process, where the court formally outlines the accusations against the defendant(s). However, Rana's sudden declaration has sent ripples through the courtroom and is expected to prompt further investigation by law enforcement agencies. Legal experts suggest that if Rana's claims are substantiated, the scope of the case could expand significantly, potentially leading to the arrest and prosecution of 'Dollar' and any other individuals involved.

Ramisa, a young woman, was found dead under mysterious circumstances earlier this year, sparking widespread public outcry and demanding swift justice. The initial investigation led to the arrest of Sohel Rana, who was identified as the main perpetrator. However, this new information suggests a more complex conspiracy than initially believed.

The prosecution team has stated that they will take Rana's statement into serious consideration and will initiate steps to identify and locate 'Dollar'. The court has adjourned the proceedings for now, with the next hearing expected to focus on the implications of this new evidence. The public is anxiously awaiting further developments in this high-profile case, hoping for a comprehensive investigation that brings all culprits to justice.
