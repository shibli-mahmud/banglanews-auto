Dhaka: The legal proceedings in the highly publicized Ramisa rape and murder case took an unexpected twist today as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, revealed a new name during the charge framing session at the court. Rana reportedly claimed that an individual known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the heinous crime. This unsolicited revelation has sent ripples through the courtroom and is expected to prompt further investigation by the authorities.

The court was hearing the case where Ramisa, a young woman, was brutally raped and murdered. The prosecution had presented its case and was proceeding with the indictment of the accused. However, Sohel Rana's interjection, naming 'Dollar' as an accomplice, has now put the investigators in a challenging position. It remains unclear whether 'Dollar' is a known associate of Rana or a new entity altogether. Law enforcement agencies are expected to meticulously examine this claim and verify its veracity.

Legal experts suggest that if Rana's statement holds true, it could lead to the arrest and prosecution of a new suspect. This could also necessitate a review of the existing evidence and potentially a re-framing of charges, depending on the extent of 'Dollar's' alleged involvement. The defense team for Sohel Rana has yet to comment on this development. The court has adjourned for the day, with the implications of Rana's statement likely to dominate discussions in the coming days as the investigation potentially expands.
