Investigators have confirmed that a 26-year-old white British man, who was arrested on suspicion of her murder, has been released from police custody and is no longer involved in the ongoing investigation. This development suggests that the initial focus of the inquiry may have been misdirected, and that the perpetrator of any assault, or the individual responsible for her death, remains at large.

The circumstances surrounding Ms. Widdecombe's death remain unclear, and the police have urged the public to come forward with any information that could assist their investigation. Detectives are appealing for witnesses who may have seen or heard anything unusual in the vicinity of Ms. Widdecombe's residence in the days leading up to her death.

Ann Widdecombe, a prominent figure in British politics for many years, served as the Member of Parliament for Maidstone and then for Christchurch. She was known for her outspoken views and distinctive parliamentary style. Following her political career, she became a frequent media commentator and television personality.

The investigation is ongoing, and further details are expected to be released as they become available. The police are treating the incident with the utmost seriousness and are committed to finding those responsible.
