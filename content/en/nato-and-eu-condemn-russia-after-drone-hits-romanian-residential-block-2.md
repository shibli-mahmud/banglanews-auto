Romania's Ministry of Defence confirmed on Tuesday that fragments of what is believed to be a Russian drone were discovered in the eastern part of the country, near the Danube River. The debris was found in the yard of a residential building in the village of Nufăru, Galați County. The ministry stated that the drone was likely targeted and brought down by Ukrainian air defense systems within Ukrainian airspace. However, it is believed to have altered its trajectory and subsequently fallen onto Romanian territory.  The Romanian authorities are investigating the incident, but initial findings suggest it was an unintended consequence of defensive actions taken by Ukraine.  This event has sparked significant diplomatic reactions.  NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg reiterated the alliance's solidarity with Romania and stated that "any attack on NATO territory would be met with a united and resolute response." The EU's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, Josep Borrell, also condemned the Russian actions, calling them a "brazen violation" of international law and a further escalation of the conflict. Both organizations emphasized the need for de-escalation and adherence to international humanitarian law, while holding Russia accountable for its aggression.  The incident underscores the precarious security situation in the region and the potential for unintended consequences as the war in Ukraine continues unabated.
