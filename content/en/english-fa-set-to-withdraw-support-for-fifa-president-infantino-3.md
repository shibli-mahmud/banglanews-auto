In a significant development that could shake the foundations of FIFA's upcoming presidential election, the English Football Association (FA) is understood to be on the verge of withdrawing its support for Gianni Infantino's bid to secure another term as the organization's president. This potential shift in allegiance from one of football's most influential nations comes as the FA deliberates its stance ahead of the election, with sources suggesting a growing unease with Infantino's leadership and FIFA's current direction.

The FA's contemplated move would align it with the Football Association of Wales (FAW), which recently announced its decision to withdraw its support for Infantino. This parallel action by two of the four British football associations could signal a broader wave of dissent within European football. While the specific reasons for the FA's reconsideration have not been officially disclosed, it is widely believed to stem from a combination of concerns, potentially including issues surrounding governance, transparency, and the allocation of resources within FIFA. 

Infantino, who has held the FIFA presidency since 2016, has been a polarizing figure. Supporters point to his efforts in increasing funding for member associations and his ambitious reform agenda. However, critics have raised questions about his leadership style, the handling of various controversies, and the perceived lack of accountability within the global football governing body. The FA's potential withdrawal of support would represent a considerable blow to Infantino's re-election campaign, highlighting the challenges he faces in maintaining unanimous backing from European confederations.
