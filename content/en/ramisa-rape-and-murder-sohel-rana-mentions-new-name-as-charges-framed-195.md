This startling revelation has sent ripples through the investigation, prompting authorities to scrutinize this new lead. While the exact context of Rana's statement is yet to be fully disclosed, sources close to the investigation suggest that he claims 'Dollar' was involved in the crime. 

The legal team representing Ramisa's family has expressed cautious optimism, emphasizing that any new information must be thoroughly investigated and corroborated. They stressed the importance of ensuring that justice is served for Ramisa and that all perpetrators are brought to account. The defense for Sohel Rana has remained tight-lipped on the matter, with lawyers stating that they will present their full case in court. 

This development comes at a critical juncture as the court formally frames charges against the accused. The inclusion of a new name, especially one presented by the prime suspect himself, is likely to lead to further interrogations and potentially the re-examination of existing evidence. The authorities are now faced with the task of locating and questioning this 'Dollar' to ascertain his alleged role in the crime. The case has already garnered significant public attention due to its brutal nature, and this latest twist is expected to intensify public scrutiny and demand for swift justice. The court is anticipated to issue further directives regarding the investigation into this new name as the trial progresses.
