The development came to light shortly after the court formally framed charges against Rana and his alleged accomplices. Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana, during his interrogation, provided the name of a new individual, whose alleged involvement is now under scrutiny. This unexpected statement has energized investigators, who are working to corroborate Rana's claims and ascertain the extent of this new suspect's participation in the heinous crime. The framing of charges signifies a crucial step forward in the legal proceedings, moving the case from the investigation phase towards a full-fledged trial. Prosecutors are reportedly meticulously reviewing all evidence, including forensic reports and witness testimonies, in preparation for the upcoming trial. The victim's family, who has been relentlessly seeking justice, expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension following this latest development. They are urging the authorities to thoroughly investigate the new lead and ensure that all perpetrators are brought to account. The public, deeply affected by the brutal nature of the crime, is closely watching the proceedings, demanding swift and decisive action. This new name, if proven to be involved, could significantly alter the complexion of the case and the sentences meted out.
