The prosecution has acknowledged Rana's statement and indicated that further investigations will be conducted to corroborate this new lead. If 'Dollar' is found to be involved, it could lead to his arrest and inclusion as a co-accused in the trial.

Ramisa, a minor, was found brutally murdered under mysterious circumstances, sparking widespread outrage and demands for swift justice. The framing of charges is a crucial step in the judicial process, marking the formal start of the trial, and Rana's statement now introduces an unexpected complexity, potentially prolonging the legal battle but also offering a glimmer of hope for uncovering the complete truth behind Ramisa's tragic demise.
