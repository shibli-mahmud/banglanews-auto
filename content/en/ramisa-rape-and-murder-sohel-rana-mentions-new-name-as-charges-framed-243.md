While details surrounding 'Dollar' and his alleged involvement remain scarce, the prosecution is expected to thoroughly investigate this new lead. The court has taken cognisance of Rana's statement and will likely seek to identify and question this individual. This unexpected disclosure has the potential to reshape the prosecution's strategy and broaden the scope of the inquiry. The case, which has gripped public attention due to its brutal nature, is now poised for further developments as authorities delve deeper into the alleged conspiracy.
