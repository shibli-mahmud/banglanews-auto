This statement, made under oath, has prompted the court to consider its implications for the ongoing investigation. 

Ramisa's brutal murder, which sent shockwaves across the nation, has been a subject of intense public scrutiny and demand for justice. The initial arrests and subsequent framing of charges aimed to bring closure to the grieving family and hold perpetrators accountable. However, Rana's latest disclosure suggests a wider network or a more complex conspiracy than initially understood. 

Legal experts are analyzing the significance of this new name. While the court will need to verify Rana's claims and gather corroborating evidence, the mention of 'Dollar' could lead to a fresh round of investigations, including tracing the individual's whereabouts and establishing their connection to the victim and the crime scene. The prosecution is expected to use this information to potentially amend charges or seek further detention for Rana to aid in the identification and arrest of 'Dollar.' The defense, on the other hand, might argue that this is an attempt by Rana to shift blame. The coming days are crucial as the authorities work to unravel the full truth behind Ramisa's tragic end.
