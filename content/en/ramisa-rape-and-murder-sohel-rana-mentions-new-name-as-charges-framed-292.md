The legal proceedings in the highly publicized Ramisa rape and murder case have taken a new turn with the framing of charges against the accused and the subsequent revelation by prime suspect Sohel Rana. During the court proceedings, Rana asserted that a previously unmentioned individual, known by the moniker 'Dollar', was also complicit in the heinous crime. This new information has prompted investigators to expand their inquiries to locate and question 'Dollar', whose alleged role could significantly alter the course of the investigation and the ensuing trial.

Rana's confession, if substantiated, suggests a potential conspiracy or a wider network of individuals involved in Ramisa's brutal demise. The police are now under pressure to corroborate Rana's claims and gather evidence that links 'Dollar' to the crime scene or the planning stages. Legal experts suggest that such a late-stage revelation could lead to delays in the trial as the prosecution may need to conduct further investigations and potentially amend the charges. The victim's family, who have been seeking justice for months, expressed a mix of hope and apprehension, emphasizing the need for a thorough and conclusive investigation. The court has adjourned the proceedings, awaiting further developments and the potential identification and apprehension of the individual named 'Dollar'. This development underscores the ongoing challenges faced by law enforcement in unraveling complex criminal cases and ensuring that all responsible parties are brought to justice.
