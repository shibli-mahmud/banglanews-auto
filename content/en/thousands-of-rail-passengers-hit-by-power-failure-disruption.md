Commuters and travelers across the North West of England faced a frustrating evening as a major power failure in Manchester brought rail services to a standstill. The disruption, which began earlier today, has resulted in a cascade of delays and cancellations affecting numerous routes operated by various rail companies. Passengers have been left stranded at stations, with many struggling to find alternative transport. 

Network Rail confirmed that engineers are working tirelessly to identify and rectify the fault, but warned that services are unlikely to return to normal before late this evening. The exact cause of the power failure remains under investigation, but it is understood to have impacted critical infrastructure within the Manchester rail corridor. 

Major stations like Manchester Piccadilly and Manchester Victoria have seen significant overcrowding as passengers grapple with the uncertainty.  Rail operators have advised passengers to check live travel updates before embarking on journeys and to consider alternative travel arrangements where possible. The incident highlights the vulnerability of the rail network to such infrastructure failures and the significant impact they can have on passenger connectivity.  Many passengers expressed their frustration on social media, detailing hours of waiting and the difficulty in obtaining reliable information.  The economic impact of such widespread disruption is also a growing concern for businesses reliant on smooth commuter flows.
