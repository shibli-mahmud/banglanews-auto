Dhaka: The harrowing case of Ramisa, whose brutal rape and murder shocked the nation, has witnessed a significant development with prime accused Sohel Rana reportedly revealing a new name to investigators. This unexpected revelation occurred just as the court formally framed charges against the accused, a procedural step that moves the trial closer to its commencement.

Sources privy to the investigation, speaking on condition of anonymity, indicated that Sohel Rana, during interrogation, implicated an individual whose name had not surfaced in the initial stages of the investigation. While the identity and alleged role of this new person are yet to be officially disclosed by the law enforcement agencies, the development is being viewed as potentially critical in uncovering the full truth behind Ramisa's death.

The framing of charges signifies that the prosecution has presented sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial, and the accused will now formally respond to these allegations. The inclusion of a new name, if substantiated, could lead to further investigations, potentially broadening the scope of the case and identifying all individuals responsible for the heinous crime.

Ramisa's case has been a focal point of public concern and demands for justice. The family, along with various human rights organizations, has been closely monitoring the proceedings, advocating for a swift and thorough investigation. The emergence of a new suspect, therefore, adds another layer of complexity and anticipation to the unfolding legal battle. Authorities have assured that the investigation will proceed rigorously to ensure that justice is served for Ramisa and her grieving family. Further details are expected to be released as the investigation progresses and more information becomes available to the public.
