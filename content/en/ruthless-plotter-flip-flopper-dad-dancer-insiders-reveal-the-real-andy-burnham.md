The recent BBC documentary, featuring interviews with more than 20 individuals who have crossed paths with Andy Burnham throughout his career, delves into the multifaceted nature of the Mayor of Greater Manchester. The narrative attempts to reconcile the often conflicting perceptions of Burnham: the ambitious politician often labelled a 'ruthless plotter' by opponents, the 'flip-flopper' accused of changing stances on key issues, and the more lighthearted image of a 'dad dancer' seen in public appearances. 

Insiders suggest that Burnham possesses a sharp political intellect and a deep understanding of public mood, enabling him to navigate complex political landscapes. His supporters highlight his dedication to Greater Manchester, emphasizing his success in securing devolved powers and advocating for the region. However, critics point to instances where his public statements and policy positions have appeared to evolve, raising questions about his core beliefs. The programme presents anecdotes that illustrate both his strategic prowess and his perceived adaptability. Several sources describe him as highly driven, with an unwavering focus on achieving his objectives, whether that be securing his party's leadership or advancing the agenda for his city-region. The documentary concludes that while Burnham's public image is subject to constant re-evaluation, his underlying ambition and capacity for political manoeuvring are undeniable, leaving a lingering question for many: what is the true blueprint for his future political journey?
