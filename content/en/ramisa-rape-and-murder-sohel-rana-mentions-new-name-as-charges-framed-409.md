The district court officially framed charges on Monday, marking a crucial step in the legal proceedings surrounding the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a case that has sent shockwaves across the nation. As the legal machinery gears up for trial, a startling disclosure has emerged from the accused, Sohel Rana. Sources close to the investigation reveal that Rana, under questioning, has implicated a previously unknown name in the commission of the crime. 

The details of this new name and the alleged involvement of the individual are yet to be fully disclosed by the prosecution. However, the mention of a new accused is expected to trigger further investigation by the law enforcement agencies. This development could potentially lead to the arrest and prosecution of additional individuals if their complicity is established. 

Ramisa's family, who have been relentless in their pursuit of justice, have expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension. While they welcome any leads that could bring all perpetrators to account, they remain cautious, emphasizing the need for thorough and unbiased investigation. The framing of charges had already been a moment of grim anticipation for the victims' loved ones, and this new revelation adds another layer of complexity to an already deeply distressing case. The court has set a new date for the next hearing, where the implications of Sohel Rana's statement are expected to be further explored.
