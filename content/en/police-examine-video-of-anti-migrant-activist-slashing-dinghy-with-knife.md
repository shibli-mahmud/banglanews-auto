Police in Kent, England, are reportedly examining footage that has surfaced online, depicting an incident involving an anti-migrant activist and a small inflatable boat, commonly known as a dinghy. The activist, identified as Daniel Thomas, who goes by the moniker Danny Tommo among his supporters, is alleged to have used a knife to slash the dinghy. The footage, which has drawn significant attention, was allegedly livestreamed by Thomas himself, providing a direct view of the actions. 

The incident is believed to have taken place in the vicinity of Dover, a port town that has frequently been in the news due to a surge in migrants attempting to cross the English Channel in small boats. Anti-migrant sentiment has been a growing concern in some parts of the UK, and Thomas is known for his vocal opposition to such crossings.

While the exact location and time of the incident are still being verified by law enforcement, the visual evidence has prompted an immediate response from the police. They are expected to determine if any laws have been broken, such as criminal damage or inciting hatred. The activist's supporters have defended his actions, framing them as a protest against perceived inaction by the government. However, critics have condemned the act as dangerous and potentially escalating tensions. The investigation is ongoing, and further details are expected to be released as the police gather more information.
