The Prime Minister's strong reaction comes after reports emerged detailing how a number of young offenders, convicted of serious sexual assault against underage victims, received non-custodial sentences. These outcomes have been widely condemned as lenient and out of step with public expectations for justice in such grave cases.  Starmer stated unequivocally that it was 'right' that these sentences were being urgently reviewed, signalling a commitment to address the perceived failings in the justice system.  The announcement of the review is intended to restore public confidence and ensure that sentencing in cases of sexual violence adequately reflects the severity of the crimes and the impact on victims.  Details surrounding the specific cases and the reasons for the original sentencing remain under scrutiny.  However, the government's immediate response indicates a recognition of the profound concern felt across the nation.  Advocacy groups for victims of sexual assault have welcomed the Prime Minister's statement, emphasizing the need for consistent and robust justice that prioritizes the safety and well-being of young people.  The urgent review is expected to examine judicial guidelines and sentencing practices, with the aim of preventing similar outcomes in the future and ensuring accountability for perpetrators of sexual violence.  The government has pledged transparency throughout the review process and promised to implement necessary changes to strengthen protections for vulnerable individuals.
