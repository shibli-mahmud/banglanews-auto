The United States Central Command (CENTCOM) confirmed in a statement that its forces carried out further strikes against Iran on the preceding night, marking the seventh consecutive day of military action. CENTCOM officials reiterated that the objective of these strikes is to dismantle and weaken Iran's ability to project power and pose threats within the region.  While specific targets were not detailed, the stated aim is to degrade Iran's offensive and defensive military infrastructure.

Concurrently, Iranian state media has reported a series of powerful explosions occurring near the Strait of Hormuz. The exact nature and origin of these blasts remain unconfirmed by independent sources, but Iranian authorities are investigating. The Strait of Hormuz is a critical chokepoint for global oil trade, and any disruption in this area carries significant economic implications worldwide.  The timing of these reported explosions, coinciding with sustained US strikes, has fueled speculation about a potential escalation of the ongoing conflict and heightened concerns about wider regional security. International observers are closely monitoring the situation, urging de-escalation and diplomatic solutions to prevent a broader confrontation. The US has previously stated its commitment to ensuring freedom of navigation in international waters, a stance that has been tested by recent events in the Persian Gulf.
