The development comes in the wake of a rigorous investigative report by the BBC's flagship current affairs programme, Panorama, in collaboration with The Times newspaper.

The investigation, which reportedly delved into various aspects of Mr. Sullivan's business dealings and public life, is understood to have uncovered material that has now been shared with law enforcement. While the precise nature of the 'new information' has not been publicly disclosed by Essex Police, their statement confirms that they are 'reviewing the details' and engaging with the relevant parties.

David Sullivan, a businessman with extensive interests in property and media, has been a co-owner of West Ham United since 2010. His tenure at the club has coincided with periods of both success and controversy. The BBC and The Times have a track record of producing in-depth investigative journalism, and their findings are expected to be significant.

Representatives for David Sullivan have yet to issue a formal statement in response to these latest developments. The police's confirmation suggests that the information provided is deemed sufficiently credible to warrant attention, though it is important to note that no allegations have been substantiated at this stage. Further updates are anticipated as Essex Police continue their review of the information presented.
