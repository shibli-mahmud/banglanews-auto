The fallout from Macklemore's abrupt exit from Ed Sheeran's 'Mathematics Tour' in North America has sent ripples through the remaining opening acts, leading to a complete withdrawal of support. Several artists, including Grammy-winning duo The 1975, and indie-rock band Noah Kahan, were slated to perform before Sheeran. However, in a significant turn of events, all have reportedly decided to pull out of their remaining dates on the tour.

Sources close to the situation suggest that the decision by the other support acts is a direct response to the removal of Macklemore. The rapper, who had been a guest performer on select dates, was allegedly dropped from the tour after he made public statements in support of Palestine during a recent concert. While the exact reasons for his removal have not been officially detailed by the tour organizers or Macklemore himself, it is widely understood within the music industry that political statements can sometimes lead to contract cancellations.

Ed Sheeran himself has addressed the controversy, asserting his non-involvement in the decision to drop Macklemore. In a statement circulated to media outlets, Sheeran clarified that he was not the one who made the call to remove the rapper from the tour. This stance aims to distance the headline act from the politically charged circumstances surrounding Macklemore's departure. The full implications of this mass withdrawal for the remainder of the tour, including potential adjustments to the show's structure or replacements for the departed acts, remain to be seen.
