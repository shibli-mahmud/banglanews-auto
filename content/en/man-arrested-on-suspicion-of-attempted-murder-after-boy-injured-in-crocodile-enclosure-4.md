A disturbing incident at a crocodile enclosure has led to the arrest of a man on suspicion of attempted murder. A three-year-old boy was found with critical injuries and was subsequently transported to Addenbrooke's Hospital. Police confirmed the boy is currently in a stable condition.

Details surrounding the exact nature of the incident remain scarce as investigations are ongoing. Cambridgeshire Police were called to the scene following reports of the injured child. Emergency services attended, and the boy received immediate medical attention before being taken to the hospital.

The man arrested is being held in police custody and will be questioned by detectives. Authorities have not yet released the identities of the boy or the arrested individual, citing the need to protect the ongoing investigation and the privacy of the child's family.

Investigators are appealing to the public for any information that may assist their inquiries. Anyone who was in the vicinity of the crocodile enclosure at the time of the incident and may have witnessed anything, no matter how insignificant it may seem, is urged to contact Cambridgeshire Police. The specific location of the enclosure has not been disclosed at this stage, but the incident has sent shockwaves through the local community, raising concerns about safety protocols at such facilities. Further updates are expected as the investigation progresses.
