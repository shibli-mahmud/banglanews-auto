Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham has lauded the importance of strong ties between the United Kingdom and the European Union, following a significant meeting with French President Emmanuel Macron at Downing Street. This encounter marks Mr. Burnham's first bilateral talks with President Macron, underscoring a growing recognition of the role sub-national leaders can play in international diplomacy. The discussions were understood to have covered a range of pressing issues, with a particular emphasis on the ongoing challenge of illegal migration across the English Channel. Both leaders are believed to have explored collaborative strategies to dismantle smuggling networks and address the root causes of migration. In addition to security concerns, the meeting also delved into avenues for enhanced defence cooperation between the UK and France. With the ongoing conflict in Ukraine remaining a critical global concern, the leaders reaffirmed their commitment to providing unwavering support to Kyiv and discussed ways to bolster their joint contributions to Ukraine's defence and reconstruction efforts. The positive tone of the meeting suggests a shared desire to foster constructive dialogue and cooperation on critical geopolitical matters, even as the UK navigates its post-Brexit relationship with the EU. Mr. Burnham's engagement highlights a pragmatic approach to international relations, seeking common ground on shared challenges.
