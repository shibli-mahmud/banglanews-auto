Cambridgeshire Police have arrested a man on suspicion of attempted murder after a three-year-old boy was critically injured near a crocodile enclosure. The incident, which occurred yesterday, saw the young child transported to Addenbrooke's Hospital. Authorities confirmed the boy is currently in a stable condition, though his injuries are described as critical.

Details surrounding the circumstances of the boy's injuries and his proximity to the crocodile enclosure remain scarce as the investigation is ongoing. Police have not yet released the identity of the arrested man or his relationship to the child. A spokesperson for Cambridgeshire Police stated that officers were called to the scene shortly after the incident was reported and that a swift arrest was made. The area where the incident took place has been cordoned off as forensic teams conduct their examinations.

Emergency services were quick to respond, with paramedics providing immediate care before the boy was airlifted to the hospital. The gravity of his injuries necessitated rapid medical intervention. Investigators are appealing to the public for any witnesses who may have seen or heard anything unusual in the vicinity of the enclosure leading up to the incident. Information from the public could prove vital in piecing together the events that led to this serious injury. The arrested man is being held at a local police station for questioning.
