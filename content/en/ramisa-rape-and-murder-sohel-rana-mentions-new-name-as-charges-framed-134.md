The District and Sessions Judge Court on Monday officially framed charges against Sohel Rana and his accomplices in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a case that has garnered widespread public outrage and demands for swift justice. During the proceedings, Sohel Rana, who has been in custody since his arrest, reportedly revealed the name of a previously unknown suspect. He stated that a man known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the commission of the crime, adding a new dimension to the ongoing investigation.

This unexpected disclosure has prompted the court and law enforcement agencies to consider further inquiries into 'Dollar's' alleged role. While details about this new suspect remain scarce, sources indicate that investigators will explore all avenues to identify and apprehend 'Dollar' if his involvement is corroborated. The prosecution aims to build a comprehensive case, and Rana's statement, if proven true, could significantly alter the trajectory of the trial.

The framing of charges signifies that the court has found sufficient grounds to proceed with a trial, where the prosecution will present evidence to prove the guilt of the accused beyond a reasonable doubt. The defense will, in turn, have the opportunity to present their arguments and challenge the evidence. The Ramisa case has been a focal point for discussions on women's safety and the efficacy of the justice system in Bangladesh. The addition of a new suspect is likely to extend the legal process, but authorities have pledged to ensure a thorough and transparent investigation to bring all perpetrators to justice.
