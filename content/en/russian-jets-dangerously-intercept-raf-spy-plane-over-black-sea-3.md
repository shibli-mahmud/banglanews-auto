The United Kingdom's Ministry of Defence has condemned the actions of Russian fighter jets after they reportedly intercepted a British Royal Air Force (RAF) RC-135 Rivet Joint surveillance aircraft in a 'dangerous' manner over the Black Sea. The incident occurred while the unarmed aircraft was conducting a 'routine international flight' in international airspace, a spokesperson for the Ministry stated. 

Details of the encounter, which took place on Monday, suggest that the Russian jets approached the Rivet Joint in a manner that posed a risk to its safety. While the Ministry did not elaborate on the specifics of the 'dangerous' intercept, such maneuvers can involve flying too close to the other aircraft, forcing sudden evasive actions, or potentially firing weapons. This event marks another instance of escalating military interactions between Russia and NATO forces operating in or near internationally recognized airspace. 

The UK government has raised concerns with its Russian counterparts regarding the intercept. The Black Sea region has been a focal point for geopolitical tensions, particularly since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. Both NATO and Russian aircraft frequently operate in the area, leading to a heightened risk of accidental encounters. The Ministry of Defence reiterated its commitment to freedom of navigation and operation in international airspace, emphasizing that the RAF aircraft was operating lawfully. The incident is expected to further strain diplomatic relations between the UK and Russia.
