A chilling account has emerged from a Thai school shooting, with a student bravely sharing the terrifying experience of witnessing her teacher being shot. The pupil, whose identity has been withheld for her protection, described the scene as chaos unfolded, stating, "I was standing right in front of my teacher when she was shot." The sudden eruption of violence left students and staff in a state of panic and disbelief.

In the immediate aftermath of the shooting, the student recounted the desperate measures she had to take to escape the deadly gunfire. Fearing for her life, she explained, "I had to climb over a school fence to flee." This desperate act of evasion highlights the sheer terror and disarray that characterized the incident. The attack, which tragically claimed the lives of five teachers, has sent shockwaves through the community and raised serious questions about school safety protocols. Authorities are currently investigating the motive behind the shooting and have launched a manhunt for the perpetrators. The psychological impact on the surviving students and staff is expected to be profound, with counseling services being made available to help them cope with the trauma. The incident serves as a grim reminder of the persistent threat of gun violence in educational institutions.
