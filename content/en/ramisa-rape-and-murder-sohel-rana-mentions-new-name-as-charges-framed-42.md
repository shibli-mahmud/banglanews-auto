Dhaka: The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken an unexpected twist with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, identifying a previously unknown accomplice. During the recent court session where charges were formally framed against him, Rana alleged that a man known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the crime. This revelation has sent ripples through the investigation, prompting authorities to expand their search and gather more information about this new suspect.

Sources close to the investigation suggest that 'Dollar' might be a key figure whose testimony or apprehension could shed further light on the events leading to Ramisa's tragic death. Police are reportedly working to ascertain the full identity and whereabouts of 'Dollar' based on Sohel Rana's statement. The framing of charges marks a crucial step in the judicial process, moving the case closer to a full trial. However, Rana's new assertion introduces complexities, potentially requiring further investigation and a revised approach to building the prosecution's case.

Ramisa's family, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, expressed a mix of hope and apprehension following this development. While the mention of another individual involved could lead to a more comprehensive understanding of the conspiracy, it also raises concerns about potential accomplices and the extent of their involvement. Legal experts anticipate that the court will now consider this new piece of evidence, and further interrogation of Sohel Rana might be conducted to elicit more details about 'Dollar's' role and connection to the crime. The investigation remains active as authorities strive to bring all perpetrators to justice.
