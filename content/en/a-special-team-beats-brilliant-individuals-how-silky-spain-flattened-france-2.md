Their "silky" style, often characterized by patient build-up play and fluid positional interchanges, was not just aesthetically pleasing but brutally effective. This victory, achieved with a blend of tactical discipline and understated brilliance, has propelled Spain into the deeper stages of the tournament and significantly altered the perception of their championship aspirations.
