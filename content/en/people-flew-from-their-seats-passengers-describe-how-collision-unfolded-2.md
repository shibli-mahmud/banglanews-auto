Eyewitness accounts paint a harrowing picture of the mid-air collision that occurred over a densely populated region this afternoon. Passengers on board the larger aircraft described a sudden, violent jolt, followed by scenes of chaos and panic. "It was like being in an earthquake, but in the sky," recounted one survivor, still visibly shaken. "People were thrown from their seats, luggage went everywhere. There was screaming."

The exact nature of the collision and the type of aircraft involved are still under investigation. However, it is understood that the incident involved a collision between a light aircraft and a commercial passenger plane. The East of England Ambulance Service has deployed a large contingent of emergency responders to the scene, confirming that nearly 90 individuals sustained injuries. Of these, over 30 are reported to be in serious condition, necessitating immediate transport to local hospitals.

Emergency services are working diligently to provide medical assistance and manage the aftermath of the incident. The area where the wreckage is believed to have fallen is a popular tourist spot, raising concerns about potential further casualties on the ground. Authorities are urging the public to avoid the area to allow emergency crews unimpeded access. Investigations into the cause of the collision are in their nascent stages, with aviation authorities expected to lead the inquiry. The focus for now remains on rescuing and treating the injured, and securing the crash sites.
