A murder investigation has been launched after the death of former Conservative Member of Parliament Ann Widdecombe at her home on Dartmoor. Devon and Cornwall Police confirmed that they are treating her death as suspicious and are actively searching for a suspect in connection with the incident. Officers were called to her property on Thursday morning, and a cordon has since been established around the residence.

While details remain scarce as the investigation is in its early stages, a spokesperson for the police stated that their primary focus is on locating a specific individual who they believe may have information relevant to Ms. Widdecombe's death. They have appealed to the public for any information that could assist in their search, urging anyone who saw anything unusual in the vicinity of her home on Dartmoor in the days leading up to Thursday to come forward.

Ann Widdecombe, a prominent figure in British politics for many years, served as the MP for Maidstone and then later for Cherwell South West from 1987 to 2005. Known for her strong conservative views and sharp wit, she became a familiar face on television following her parliamentary career. Her death has sent ripples of shock through political circles and her former constituency.

Investigators have not yet released further information regarding the circumstances of her death, citing the need to protect the integrity of the ongoing inquiry. The Dartmoor area, typically a tranquil rural landscape, is now a focal point of a significant police operation. Residents have expressed their dismay and concern, with many recalling Ms. Widdecombe as a private but respected figure in the local community.
