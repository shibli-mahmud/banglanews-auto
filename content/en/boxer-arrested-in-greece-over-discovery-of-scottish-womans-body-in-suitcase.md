The grim discovery was made by authorities in the coastal town of Porto Rafti, east of Athens, on Monday. Police were alerted to a suspicious suitcase left by the roadside. Upon opening it, they found the dismembered remains of a woman. Forensic analysis later confirmed the victim was Caroline Kane, a mother of two from Scotland, who had been reported missing by her family.

Investigators have since arrested Sharif Ahmadzai, a 29-year-old boxer. While details of his connection to Ms. Kane are still being investigated, reports suggest they may have known each other. Ahmadzai, who reportedly resides in the UK, was apprehended in Athens. He is expected to appear in court on Wednesday to face preliminary charges. The motive behind the alleged murder remains unclear, and the investigation is ongoing. Authorities are examining CCTV footage and interviewing witnesses to piece together Ms. Kane's final movements. The case has sent shockwaves through the local community and has drawn significant attention from international media due to its gruesome nature and the victim's nationality. The Greek police are cooperating with their Scottish counterparts as the investigation progresses.
