West Ham United Football Club has announced that its joint chairman and director, David Sullivan, has stepped down from his positions with immediate effect. The club confirmed that Sullivan’s departure comes after he was informed of 'serious historic allegations' that are reportedly on the verge of public disclosure. While the nature of these allegations remains undisclosed at this time, their gravity has prompted Sullivan’s decision to relinquish his immediate involvement with the club’s management.

Sullivan, who has been a co-owner and a driving force behind the club’s strategic direction for many years, has played a significant role in West Ham’s recent history, including their move to the London Stadium and various transfer dealings. His departure marks a significant shift in the club’s leadership structure.

This development comes at a critical juncture for West Ham, who are currently navigating a demanding Premier League season and European competition. The club has stated its commitment to transparency and will likely provide further updates as the situation develops. In the interim, the remaining leadership will assume Sullivan's responsibilities. The full implications of these allegations and Sullivan's resignation on the club's operations and future remain to be seen.
