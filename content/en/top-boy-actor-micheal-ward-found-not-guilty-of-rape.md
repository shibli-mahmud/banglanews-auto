London – Micheal Ward, the acclaimed actor celebrated for his portrayal of Jamie Tovell in the popular Netflix drama 'Top Boy', has been cleared of all charges of rape. The verdict was delivered by a jury at Snaresbrook Crown Court after a trial that captivated public attention. Ward had been accused of sexually assaulting a woman, who cannot be named for legal reasons, in September 2020. The alleged incident occurred after a night out in the Shoreditch area of east London.

During the proceedings, Ward firmly asserted that the sexual encounter with his accuser was entirely consensual. He presented his defense by detailing the events of the night, emphasizing that there was no coercion or lack of consent involved. The prosecution had argued that the complainant did not consent to the sexual act. However, the jury, after deliberating for several hours, returned a unanimous verdict of not guilty on all counts. Ward's legal team expressed their relief and satisfaction with the outcome, stating that he has consistently maintained his innocence. The actor, who has also appeared in films such as 'The Kitchen' and 'Empire of Dust', did not comment as he left the court. The acquittal brings an end to a challenging period for the rising star, allowing him to focus on his promising acting career.
