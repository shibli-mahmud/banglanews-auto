According to an investigative report, former President Donald Trump orchestrated a highly unusual and clandestine operation to depart from a NATO summit in Turkey, a move reportedly designed to counter perceived threats from Iran. The report claims that instead of taking the usual presidential aircraft, Air Force One, Trump was whisked away in a less conspicuous manner, with details suggesting he utilized a catering truck to reach a separate, unmarked aircraft. This unusual method of transport was reportedly part of a broader ruse intended to conceal his departure from White House journalists, who were allegedly led to believe Trump was still aboard Air Force One as it prepared to leave.

The elaborate deception was reportedly conceived in the aftermath of the U.S. drone strike that killed Iranian General Qassem Soleimani in January 2020. At the time, heightened tensions with Iran raised concerns about potential retaliatory attacks. The report alleges that the secrecy surrounding Trump's departure was a direct response to these security concerns, aiming to prevent any knowledge of his location from falling into enemy hands. The operation involved a complex series of maneuvers to move the President from the summit location to an alternate airfield, where another aircraft was waiting. The report further details that the departure was timed to coincide with the departure of Air Force One, further obscuring the President's actual movements. The incident, if accurate, highlights the extraordinary measures taken by the Trump administration to ensure the President's safety during a period of significant geopolitical instability.
