The prosecution is now faced with the immediate challenge of verifying this claim and potentially identifying and bringing 'Dollar' into the legal fold.

The implications of this revelation are substantial. It could indicate a wider conspiracy or a network of individuals involved in the crime, moving beyond the initial scope of the investigation. The framing of charges marks a pivotal moment, and this new development promises to add further complexity to an already sensitive and high-profile case.
