Sunday marked the hottest day of 2024 to date across the United Kingdom, as sweltering temperatures prompted widespread heat advisories. The mercury climbed to a remarkable 31.2°C in Chertsey, Surrey, according to provisional figures from the Met Office. This peak temperature easily surpassed the previous high of 29.7°C recorded in the same location just last Saturday.

In response to the intense heat, amber heat health alerts have been issued for large swathes of England, specifically covering the Midlands, eastern, and south-east regions. These alerts are designed to highlight the potential risks to health, particularly for the elderly, infants, and those with pre-existing medical conditions. Public health officials are advising individuals in these affected areas to stay hydrated, seek shade, and avoid strenuous activity during the hottest parts of the day.

The prolonged spell of warm weather is attributed to a southerly airflow bringing hot air from the continent. While many are enjoying the sunshine and outdoor activities over the bank holiday weekend, the rising temperatures serve as a reminder of the importance of heat safety. Forecasters predict that the warm conditions are likely to continue for several more days, although the most extreme temperatures may begin to ease slightly by Tuesday. Residents are encouraged to monitor Met Office updates and follow local guidance on staying safe in the heat.
