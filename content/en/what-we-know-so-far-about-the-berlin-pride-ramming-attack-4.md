Berlin awoke on Sunday to the disturbing aftermath of a violent incident that disrupted its annual Pride celebrations. On Saturday evening, a vehicle deliberately drove into a gathering of people participating in the Christopher Street Day (CSD) parade in the Schöneberg district. Emergency services were immediately dispatched to the scene, with reports indicating multiple injuries. While the exact number and severity of the injuries are still being confirmed, early reports suggest several individuals have been hospitalized.

The driver of the car, described by witnesses as a man, fled the scene on foot immediately after the attack. Police have launched a large-scale manhunt, appealing to the public for any information that could lead to the suspect's identification and apprehension. The motive behind the attack remains unclear, and authorities are exploring all possibilities, including whether it was a targeted act of violence or a random assault.

Security at the Pride event was heightened in recent years due to general concerns, but this incident has understandably amplified fears within the LGBTQ+ community. Officials have expressed solidarity with the victims and reiterated their commitment to ensuring the safety of future events. Investigations are ongoing, with forensic teams examining the vehicle and the immediate area for clues. The attack has cast a somber shadow over what is typically a joyous and celebratory occasion, highlighting the ongoing challenges faced by minority groups in asserting their right to public assembly and expression.
