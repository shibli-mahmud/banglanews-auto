The mercury soared across parts of England on Friday, marking the hottest day of the year to date. Temperatures climbed to a sweltering 28.4 degrees Celsius in certain areas, providing a welcome, albeit brief, respite from recent cooler weather for many. This summery spell arrived just in time for the Spring Bank Holiday weekend, prompting a surge in travel plans across the nation. 

However, the promise of sunshine and leisurely breaks was somewhat dampened by widespread travel delays. Passengers heading for continental Europe faced arduous queues at the Port of Dover, with ferry operators struggling to manage the volume of traffic. Similar scenes of congestion were observed at Birmingham Airport, where travellers reported lengthy waits for check-in and security, impacting the start of their holidays. 

Authorities attributed the delays to a combination of factors, including increased passenger numbers due to the holiday period and ongoing operational challenges at some ports and airports. While the weather offered a delightful backdrop for many, the persistent travel snarls served as a stark reminder of the logistical hurdles that can accompany peak travel times. Holidaymakers are advised to check travel updates and allow ample extra time for journeys throughout the weekend.
