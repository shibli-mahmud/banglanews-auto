This marks the first time this name has been brought into the investigation, and it has immediately raised questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the identity of all individuals involved in the brutal crime.

Authorities are now reportedly scrambling to identify and locate this individual, 'Dollar', to ascertain his role in the incident. The prosecution is expected to use this new information to potentially amend charges or pursue further investigations. The case, which has gripped the nation's attention due to its gruesome nature and the victim's young age, has now entered a more complex phase. Families of the victim and the accused, as well as the general public, are keenly awaiting further developments as the trial progresses. The mention of a new name could significantly impact the direction of the investigation and the eventual legal proceedings, potentially leading to a broader net being cast for those responsible for Ramisa's death.
