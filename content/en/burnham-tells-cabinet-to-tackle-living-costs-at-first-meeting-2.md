In his inaugural address to the cabinet, Prime Minister Keir Starmer emphasized that tackling the cost of living crisis must be the government's foremost priority.  He stressed the urgency of the situation, highlighting the severe impact of rising energy prices, inflation, and essential goods on ordinary citizens. Starmer's message underscored a commitment to providing tangible support and finding sustainable solutions to alleviate financial pressures.

"This is the defining challenge of our time, and every department must be geared towards helping people through this difficult period," Starmer stated. "We will not stand by while families struggle to heat their homes or put food on the table. Our focus will be on delivering practical measures that make a real difference."

The Prime Minister's directive was followed by the announcement of a VAT cut on domestic electricity. This measure, effective immediately, is expected to offer some respite to households facing unprecedented energy bills. The government also signaled its intention to explore further avenues for support, including measures to stabilize energy markets and address underlying inflationary pressures.

Cabinet ministers were reportedly instructed to present immediate action plans within their respective portfolios, focusing on how they can contribute to easing the financial burden on the public. This includes examining ways to enhance support for low-income households, stimulate economic growth, and ensure the affordability of essential services. The meeting set a tone of proactive engagement and a clear determination to confront the economic challenges head-on.
