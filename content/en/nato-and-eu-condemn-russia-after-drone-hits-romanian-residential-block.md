Romania, a member of both NATO and the European Union, has reported that fragments from a drone, suspected to be of Russian origin, landed on a residential building within its territory. The incident has sparked outrage and strong condemnation from both the transatlantic alliance and the EU, who are holding Russia accountable for the breach of Romanian airspace. 

According to the Romanian Ministry of National Defence, the drone was likely targeted and shot down by Ukrainian air defence systems while it was operating over Ukrainian territory. Following its destruction, the ministry stated, the drone's trajectory was altered, leading to its debris falling onto Romanian soil. While no casualties have been reported, the incident has raised serious concerns about the potential for spillover effects from the conflict in neighboring Ukraine.

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg reiterated the alliance's solidarity with Romania and emphasized that any attack on a NATO member would be met with a united and resolute response. The EU's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, Josep Borrell, also voiced his condemnation, labeling the incident as a serious escalation and a direct affront to NATO's territorial integrity. Both organizations are demanding accountability from Moscow and are closely monitoring the situation to assess any further implications for regional security.
