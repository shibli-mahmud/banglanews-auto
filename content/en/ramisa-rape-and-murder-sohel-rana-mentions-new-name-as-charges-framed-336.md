The framing of charges signifies the court's belief that there is sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial, moving the case from the investigative phase to the adjudication stage.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana, during questioning, implicated 'Dollar,' suggesting his active participation in the planning or execution of the crime. While the exact identity and whereabouts of 'Dollar' remain unclear at this juncture, law enforcement agencies are expected to launch a comprehensive search and investigation to locate and apprehend him. This development is likely to complicate the legal proceedings further, potentially leading to additional charges or a need to revisit the prosecution's strategy.

The Ramisa case, which has garnered widespread public attention and outrage, involves the brutal rape and murder of a young woman. The initial investigation led to the arrest of Sohel Rana, who has since been the central figure in the legal proceedings. The inclusion of a new name, 'Dollar,' raises questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the number of individuals involved.

Legal experts suggest that the prosecution will now need to gather substantial evidence to corroborate Rana's claims against 'Dollar.' This could involve further forensic analysis, witness testimonies, and digital evidence. The defense, on the other hand, may attempt to use this new information to create doubt or to shift blame.
