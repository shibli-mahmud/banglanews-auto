The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, revealing a new name during the framing of charges. This development, confirmed by sources close to the investigation, suggests that Rana may be attempting to shift blame or that there is indeed a wider network involved in the heinous crime. The court is expected to scrutinize this new information closely as it proceeds with the trial.

Ramisa's case, which has garnered widespread public attention and outrage, has been a protracted legal battle. The framing of charges marks a crucial stage, signaling the commencement of the trial proper. However, the introduction of a new suspect at this juncture complicates the established narrative and could potentially delay proceedings as further investigations might be deemed necessary. Authorities are reportedly working to ascertain the veracity of Rana's claims and to identify and apprehend the individual he has named.

Legal experts suggest that the timing of this revelation could be strategic, either by the accused to mitigate his own sentence or by the prosecution to build a stronger case by unearthing all conspirators. The victim's family has expressed a desire for swift justice and for all perpetrators to be brought to account. The police have assured the public that no stone will be left unturned in ensuring that justice is served in this deeply disturbing case.
