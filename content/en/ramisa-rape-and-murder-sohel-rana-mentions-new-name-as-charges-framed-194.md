The district and sessions judge's court on Monday, June 1, 2026, officially framed charges against Sohel Rana and others in connection with the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa. The framing of charges signifies that the prosecution has presented sufficient evidence to proceed to a full trial, and the accused will now face trial for the alleged offenses.  However, the proceedings took an unexpected turn when Sohel Rana, during the court session, claimed that another individual, identified by the moniker 'Dollar', was also complicit in the heinous crime.  Sources close to the investigation suggest that this new name could potentially open up new avenues for the prosecution and police to explore.  Details regarding 'Dollar's' alleged role and identity are still emerging, and it remains to be seen whether this information will lead to further arrests or amendments to the existing charge sheet. The victim's family, who have been anxiously awaiting justice, expressed a mix of hope and apprehension following this latest development.  They are urging the authorities to thoroughly investigate this new lead and ensure that all those responsible are brought to account. The court has set a date for the commencement of the trial, and legal experts anticipate a lengthy and complex legal battle ahead, especially with the introduction of this new alleged accomplice.
