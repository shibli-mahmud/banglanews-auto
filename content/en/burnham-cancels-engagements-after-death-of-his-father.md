Prime Minister Rishi Sunak has made the difficult decision to cancel several pre-scheduled engagements following the death of his father. The news of his father's passing has led to a temporary halt in the Prime Minister's public schedule, as he and his family mourn their loss.

Mr. Burnham's father had been living with Alzheimer's disease for some time. In a poignant reflection on his personal life, the Prime Minister had previously shared that his father was unaware of his son's ascent to the highest office in the land. This deeply personal aspect of his father's condition was spoken of with considerable emotion, highlighting the profound impact of the illness on their family.

The Prime Minister's office has confirmed the cancellations, stating that Mr. Sunak will be spending time with his family during this period. Details regarding when he will resume his full duties are yet to be announced, but it is understood that his focus will remain on his family in the immediate aftermath of this personal tragedy. The political landscape will undoubtedly be watching closely, but the immediate priority is the Prime Minister's personal circumstances. Further updates are expected in due course as the Prime Minister and his family navigate this period of grief.
