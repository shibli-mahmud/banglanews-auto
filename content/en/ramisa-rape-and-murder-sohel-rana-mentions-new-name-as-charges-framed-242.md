The charges were formally framed today, marking a significant step in the judicial process that has gripped the nation. However, Rana's statement has added a layer of complexity to an already sensitive investigation.

Details surrounding 'Dollar' remain scarce. Investigators are now under pressure to identify and locate this individual, who Rana alleges played a role in the brutal incident that led to Ramisa's death. This unexpected revelation occurred as the court was meticulously laying out the charges against the accused, preparing the case for trial. Legal analysts suggest that such disclosures during this stage can sometimes be strategic, aimed at influencing the proceedings or implicating others.

The Ramisa case has been under intense public scrutiny since the crime was first reported. The initial investigation had focused on a limited number of suspects, with Sohel Rana being the primary focus. His latest statement, however, suggests that the network of individuals involved might be wider than initially anticipated. Law enforcement agencies have stated they will thoroughly investigate this new lead, working to corroborate Rana's claims and determine the extent of 'Dollar's' alleged involvement.

The prosecution is expected to present a comprehensive case, building upon the existing evidence. The introduction of a new potential suspect, however, necessitates further diligence from all parties involved. The court will undoubtedly consider this new information as the trial progresses, and the pursuit of justice for Ramisa now includes a broadened scope of inquiry.
