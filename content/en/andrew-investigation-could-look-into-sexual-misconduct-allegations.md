The ongoing investigation into Prince Andrew, which initially focused on allegations of misconduct in public office stemming from his association with convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, may be set to expand. Sources close to the inquiry suggest that investigators are considering whether the scope of the investigation should be broadened to include allegations of sexual misconduct.  The former prince was arrested in February, and while the specifics of the initial charges remain somewhat guarded, the potential inclusion of sexual misconduct allegations signals a significant escalation in the scrutiny he faces.

This development comes amid mounting pressure for a thorough examination of Prince Andrew's past dealings and relationships.  The original investigation was launched following public outcry and renewed focus on his ties to Epstein, who was convicted of sex trafficking.  While the "misconduct in public office" charge implies impropriety in his former royal duties, the prospect of addressing direct sexual misconduct claims introduces a more serious and potentially criminal dimension to the inquiry.  Legal experts have noted that depending on the evidence unearthed, a wider net could be cast, potentially involving interviews with more individuals and a deeper dive into historical events.  The palace has maintained a policy of "no comment" on the specifics of the investigation, but the potential for this probe to encompass such serious allegations has undoubtedly amplified public interest and concern.
