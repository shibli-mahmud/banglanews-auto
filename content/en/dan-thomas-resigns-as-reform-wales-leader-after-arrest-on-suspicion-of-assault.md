Dan Thomas, who recently took the helm of the Reform Wales political party, has stepped down as its leader with immediate effect. The decision comes in the wake of his arrest by Gwent Police on suspicion of assault. The circumstances surrounding the alleged incident remain unclear, and the police have not yet released further details.

In a statement released following the news of his resignation, Thomas asserted his innocence. "I want to be absolutely clear that I have done nothing wrong," he stated. He indicated that he is cooperating fully with the police investigation and expects to be cleared of any wrongdoing. The arrest has sent ripples through the nascent political movement, which aims to champion Welsh independence and reform. Reform Wales has been actively campaigning on a platform of national sovereignty and addressing local concerns, but this personal matter now overshadows its political agenda.

Party officials have acknowledged the seriousness of the situation and have stated their commitment to transparency. They are reportedly in discussions regarding the interim leadership of Reform Wales and how to navigate this challenging period. The resignation of its leader at such an early stage of its development presents a significant hurdle for the party, which has been seeking to build momentum and establish its presence in Welsh politics. The focus for the party now shifts to internal stability and ensuring that the allegations do not derail its broader objectives. Further updates are expected as the police investigation progresses.
