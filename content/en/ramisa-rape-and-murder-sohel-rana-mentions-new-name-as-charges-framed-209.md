Dhaka: The judicial process in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken an unexpected turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, naming a hitherto unknown individual, referred to as 'Dollar', as a potential accomplice. The charges were formally framed in the court of Additional Sessions Judge on Monday, June 1, 2026, marking a crucial stage in the legal proceedings.

Sources close to the investigation reveal that Rana, when confronted with the charges, provided an unsolicited statement, alleging that 'Dollar' was also involved in the heinous crime. The motive behind Rana's sudden revelation remains unclear, but it has prompted the prosecution to consider further investigation into this new lead. Legal experts suggest that this could be a strategy by the accused to deflect blame or a genuine attempt to disclose further information. Regardless, the court has taken cognisance of Rana's statement and the prosecution is likely to be directed to look into the existence and potential involvement of this 'Dollar'.

The Ramisa case has garnered considerable public attention and outrage due to its gruesome nature and the perceived urgency for justice. The framing of charges signifies that the court believes there is sufficient evidence to proceed with a full trial. However, the introduction of a new name at this juncture could potentially lead to delays if further investigations are required. The prosecution team has indicated they will review Rana's statement carefully and will seek court permission for further inquiries if deemed necessary. The next hearing is scheduled for later this month, where the court will likely address the implications of this new information.
