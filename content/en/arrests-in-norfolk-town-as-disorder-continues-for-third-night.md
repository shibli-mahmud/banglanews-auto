The small market town in Norfolk has been the scene of significant unrest over the past three nights, culminating in a series of arrests as police worked to contain escalating disorder. The events are understood to be connected to demonstrations with an anti-immigration focus, which have drawn a strong police response. On the latest night of disturbances, officers were deployed in force, leading to the apprehension of dozens of individuals. Details regarding the specific charges are still emerging, but the arrests are a direct consequence of the persistent public disorder that has gripped the community.

Local residents have expressed growing concern over the sustained disruption, with reports of intimidation and damage to property. The mayor of the town issued a statement earlier today, urging for an end to the violence and calling for dialogue to address any underlying grievances. He emphasized the importance of maintaining peace and safety for all residents and visitors. The police have stated that their operation will continue until order is fully restored and have appealed to the public to avoid the affected areas. The prolonged nature of the unrest has placed a considerable strain on local resources and has cast a shadow over the normally tranquil town. Further updates are expected as investigations progress and charges are formally laid.
