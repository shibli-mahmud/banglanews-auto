The victim, whose identity is protected, detailed the immense emotional and psychological toll the legal process took. She recounted the courage it required to relive her trauma by testifying and facing her perpetrators in court.  However, the subsequent decision by the judge, which resulted in suspended sentences rather than immediate imprisonment for the two teenage boys convicted of the rape, left her feeling betrayed and re-victimized.  "Why did I put myself through all of that pain, all of that reliving, if this is the outcome?" she questioned, her voice heavy with despair. She expressed a deep sense of disillusionment with the justice system, feeling that her suffering had been minimized and her attackers' actions insufficiently punished.  This experience has not only exacerbated her trauma but also raised critical questions about sentencing guidelines and the support systems available for victims of sexual assault.  Her courageous account serves as a stark reminder of the long and arduous journey survivors face, and the profound impact that judicial decisions can have on their path to healing and regaining a sense of safety and justice.
