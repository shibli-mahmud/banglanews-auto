A sea of white and three lions adorned the iconic Trafalgar Square and surrounding areas as England fans reveled in their team's dominant performance against Norway. The final whistle was met with an explosion of joy, as flares illuminated the night sky and the roar of the crowd echoed through the city.  Beer flowed freely, and strangers embraced in shared elation, the victory a sweet reward for the nation's unwavering support.

"It was incredible! The energy was electric, and the team played out of their skin," exclaimed Sarah Jenkins, a lifelong England supporter, her voice hoarse from singing. "We've been waiting for this. This is our year!"

However, amidst the revelry, a palpable shift in sentiment soon became evident. As the initial euphoria subsided, conversations inevitably turned towards the next stage of the tournament. The prospect of facing football powerhouse Argentina loomed large in the minds of many.

"We've done our job, now it's time to focus," declared Mark Davies, hoisting a miniature St. George's Cross flag. "Argentina are going to be a tough test, but this England team has the grit and the talent. We're telling them, 'Get ready, Argentina!' This squad is hungry."

The sentiment was echoed across the jubilant crowd, with chants of "Argentina, get ready!" starting to mingle with the existing cheers. The victory against Norway was a significant milestone, but for these passionate fans, it was merely a stepping stone towards their ultimate World Cup dream.
