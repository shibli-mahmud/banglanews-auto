A planned balloon release to commemorate a young man who lost his life in a devastating A66 car crash descended into chaos on Saturday, with disturbing footage circulating online showing vehicles being set on fire. The incident, which took place in a public area, has drawn sharp criticism from the local MP, [MP's Name], who has labelled the actions as 'completely unacceptable' and a serious threat to public safety. The memorial event, intended to honour the deceased and offer a moment of reflection for friends and family, was reportedly attended by a large number of people, many of whom were in vehicles. Eyewitness accounts and video evidence depict scenes of disorder, including the deliberate ignition of at least one car, sending plumes of thick smoke into the air and creating a dangerous environment. Police were reportedly called to the scene, and an investigation into the disturbances is understood to be underway. The MP has expressed deep concern, stating, 'This is not a fitting tribute, and by engaging in such reckless behaviour, these individuals have put the lives of innocent members of the public, and indeed themselves, at grave risk. Arson is a serious crime, and the emergency services were put under unnecessary strain. We must remember the victims of the A66 crash with dignity, not through dangerous and destructive acts.' The A66 crash itself resulted in multiple fatalities, sparking widespread grief in the community and calls for improved road safety measures.
