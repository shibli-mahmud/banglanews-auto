Prosecutors are now meticulously reviewing the new information to ascertain its relevance and potential to strengthen their case.

This might involve re-examining existing evidence, gathering new witness testimonies, and establishing the new individual's alleged role in the commission of the crime. The defense team, on the other hand, may attempt to leverage this new development to their advantage. 

The victim's family, who have been patiently awaiting justice, have responded with a mixture of hope and apprehension. They are urging the authorities to thoroughly investigate the claims and ensure that every person responsible for Ramisa's horrific fate is brought to account. The family's primary concern remains to see justice served and to find a measure of peace through the successful prosecution of all involved.
