The Ramisa rape and murder case, which has gripped the nation's attention, took a dramatic turn on Monday when the prime accused, Sohel Rana, brought forth a new name during the charge framing proceedings. Rana, who is already facing severe charges related to the heinous crime, claimed that a previously unmentioned individual, known only by the moniker 'Dollar', was also involved in the incident. This unexpected revelation has prompted immediate calls for a thorough reinvestigation into the alleged accomplice's role.

The legal team representing the victim's family has expressed both surprise and a renewed sense of urgency. They emphasized the need for the authorities to swiftly apprehend and question this 'Dollar' to ascertain the extent of his alleged involvement. The prosecution, while acknowledging the new information, stated that they would review the evidence and proceed with the charges as framed, while also exploring the credibility of Rana's latest statement. 

Details surrounding 'Dollar' remain scarce, with speculation rife about his identity and connection to the other accused. Security around the courtroom and the prison where Rana is held has been reportedly heightened. The police have stated they are actively pursuing leads to identify and locate the individual. This case, already marked by its brutality and public outcry, now faces the challenge of unraveling a potentially wider conspiracy. The framing of charges marks a crucial step, but the mention of 'Dollar' promises a more protracted and intricate legal battle ahead, as justice for Ramisa remains the paramount concern.
