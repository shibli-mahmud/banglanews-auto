Berlin was shaken on Saturday night when a car intentionally drove into a crowd gathered for the city's annual Pride parade. The attack, which occurred around 9:30 PM local time on the Kurfürstendamm, a major boulevard in the western part of the city, sent shockwaves through the LGBTQ+ community and beyond. Emergency services were quickly on the scene, treating numerous injured individuals. While the exact number of casualties is still being confirmed, initial reports indicated several people sustained serious injuries, with some requiring hospitalization. The driver of the vehicle, who is believed to be acting alone, fled the scene on foot. However, police apprehended a suspect a short distance away. Authorities have not yet released the identity of the suspect or their potential motives. However, the nature of the attack and the target—a major LGBTQ+ celebration—has led investigators to consider the possibility of a politically or ideologically driven act. The public prosecutor's office has taken over the investigation, and a special unit has been assigned to the case. Security measures around similar events in Germany are likely to be reviewed in light of this incident. The Pride organizers have expressed their deep shock and solidarity with those affected, emphasizing that such acts of violence will not deter their commitment to inclusivity and celebration.
