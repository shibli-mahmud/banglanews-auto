The National Police Chiefs' Council (NPCC) has expressed significant dissatisfaction with the "late announcement" concerning extended pub opening hours for England's upcoming Euro 2024 fixture. The decision, allowing licensed premises to remain open until 5:00 AM on Monday, has been met with frustration from law enforcement officials who argue that the timing has created a logistical challenge. 

According to the NPCC, such last-minute policy changes necessitate the reallocation of police officers from their regular duties. This diversion of personnel can impact the force's ability to respond to other calls for service, potentially affecting public safety and routine policing operations across the country. 

Chiefs have highlighted that operational planning relies on timely information to ensure adequate staffing and resource deployment. A decision made with such short notice makes it difficult for forces to make necessary arrangements without compromising other essential policing activities. The council stated that while they understand the public's desire to celebrate and support the national team, the manner in which such decisions are communicated needs to be more considerate of the operational demands placed upon the police. 

This criticism underscores a recurring concern within police ranks about the impact of government policy announcements, particularly those affecting public events, on their ability to effectively manage resources and maintain public order. The NPCC is urging for earlier consultation and communication in the future to allow for better planning and to mitigate any potential negative consequences on police operations.
