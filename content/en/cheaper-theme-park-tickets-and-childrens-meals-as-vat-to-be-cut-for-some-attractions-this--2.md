In a move set to delight families and boost the tourism sector, Chancellor Rachel Reeves has unveiled plans for a temporary VAT reduction on certain theme park tickets and a permanent cut on VAT for children's meals. This strategic announcement comes as part of a broader package of measures aimed at addressing the ongoing cost of living crisis, with a particular focus on easing pressures during the summer months. The VAT cut for theme parks, though specific to certain attractions, is expected to make day trips and family holidays more affordable, encouraging domestic tourism and providing a welcome respite for families struggling with rising expenses. The reduction is anticipated to be in effect throughout the summer, offering a tangible benefit during a popular period for leisure activities.

Furthermore, the Chancellor confirmed a permanent reduction in VAT for all children's meals served in pubs and restaurants. This long-standing demand from the hospitality industry and families alike is expected to make dining out more accessible for those with younger children. The move is seen as a positive step towards supporting families and ensuring that dining out remains an enjoyable, rather than a prohibitive, option. These fiscal adjustments are part of the government's commitment to provide targeted relief and support economic recovery, with the hope that such measures will inject confidence back into consumer spending and provide a much-needed boost to key sectors of the economy.
