FIFA President Gianni Infantino has strongly refuted accusations that UEFA paid off an alleged former lover during his tenure as the organization's general secretary. The allegations, brought to light by a Daily Telegraph investigation, claim that a significant severance package was provided to a woman with whom Infantino is said to have had a relationship.  The report suggests that this payment was made while Infantino was in a senior leadership role at UEFA, between 2009 and 2016.

UEFA, in a statement released in response to the investigation, has categorically denied any wrongdoing. The organization stated that the severance payment in question was made to an employee who was departing the organization under agreed terms, and that there was no connection to any alleged personal relationship with Infantino.  They further emphasized that all payments made by UEFA are subject to strict financial controls and due diligence.

Gianni Infantino, who ascended to the FIFA presidency in 2016, has consistently maintained his integrity throughout his career. This latest claim casts a shadow over his past leadership at UEFA, a period that saw him instrumental in various reforms and initiatives within European football.  The controversy is likely to draw further scrutiny from football's governing bodies and the wider media, particularly given the sensitive nature of the allegations and their potential implications for Infantino's reputation and leadership at FIFA.
