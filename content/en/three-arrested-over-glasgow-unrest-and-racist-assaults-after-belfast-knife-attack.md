In the wake of a disturbing knife attack in Belfast, a wave of unrest and racially motivated assaults has swept through Glasgow, leading to the arrest of three people by Police Scotland. The incidents, which took place on Tuesday evening, saw members of the public targeted and attacked specifically because of their ethnicity and skin colour.  Authorities have stated that the investigations into these violent acts are ongoing and are exploring a potential connection to the earlier incident in Northern Ireland.  The nature of the Belfast attack, while not fully detailed, has reportedly inflamed tensions and is being considered as a catalyst for the subsequent violence in Glasgow.  Police Scotland has condemned the racist assaults, emphasizing their zero-tolerance policy towards hate crimes and urging anyone with information to come forward.  The arrests were made following a swift police response to reports of disturbances and assaults across the city.  Further details regarding the identities of the arrested individuals and the specifics of the charges are expected to be released as the investigation progresses.  The incidents have sparked widespread concern and condemnation from community leaders and the public, highlighting the persistent issue of racism and its potential to ignite further conflict.  Authorities are working to ensure the safety of all communities and prevent any escalation of tensions.
