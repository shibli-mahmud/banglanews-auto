The Met Office has issued severe weather warnings as temperatures continue to climb, leaving large swathes of the country tinder-dry. In response to the escalating wildfire threat, Mayor Andy Burnham is reportedly exploring the feasibility of implementing a ban on disposable barbecues. These readily available and affordable grills, a common feature of summer gatherings, are a significant fire hazard when used in dry, grassy areas. Officials are increasingly worried about the potential for these devices to ignite surrounding vegetation, leading to devastating fires that are difficult to control and can have a profound impact on local ecosystems and communities.

The potential ban comes as fire services across the UK report a surge in incidents. In recent weeks, several large-scale wildfires have broken out, requiring extensive resources to extinguish. While the exact details of a potential ban are yet to be confirmed, it is understood that the Mayor's office is in discussions with relevant authorities to assess the legal and practical implications. The public is being urged to exercise extreme caution when outdoors and to avoid any activities that could inadvertently start a fire. The current spell of dry weather is expected to continue, amplifying the need for responsible behaviour to prevent further incidents and protect valuable natural landscapes. A decision on the ban is anticipated in the coming days, as authorities grapple with balancing public enjoyment of outdoor spaces with the urgent need for fire safety.
