The community's hopes were dashed on Tuesday night when a body was discovered during the extensive search for a 12-year-old boy who had gone missing in the river. The alarm was raised earlier in the day, prompting a swift and coordinated response from multiple emergency services. Police officers, alongside fire brigades and highly trained underwater search and rescue teams, scoured the water and its surrounding banks for any sign of the young boy. Eyewitnesses reported a significant presence of emergency vehicles and personnel throughout the afternoon and into the evening, underscoring the gravity of the situation. The operation involved divers meticulously examining the riverbed and currents, while surface teams maintained a vigilant watch. Despite the best efforts of all involved, the search concluded with the recovery of a body, which authorities believe to be the missing child. While formal identification is pending, the discovery has sent ripples of grief through the local area. The incident serves as a somber reminder of the inherent dangers associated with waterways, especially for young individuals. Investigations into the circumstances leading to the boy's disappearance are ongoing, with officials urging anyone with information to come forward.
