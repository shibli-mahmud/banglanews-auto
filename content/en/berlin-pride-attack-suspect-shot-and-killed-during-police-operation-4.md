Berlin authorities confirmed on Saturday that the man believed to be responsible for a knife attack that injured several people near a Berlin Pride event has been shot and killed by police. The suspect, identified as 32-year-old Abdul Ballout, was confronted by officers in the western borough of Spandau. According to a police spokesperson, Ballout produced a knife and advanced aggressively towards the officers, who then opened fire. The incident occurred late on Friday evening, casting a shadow over the city's vibrant LGBTQ+ community and the upcoming Pride celebrations. The motive behind the attack remains under investigation, though initial reports suggest it may have been religiously motivated.  Ballout was reportedly known to security services for past extremist associations, though he had no prior convictions.  The injured victims are reported to be in stable condition. The German government has condemned the attack and reiterated its commitment to protecting the rights and safety of the LGBTQ+ community. Investigations are ongoing to determine the full circumstances of both the attack and the subsequent police operation.
