The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a new turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, identifying a hitherto unknown individual named 'Dollar' as being involved in the crime. This disclosure occurred as the court formally framed charges against Rana and his co-accused, a procedural step that moves the case towards an indictment and potential trial. The framing of charges indicates that the prosecution has submitted enough preliminary evidence to warrant a full judicial inquiry. Rana's assertion that 'Dollar' was also complicit in the heinous act adds a layer of complexity to the ongoing investigation, suggesting a wider network of individuals may have been involved in the planning or execution of the crime. Law enforcement agencies are reportedly prioritizing the identification and apprehension of this new suspect, 'Dollar,' to explore his potential connection to Ramisa's murder and gather crucial corroborating evidence. The prosecution team is committed to assembling a comprehensive case, meticulously presenting all collected evidence, including forensic reports and witness statements, to secure a conviction and deliver justice for the victim and her grieving family. The formal framing of charges represents a significant milestone in the pursuit of accountability, reinforcing the legal process and providing a measure of reassurance to a community deeply affected by the tragedy.
