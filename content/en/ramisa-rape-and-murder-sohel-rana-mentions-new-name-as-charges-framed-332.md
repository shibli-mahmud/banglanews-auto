The highly publicized Ramisa rape and murder case took an unexpected twist today as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, brought forth a new name during the charge framing session at the district court. Rana alleged that a man identified only as 'Dollar' was also complicit in the heinous crime that has gripped the nation.

The court, which had convened to formally indict Rana and other potential co-conspirators based on the evidence gathered by the investigation agency, witnessed a significant development when Rana, under oath, pointed fingers at this previously unmentioned individual. The precise nature of 'Dollar's' alleged involvement remains unclear, but Rana's statement has prompted the prosecution to consider further investigation into this new lead.

Sources within the courtroom indicated that the judge acknowledged Rana's statement, though no immediate action was taken against 'Dollar'. The court has decided to proceed with the charge framing against the existing accused, while reserving the right to re-examine the case should further evidence surface regarding 'Dollar's' purported role. This development has introduced a new layer of complexity to an already sensitive and high-profile investigation.

The victim, Ramisa, a young woman whose brutal death ignited widespread public outcry and demands for swift justice, deserves a thorough and transparent trial. The inclusion of this new name, if substantiated, could significantly alter the trajectory of the legal proceedings. The court has adjourned for the day, with the next hearing scheduled for a later date in June, during which the charges are expected to be officially read and the defense's response solicited. The public awaits further updates with bated breath, hoping for conclusive answers and justice for Ramisa.
