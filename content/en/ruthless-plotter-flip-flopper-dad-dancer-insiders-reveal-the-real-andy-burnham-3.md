Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham, a prominent figure in British politics, is often characterized by a series of seemingly contradictory labels: a ruthless political strategist, an indecisive 'flip-flopper', and even a self-deprecating 'dad dancer'. But who is the real Andy Burnham? BBC's Laura Kuenssberg dives deep into the man behind the public persona, speaking to more than 20 individuals who have worked closely with him, from former parliamentary colleagues and advisors to friends and family.

The findings paint a complex picture. Some insiders describe a fiercely ambitious politician, adept at navigating the cut-and-thrust of Westminster, capable of meticulous planning and decisive action when necessary. They point to his strategic rise through the Labour Party ranks and his assertive approach to devolved powers for Greater Manchester as evidence of his political acumen. This perspective often highlights his ability to command attention and steer narratives, suggesting a sharp, calculating mind at work.

Conversely, other sources emphasize a more cautious and sometimes hesitant approach, leading to accusations of being a 'flip-flopper'. These accounts suggest that Burnham is a politician who genuinely listens and can be swayed by new information or changing public opinion, rather than a master manipulator. They suggest his pragmatism and desire to find consensus can sometimes be misconstrued as a lack of conviction.

Then there's the lighter side, the 'dad dancer' persona, which Burnham himself has embraced with a degree of self-awareness. Insiders confirm a relatable, often jovial personality beneath the political veneer, someone who doesn't take himself too seriously and can connect with people on a personal level. This aspect, while seemingly trivial, is presented as crucial to understanding his appeal and his ability to resonate with a broad electorate.

Kuenssberg's exploration aims to reconcile these disparate views, suggesting that the 'real' Andy Burnham is likely a blend of all these characteristics. The report explores how his ambition fuels his drive, how his listening ear can lead to perceived indecision, and how his down-to-earth nature complements his political aspirations. By speaking to those who know him best, the investigation offers a nuanced portrait of a politician who continues to intrigue and shape the political landscape.
