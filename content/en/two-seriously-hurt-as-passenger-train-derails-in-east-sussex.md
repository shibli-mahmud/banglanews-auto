A passenger train has derailed in East Sussex, leading to two people being rushed to hospital with serious injuries. The incident occurred on Monday morning, with emergency services swiftly arriving at the scene to attend to the casualties and evacuate passengers.  Eyewitness accounts paint a harrowing picture of the event. One passenger, who was traveling with his six-year-old daughter, recounted the terrifying moments, stating, "It was pandemonium... I could feel the carriage rocking." He described a sudden jolt and the unsettling sensation of the train's movement, which instilled immediate fear among those onboard.

Paramedics and fire crews are working to secure the site and ensure the safety of everyone involved. The cause of the derailment is not yet known and an investigation is expected to commence.  Network Rail has confirmed that services in the affected area are suspended, causing significant disruption to rail travel. Passengers are being advised to check with their train operators for the latest updates on services and alternative arrangements. The full extent of the damage to the train and the track is still being assessed by authorities. Further details are expected to be released as the situation develops.
