This victory not only delighted the home crowd but also cemented England's best men's World Cup finish since they lifted the trophy on home soil in 1966.

France, however, responded with characteristic flair, leveling the score through a moment of individual brilliance.  The goals continued to flow, with both sides exchanging blows in a captivating display of offensive prowess.  Saka completed his hat-trick with two further impressive strikes, showcasing his composure and lethal finishing.

Despite France's valiant efforts to claw their way back into the game, England's attacking depth and defensive solidity proved too much.  The final whistle confirmed a deserved victory for Gareth Southgate's men, who have undoubtedly captured the hearts of the nation with their spirited performances throughout the tournament.
