This unexpected disclosure has prompted law enforcement agencies to prioritize the identification and apprehension of this new individual.

The development occurs at a crucial juncture as the legal process moves towards the framing of formal charges against the existing suspects. The inclusion of a new name could necessitate a review of the evidence collected so far and potentially lead to further arrests. Sources close to the investigation indicate that the authorities are treating this information with utmost seriousness and are dedicating resources to verify Rana's claims and locate the person in question.

The Ramisa case has gripped the nation, marked by public outcry and demands for swift justice. The initial arrests brought some measure of relief, but this latest revelation from the prime accused adds a new dimension to the pursuit of truth. The prosecution is expected to present its case, and the framing of charges will be a critical step in moving the trial forward. The mention of a new suspect by Sohel Rana, however, raises questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the potential for more individuals to be brought to account. The coming days are likely to be pivotal as investigators work to corroborate Rana's statement and incorporate any new findings into the formal charges.
