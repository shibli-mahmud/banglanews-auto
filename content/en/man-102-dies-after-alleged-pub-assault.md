Police have confirmed the death of Phillip Ormerod, aged 102, following an alleged assault at a pub in Accrington, Lancashire. Mr. Ormerod was reportedly taken to Royal Blackburn Hospital with critical injuries and sadly passed away shortly after. The incident is believed to have occurred at The Market Tavern on Peel Street on Saturday evening.  A spokesperson for Lancashire Police stated that officers were called to the pub at approximately 9:30 PM following reports of an altercation. Mr. Ormerod was attended to by paramedics at the scene before being transported to hospital. Detectives are now appealing for witnesses who may have been present at the pub during the evening and saw the incident unfold.  A 51-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of grievous bodily harm and has since been bailed pending further inquiries. The investigation is ongoing, and authorities are urging anyone with information to come forward. The news has sent shockwaves through the local community, with many expressing their disbelief and sadness at the death of a man of such advanced age under such violent circumstances.  Mr. Ormerod was a well-known figure in the area, and tributes have begun to pour in.
