Retired couple, Richard and Carole Davies, recounted their unsettling encounter with a Russian warship in the English Channel on BBC Newsnight. They stated that the incident, which involved warning shots being fired in their vicinity, was a 'surreal' experience. The couple, who were sailing on their yacht, described their efforts to communicate with the warship, explaining that they had changed their planned course. Despite their attempts to signal, the warship proceeded to fire shots in their direction, causing significant alarm.  The Royal Navy confirmed that the Russian warship, the RFS Vice-Admiral Kulakov, was exercising in the area and that the shots were indeed warning rounds.  However, the Davies' account highlights the tension and potential dangers faced by civilian vessels operating in proximity to military exercises, particularly those involving foreign navies.  The couple emphasized the frightening nature of the event, noting the proximity of the shots and the intimidating presence of the warship. Their experience serves as a stark reminder of the unpredictable nature of maritime operations and the importance of clear communication and de-escalation in international waters.
