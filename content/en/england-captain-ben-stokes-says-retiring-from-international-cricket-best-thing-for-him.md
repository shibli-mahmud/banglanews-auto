Ben Stokes, the talismanic former captain of England's Test team, has candidly discussed the profound impact his decision to retire from international cricket had on his life. Speaking recently, Stokes described the immense pressure and relentless schedule as leading to a state of severe burnout, making the retirement not just a consideration, but a necessity for his own well-being.  He elaborated on how the constant demands, both physically and mentally, had taken a significant toll, impacting his ability to perform at the highest level and, more importantly, his overall happiness.

Stokes, who led England to a historic Ashes victory in 2019 and played a pivotal role in their T20 World Cup triumph in 2022, admitted that the build-up to the English summer had been particularly challenging. He felt his body and mind were no longer capable of sustaining the intense commitment required for international cricket.  The all-rounder's decision sent shockwaves through the cricketing world, with many acknowledging the immense sacrifice he had made throughout his illustrious career. Stokes emphasized that stepping away from the international stage was ultimately the 'best thing' he could have done for himself, allowing him to focus on his physical recovery and mental health, and to continue enjoying the sport he loves in a more sustainable capacity, primarily in domestic competitions. This introspection highlights the growing awareness and importance of mental health within professional sports.
