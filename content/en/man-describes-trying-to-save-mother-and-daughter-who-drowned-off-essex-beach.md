Craig Bowe has bravely shared his distressing account of the tragic incident that saw a mother and her young daughter lose their lives off the coast of Shoebury East Beach in Essex. Bowe was on a family outing to celebrate his daughter's birthday when he heard desperate cries for help emanating from the water.  Without hesitation, he ran towards the source of the screams, spotting the struggling pair and immediately entering the choppy sea in a bid to rescue them.

"I heard screams... I thought it was kids playing at first," Bowe recounted, his voice heavy with emotion. "Then I realised it was serious and I just ran down and got in the water."

Despite his courageous efforts, the conditions and the severity of the situation proved overwhelming. Bowe described the immense difficulty of reaching the mother and daughter and the overwhelming sense of helplessness he experienced as he fought against the unforgiving elements.  Emergency services, including the RNLI lifeboat and coastguard helicopter, were quickly on the scene. However, despite their rapid response and extensive search efforts, the woman and child could not be saved.  The community has been left in shock and mourning following the devastating incident, with Bowe's bravery in attempting a rescue coming in for significant praise amidst the profound sadness.
