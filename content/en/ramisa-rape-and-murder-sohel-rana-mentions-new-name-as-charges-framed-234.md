Dhaka: In a dramatic turn of events during the charges framing hearing at a Dhaka court on Monday, prime accused Sohel Rana, who is facing charges in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, implicated a new individual in the crime. Rana, a local businessman with alleged underworld connections, claimed that a man identified only by the moniker 'Dollar' was also involved in the conspiracy and execution of the murder of the young woman. The court was assembled to formally lay out the charges against Rana and any co-accused, marking a significant step in the judicial process.

However, Rana's unexpected revelation has thrown a curveball, potentially requiring further investigation and possibly amending the charges. He did not provide extensive details about 'Dollar's' identity or role, leaving the prosecution and the court to ponder the implications of this new information. The prosecution team is expected to thoroughly investigate Rana's claims, examining any corroborating evidence that might exist. If 'Dollar' is indeed found to be involved, it could lead to a broader network of perpetrators being brought to justice. The defense, on the other hand, might see this as an attempt by Rana to deflect blame or reduce his own culpability. The case, which has already garnered significant public attention and outcry due to its horrific nature, now faces a new layer of complexity. The court has adjourned, and further proceedings will depend on the outcome of the investigation into this newly named individual.
