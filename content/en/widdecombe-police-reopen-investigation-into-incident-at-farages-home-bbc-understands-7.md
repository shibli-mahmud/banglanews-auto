This re-examination comes as the same unit is already engaged in a broader inquiry into the circumstances surrounding Mr. Farage's death, which occurred recently. 

Sources close to the investigation suggest that the reopened inquiry into the burglary attempt is not being treated as a standalone event, but rather as a potential element that could shed light on the wider investigation. While details of the attempted burglary remain scarce, the decision to revisit it indicates a growing concern among investigators that it may have relevance to the ongoing probe into Mr. Farage's demise. 

Counter-terrorism units are often involved in cases with sensitive political dimensions or where there is a perceived threat to national security. The involvement of such a unit in the investigation into Mr. Farage's death, and now the reopening of the burglary inquiry, underscores the seriousness with which these matters are being treated.
