Dhaka: In a significant turn of events during the trial for the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, the accused, Sohel Rana, has named a previously unmentioned individual as being involved in the crime. While standing before the court for the framing of charges, Rana claimed that a man identified only as 'Dollar' was also an accomplice. This assertion has sent ripples through the investigation, prompting authorities to consider this new lead.

The case, which has gripped public attention due to its horrific nature, has been proceeding with the prosecution presenting its evidence against the initial accused. However, Sohel Rana's sudden revelation during the court proceedings has necessitated a re-evaluation of the investigative angles. The court has reportedly taken note of Rana's statement and may seek further corroboration or initiate inquiries into the identity and whereabouts of this 'Dollar'.

Legal experts suggest that such disclosures, particularly at the charge framing stage, can either be an attempt by the accused to deflect blame or a genuine effort to reveal a wider conspiracy. The prosecution is expected to rigorously examine this new claim, potentially leading to further arrests or a more complex legal battle. The family of Ramisa, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, will be closely watching how this new development impacts the trial. The court's next steps will be crucial in determining whether 'Dollar' will become a central figure in the Ramisa case.
