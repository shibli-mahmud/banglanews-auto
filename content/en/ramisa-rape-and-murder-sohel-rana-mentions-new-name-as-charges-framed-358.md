The Faridpur District and Sessions Judge's Court has officially framed charges against the accused in the horrific rape and murder of Ramisa, a case that has garnered widespread attention and public outcry. The framing of charges is a pivotal step, setting the stage for the trial to commence.  However, the proceedings took an unexpected turn when Sohel Rana, the primary suspect, allegedly disclosed the name of a previously unknown individual allegedly involved in the crime. This new information has prompted authorities to re-examine the evidence and potentially broaden the scope of the investigation.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana's statement, made during a closed-door interrogation, suggests a more intricate network of individuals may have been complicit in the planning and execution of the heinous act. While details of this new name and the alleged role of this individual remain under wraps due to the sensitive nature of the investigation, it is understood that law enforcement agencies are actively pursuing this lead. The prosecution expressed their commitment to thoroughly exploring all avenues to ensure justice for Ramisa and her family. The court has scheduled the next hearing for further proceedings, as the case enters a new and potentially more complex phase.
