Berlin's annual Christopher Street Day (CSD) Pride parade, a celebration of LGBTQ+ rights, was disrupted on Saturday afternoon when a vehicle allegedly rammed into a gathering of attendees in the city's central Tiergarten area. Emergency services were immediately dispatched to the scene, with initial reports indicating several individuals sustained injuries. The exact number and severity of the injuries are still being assessed by authorities.

Witnesses described a scene of shock and confusion as the vehicle, described by some as a van, veered into the crowd. Police quickly cordoned off the area and initiated a swift manhunt for the driver. The motive behind the incident remains unclear, and investigators are working to determine if it was an intentional act or a tragic accident. 

In a statement released shortly after the incident, the Berlin Police Department confirmed that an intensive operation was underway to locate and apprehend the individual(s) responsible. They urged the public to avoid the area and report any relevant information. Later in the evening, police announced that a suspect had been taken into custody. Further details regarding the suspect's identity and any potential charges are expected to be released following ongoing investigations. The Pride celebrations were temporarily paused in the affected vicinity, but organizers have stated that other parts of the event will continue with heightened security measures.
