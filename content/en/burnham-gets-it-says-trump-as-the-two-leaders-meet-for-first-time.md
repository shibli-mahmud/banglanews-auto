Donald Trump has offered a surprisingly conciliatory assessment of Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham, declaring that the Labour politician 'gets it' during their first personal encounter in New York. The meeting, held privately, reportedly focused on potential business and investment opportunities, with Trump expressing optimism about the possibility of future collaboration.

'We had a very good meeting. Andy Burnham gets it,' Trump told reporters after the discussions. 'He understands how to get things done, and I think we can do some very good business together.' The comments come as a surprise, given the differing political ideologies of the two leaders and Trump's often combative public persona.

However, the former President was not entirely complimentary. He took the opportunity to reiterate his strong disapproval of the agreement concerning the Chagos Islands, which has been a point of contention in international relations. 'That Chagos deal is terrible. It's a terrible deal,' Trump stated emphatically. 'A lot of people are unhappy about it, and I think it's something that needs to be looked at again.'

The Chagos Archipelago, a British Overseas Territory in the Indian Ocean, has been the subject of a long-standing dispute between Mauritius and the United Kingdom, with the International Court of Justice and the International Criminal Court ruling in favour of Mauritius's sovereignty claim.

Burnham's office has yet to release a detailed statement on the meeting, though it is understood that the discussion also touched upon areas of shared interest, including economic development and international trade. The encounter marks a notable moment, bridging political divides and focusing on pragmatic engagement.
