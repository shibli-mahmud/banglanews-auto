In a significant assertion of sovereign prerogative, Prime Minister Keir Starmer has declared his preparedness to publicly critique or confront U.S. President Donald Trump should the circumstances demand it for the benefit of Britain. Speaking in an exclusive interview with the BBC, Starmer articulated a clear foreign policy doctrine: the unwavering defense of national interests above all else.  He characterized his initial conversation with the new U.S. president as 'good,' suggesting a foundation for dialogue, but immediately underscored that this cordiality would not preclude him from taking a firm stance if necessary.  'You have to defend your own national interest,' Starmer stated unequivocally, emphasizing that the government's primary duty is to its citizens and the prosperity of the United Kingdom. This stance signals a potentially robust approach to international relations, particularly with key allies, where bilateral ties will be assessed through the prism of British advantage.  While acknowledging the importance of alliances, the Prime Minister’s comments indicate a pragmatic and resolute leadership style, prioritizing national sovereignty and economic well-being in a complex global landscape.  The remarks are likely to be interpreted as a strong signal to both domestic and international audiences about Starmer's commitment to a self-reliant and assertive foreign policy.
