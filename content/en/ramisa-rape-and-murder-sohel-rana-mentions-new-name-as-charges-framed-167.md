This revelation comes as a surprise to investigators and the prosecution, who had focused their efforts primarily on Rana and a few other known associates. The mention of 'Dollar' by Rana suggests a potentially wider network of individuals involved in the crime than previously understood. Prosecutors are now under pressure to verify this new information and ascertain the true identity and role of this alleged accomplice. The defense team, on the other hand, may see this as an opportunity to shift some blame or create doubt. The court has taken note of Rana's statement, and it is expected to be a crucial piece of evidence as the trial progresses. The implications of this new name could lead to further investigation, potential new arrests, and a more complex legal battle. The public, which has been closely following the case with intense interest and distress, will be looking for swift and thorough action from the authorities to bring all perpetrators to justice.
