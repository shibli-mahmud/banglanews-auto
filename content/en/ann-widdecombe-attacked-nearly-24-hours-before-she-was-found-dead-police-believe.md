Westminster police are reportedly treating the death of prominent broadcaster and former Conservative MP, Ann Widdecombe, as suspicious following the discovery of her body at her London residence. In a significant development, investigators now believe that Ms. Widdecombe may have been the victim of an assault that occurred approximately 24 hours before her body was found. This revised timeline has broadened the scope of the inquiry, as detectives work to piece together her final movements and identify any potential perpetrators.

Sources close to the investigation have confirmed that a 26-year-old white British man, who was apprehended on suspicion of murder, has been released from police custody without charge and is no longer considered a suspect. The reasons for his initial arrest and subsequent release have not been disclosed, but the development underscores the complex nature of the ongoing investigation. Detectives are reportedly examining all possible avenues, including reviewing CCTV footage, interviewing neighbours and associates, and conducting a thorough forensic examination of Ms. Widdecombe's home.

Ann Widdecombe, a familiar face in British public life for decades, was known for her outspoken views and her career in both politics and broadcasting. Her unexpected death has sent shockwaves through political and media circles. The Metropolitan Police have appealed to the public for any information that might assist their investigation, urging anyone who saw or heard anything unusual in the vicinity of Ms. Widdecombe's residence in the days leading up to her discovery to come forward.
