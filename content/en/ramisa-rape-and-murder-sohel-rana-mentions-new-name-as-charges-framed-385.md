This disclosure was made shortly before the court officially framed charges against the accused individuals. While the specifics of the new name and the alleged involvement of this individual have not been publicly disclosed by law enforcement, sources close to the investigation suggest it could significantly alter the trajectory of the case.

The framing of charges by the court signifies the culmination of the initial investigative phase, moving the case closer to a full trial. The prosecution is expected to present a robust case, armed with forensic evidence and witness testimonies. However, Rana's recent statement adds an element of uncertainty, potentially necessitating further investigation into the newly named individual. Authorities are reportedly working to verify the information provided by Rana and to determine the extent of this new person's alleged involvement in the heinous crime. The legal fraternity and the public alike are keenly awaiting further details as the case progresses through the judicial system, with hopes for swift justice for the victim and her family.
