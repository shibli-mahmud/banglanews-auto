Emergency services in Spain have successfully rescued a British couple who were trapped in a ravine while wildfires raged through the Almeria province. Reports from local Spanish media suggest the unnamed pair were discovered in a semi-conscious state, suffering from burns covering approximately 40% of their bodies. The incident occurred as firefighters battled several blazes across the region, which have been exacerbated by dry conditions and high temperatures.

Details surrounding how the couple became trapped remain unclear, but it is understood they were overcome by the intensity of the fire and smoke. The Almeria province has been particularly affected by the wildfires, prompting evacuations in some areas and a significant deployment of firefighting resources. 

Upon their discovery, the couple received immediate medical attention and were airlifted to a specialized burns unit for urgent treatment. The severity of their injuries has raised concerns for their recovery. Spanish authorities have not yet released official statements regarding the couple's condition or the cause of the fire, but investigations are expected to be launched. The incident serves as a stark reminder of the dangers posed by the current wildfire season in Spain and the devastating impact such events can have on individuals.
