Emergency services are currently on the scene following a train derailment that occurred in the early hours of this morning near Lewes, East Sussex. The British Transport Police (BTP) have confirmed the incident and have cordoned off the area. While the exact cause of the derailment is yet to be determined, BTP and other relevant agencies have launched a full investigation.

Aerial footage captured by a drone offers a dramatic perspective on the extent of the disruption. The images show several carriages of the train off the tracks, with some lying on their sides. The surrounding landscape appears to be largely unaffected, but the scale of the derailment is evident from the air.

Fortunately, initial reports suggest there have been no serious injuries among passengers or crew. However, passengers have been safely evacuated and are being assisted by railway staff and emergency responders. The disruption to rail services in the region is expected to be significant, with cancellations and diversions in place for an extended period.

Authorities are urging the public to avoid the area to allow emergency services and investigators to carry out their work unimpeded. Further updates will be provided as the investigation progresses and more information becomes available regarding the cause of the derailment and the timeline for restoring rail services.
