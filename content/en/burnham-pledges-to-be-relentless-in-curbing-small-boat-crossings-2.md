Prime Minister Rishi Sunak has declared his unwavering determination to bring an end to the perilous journeys undertaken by migrants crossing the English Channel in small boats. Speaking to reporters, he described his government's strategy as 'relentless,' indicating a significant ramp-up in enforcement efforts to disrupt these dangerous crossings.  He stated that the UK Border Force and law enforcement agencies are working tirelessly to prevent illegal entry and dismantle criminal trafficking networks responsible for facilitating these journeys.

However, Mr. Sunak also conceded that a purely enforcement-led strategy is insufficient. He highlighted the crucial need to address the underlying issues that drive individuals to seek dangerous routes to the UK. To this end, the Prime Minister outlined plans to expand the number of safe and legal routes for people to come to the United Kingdom. This dual approach, combining robust border security with the provision of legitimate pathways, is intended to deter illegal crossings and offer a more humane and controlled immigration system.  The government aims to work with international partners to tackle the root causes of migration and to ensure that asylum claims are processed more efficiently, thereby reducing the incentive for individuals to take dangerous risks. The focus, he stressed, is on a comprehensive solution that is both effective in protecting borders and compassionate in its treatment of asylum seekers.
