Two sisters have tragically lost their lives after getting into difficulty in the sea off Brighton beach. Emergency services were called to the scene on Saturday afternoon following reports of people in distress in the water. Despite the efforts of lifeguards and other rescuers, the two sisters could not be saved. Their identities have not yet been officially released, but their father, in a statement released through the police, spoke of his profound sorrow. "No words can truly describe the pain of losing three daughters," he said, revealing the devastating loss of another daughter in circumstances not immediately linked to this incident, but compounding his immense pain. The father's statement painted a picture of a family shattered by grief. "They were beautiful, vibrant young women, and their loss has left a gaping hole in our lives. We are utterly heartbroken and will cherish their memories forever." The incident has prompted renewed calls for water safety, with authorities urging the public to exercise extreme caution when entering the sea, especially during unpredictable weather conditions. Investigations into the exact circumstances of the drowning are ongoing. The community of Brighton has rallied around the grieving father, offering support and condolences during this incredibly difficult time.
