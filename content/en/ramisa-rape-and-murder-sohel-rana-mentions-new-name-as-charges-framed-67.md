However, his assertion has prompted the prosecution and investigating agencies to re-examine existing evidence and potentially widen the scope of their inquiry. The legal team representing Ramisa's family has expressed hope that this new lead could shed further light on the motive and execution of the crime, potentially bringing all those responsible to justice.

Ramisa, a young woman whose life was tragically cut short, became a symbol of the pervasive violence against women. Her case has garnered significant public attention and calls for swift and thorough justice. The inclusion of 'Dollar' in Rana's statement raises critical questions about the extent of the network involved and the possibility of a larger criminal enterprise. The court is expected to take Rana's statement into consideration as the trial progresses. Further investigation into the identity and involvement of 'Dollar' will be crucial in unraveling the full truth behind this heinous crime.
