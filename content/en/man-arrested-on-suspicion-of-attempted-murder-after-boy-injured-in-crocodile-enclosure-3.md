Cambridgeshire Police have confirmed the arrest of a man in connection with a serious incident that left a three-year-old boy critically injured. The young child was reportedly injured within the vicinity of a crocodile enclosure, though details surrounding the exact nature of the incident remain under investigation. Police were called to the scene following reports of the child's injuries. Emergency services, including paramedics, swiftly attended and provided immediate medical assistance. The boy was subsequently transported to Addenbrooke's Hospital, where he received urgent treatment for his critical injuries. Authorities have since stated that the child's condition is now stable. Detectives have launched a thorough investigation and have arrested a male suspect on suspicion of attempted murder. Investigators are appealing to the public for any witnesses or individuals who may possess information relevant to the case to come forward. The identity of the arrested man and the relationship between the suspect and the victim have not yet been disclosed. The location of the incident, specifically which establishment or private residence housed the crocodile enclosure, is also not public knowledge at this stage. Cambridgeshire Police are continuing their inquiries to piece together the events leading up to the boy's injuries.
