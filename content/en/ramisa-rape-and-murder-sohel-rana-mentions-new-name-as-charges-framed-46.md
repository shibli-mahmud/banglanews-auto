The Ramisa rape and murder case, which has sent shockwaves across the nation, took a new turn on Monday when the prime accused, Sohel Rana, provided a previously unmentioned name to investigators and the court. According to sources close to the proceedings, Rana stated that a man known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the heinous crime. This testimony was given shortly after charges were officially framed against Rana and his alleged accomplices. The introduction of 'Dollar' into the investigation immediately prompted authorities to broaden their scope, seeking to identify and apprehend this new individual. Details surrounding Dollar's alleged role remain scarce, fueling speculation and concern among Ramisa's family and the public.  The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the legal battle, signifying that sufficient evidence has been gathered to proceed to trial. However, the emergence of a new suspect could potentially complicate the existing legal strategy and necessitate further investigation.  Ramisa's family, who have been relentlessly pursuing justice, expressed a mix of hope and anxiety. While the framing of charges is a step forward, the mention of a new conspirator underscores the depth of the alleged plot. Legal experts suggest that this new information could lead to amended charges or separate proceedings for Dollar, should he be identified and apprehended. The court has indicated that it will take Rana's statement into consideration as the investigation progresses, emphasizing the commitment to uncovering all individuals responsible for the crime.
