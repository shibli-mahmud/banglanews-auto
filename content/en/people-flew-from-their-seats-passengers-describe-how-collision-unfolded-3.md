A severe collision has left numerous passengers traumatized and injured, with emergency services reporting close to 90 casualties, more than 30 of whom are in a serious condition. Eyewitness accounts paint a vivid picture of the sudden and violent impact. Passengers have described the terrifying moment the collision occurred, with many being violently thrown from their seats. One survivor recounted, "It was just a sudden jolt, and then everything went flying. People were screaming, and there was an awful lot of noise." Another passenger stated, "I was thrown forward and hit the seat in front of me. It was pure chaos, and for a moment, I thought that was it." The East of England Ambulance Service was quickly on the scene, with multiple ambulances and medical teams working to assess and treat the injured. The severity of some injuries has necessitated extensive medical intervention. Investigations into the cause of the collision are underway, but the immediate focus remains on providing care for the victims and supporting those affected by the traumatic event. The scale of the incident has overwhelmed local resources, prompting a coordinated response from multiple emergency agencies. The full extent of the damage and the number of people involved are still being assessed as authorities work to understand the sequence of events that led to this distressing incident.
