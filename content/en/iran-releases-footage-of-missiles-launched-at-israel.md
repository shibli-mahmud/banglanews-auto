Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has released video footage purportedly showing the launch of ballistic missiles and drones targeting Israel. The unprecedented aerial assault, which began late Saturday and continued into Sunday, involved hundreds of drones and missiles fired from Iran, Iraq, and Yemen. Iran stated that these attacks were in retaliation for a suspected Israeli airstrike on its consulate building in Damascus, Syria, on April 1, which killed several senior IRGC commanders.  

The IRGC declared that the operation, dubbed 'True Promise,' was "the beginning of a full week of continuous strikes" unless Israel "immediately ceases its stupidity."  The footage released shows what appear to be multiple launches of missiles and drones from Iranian soil, illuminating the night sky.  While Iran asserts the strikes were precise and targeted military objectives, Israel reports that its air defense systems, supported by allies including the US, UK, and Jordan, successfully intercepted the vast majority of the projectiles.  Some reports indicate minor damage at an airbase in southern Israel.  This direct exchange of fire between Iran and Israel marks a significant escalation in their long-standing shadow war, raising global concerns about a wider regional conflict.
