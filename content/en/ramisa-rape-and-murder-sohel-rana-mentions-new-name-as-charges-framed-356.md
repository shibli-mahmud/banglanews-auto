Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana's statement implicating the new suspect has been submitted to the court, and authorities are now working to verify the information and locate the individual for questioning.

The framing of charges by the court signifies that there is sufficient evidence to proceed to trial. This process involves presenting the prosecution's case and allowing the defense to respond, ultimately leading to a verdict. The inclusion of a new suspect, however, could necessitate further investigation and potentially delay the trial if the new information warrants a revised indictment or additional arrests. 

The public has been closely following the Ramisa case, which has garnered significant attention due to its gruesome nature and the demand for justice. Families of victims and human rights organizations have consistently urged for a swift and thorough investigation, and this latest development is expected to intensify that pressure. The authorities have assured the public that they are committed to a transparent and exhaustive inquiry, ensuring that all individuals responsible for Ramisa's death are brought to justice. The coming days are anticipated to bring more clarity as the legal process unfolds and the new allegations are thoroughly examined.
