The distressing incident occurred in the first half of Denmark's opening Euro 2020 fixture against Finland at the Parken Stadium in Copenhagen on Saturday. Approximately 43 minutes into the game, Eriksen, a key player for both Inter Milan and the Danish national team, suddenly fell to the ground and appeared to lose consciousness.  Medical staff were immediately called onto the pitch and rushed to his aid, administering CPR.  The match was temporarily suspended as players from both teams formed a protective barrier around their stricken colleague.  Following extensive treatment on the field, Eriksen was stretchered off and transported to a nearby hospital.  In a statement released shortly after the event, the Danish Football Association (DBU) confirmed that Eriksen was conscious and undergoing further medical evaluation.  The news provided a significant measure of relief to fans and colleagues alike, who had been gripped by anxiety. The Euro 2020 match was eventually resumed later in the evening, with Finland securing a 1-0 victory, but the result was overshadowed by the harrowing events surrounding Eriksen's collapse.  Tributes and messages of support have poured in from across the globe for the 29-year-old midfielder, highlighting his popularity and respected status within the football community.  Further updates on his condition are expected as medical professionals continue their assessment.
