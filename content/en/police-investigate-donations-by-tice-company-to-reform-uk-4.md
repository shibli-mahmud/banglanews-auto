The Metropolitan Police have confirmed an ongoing investigation into donations made by Britain Means Business, a company associated with prominent Reform UK figure Richard Tice, to the political party. The company, reportedly owned by Tice, the party's deputy leader, made two donations of £250,000 each in the period preceding the 2024 general election. These substantial contributions have now become the subject of a formal police inquiry.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that the focus is on ensuring transparency and compliance with electoral funding regulations. Authorities will be scrutinizing the origins of the funds and whether they were fully declared in accordance with established rules governing political donations in the UK. Such investigations are crucial for maintaining public trust in the electoral process and ensuring a level playing field for all political parties.

Richard Tice, a prominent businessman and a key figure in the Reform UK party, has not yet issued a public statement regarding the investigation. However, the party itself has previously emphasized its commitment to transparency in its funding. This development comes at a critical time for Reform UK, which is actively campaigning for the upcoming general election and seeking to increase its representation in Parliament. The scrutiny of its funding sources, particularly those linked to its senior leadership, could have implications for its public perception and electoral campaign. Further details are expected to emerge as the investigation progresses.
