While facing charges in the court, Rana reportedly asserted that a man known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the crime that has sent shockwaves across the nation.

The details of 'Dollar's' alleged role and connection to Ramisa's tragic demise remain unclear at this stage. However, Sohel Rana's statement has prompted the prosecution to consider further investigation into this new lead. The court has framed charges against Sohel Rana and others allegedly involved, moving the trial a step closer to its conclusion. The family of the victim, Ramisa, has expressed their desire for swift justice and full accountability for all perpetrators.

Ramisa, a young woman whose life was brutally cut short, became a symbol of the growing concern over women's safety in the country. Her case has garnered widespread attention, with activists and the public demanding stringent punishment for those responsible. The mention of 'Dollar' by the prime accused could potentially complicate the legal process but also offers the possibility of uncovering the full extent of the conspiracy and bringing all those responsible to justice. The court has scheduled the next hearing to address the new information and ensure a thorough investigation.
