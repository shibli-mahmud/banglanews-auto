James Cleverly, the former Secretary of State for Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Affairs, has announced his resignation from the shadow cabinet to pursue a bid for the London mayoralty. The move, though surprising given the 2028 election date, signals a significant shift in his political trajectory and sets the stage for a potentially high-profile contest. Cleverly, a prominent figure within the Conservative party, has been a vocal advocate for London's economic recovery and has often spoken about the challenges facing the capital. His decision to step down from his current role allows him to dedicate his full attention to campaigning and building support among Londoners.  Sources close to Cleverly suggest that he believes his extensive experience in national politics and his understanding of the complexities of governance make him the ideal candidate to lead London.  He is expected to focus his campaign on issues such as public safety, affordable housing, and transport infrastructure, aiming to present a clear alternative to the incumbent administration.  While the election is still some years away, Cleverly's early declaration of intent could shape the political landscape and encourage other potential candidates to emerge. His departure from the shadow cabinet, however, does leave a vacancy that the party will need to address, potentially leading to further reshuffling within its front benches.  The coming months will likely see more details emerge about his policy proposals and campaign strategy as he begins his journey towards the mayor's office.
