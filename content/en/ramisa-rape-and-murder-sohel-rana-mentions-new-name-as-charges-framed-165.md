The judicial process in the deeply disturbing Ramisa rape and murder case has taken a new turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, identifying another individual who he claims is linked to the heinous crime. The charges have now been formally framed by the court, setting the stage for a trial that has captivated public attention and underscored the urgent need for justice for the victim, Ramisa. 

Sources close to the investigation indicate that Sohel Rana, during his deposition or interrogation, provided the name of a previously unmentioned person, suggesting their active participation in the events leading to Ramisa's death and the subsequent sexual assault. The specifics of this new suspect's alleged role and the evidence supporting Rana's claim are yet to be fully disclosed, but this development is likely to broaden the scope of the investigation and potentially lead to further arrests.

The framing of charges signifies that the court believes there is sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial against the accused. The prosecution is expected to build its case based on the existing evidence, and now, potentially, on the testimony and information provided by Rana himself concerning the new suspect.

Ramisa's family and human rights organizations have been relentlessly pursuing justice since the crime came to light. They have expressed a mix of hope and caution regarding this latest development. While they welcome any progress that leads to the full exposure of all those responsible, they emphasize the need for thorough investigation and due process to ensure that justice is served without any room for doubt. The addition of a new name to the list of suspects will undoubtedly add complexity to the already sensitive case, and the court will need to meticulously examine all evidence presented.
