Sources close to the investigation report that Rana, during his interrogation, pointed to a man known as 'Dollar' as a co-conspirator. This unexpected disclosure has prompted authorities to widen their net, with efforts now underway to locate and apprehend this newly identified suspect.  The framing of charges, a crucial stage in the legal proceedings, is now poised to incorporate this new information, potentially leading to a revised indictment. The victim's family, who have been steadfast in their demand for justice, have expressed cautious optimism, hoping this development will bring all perpetrators to account. Legal analysts suggest that the introduction of a new suspect at this juncture could necessitate further investigative steps, possibly delaying the trial but ultimately aiming for a more comprehensive understanding of the events leading to Ramisa's tragic death. The police have refrained from providing specific details regarding 'Dollar's' identity or his alleged role, citing the ongoing nature of the investigation. However, the commitment to a thorough inquiry remains evident, as the authorities strive to ensure that no stone is left unturned in their pursuit of justice for Ramisa.
