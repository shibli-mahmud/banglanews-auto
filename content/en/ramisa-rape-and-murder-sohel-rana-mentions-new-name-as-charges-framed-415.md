The court was in the process of formally charging the individuals believed to be involved in the heinous crime that has sent shockwaves across the nation.  However, Rana’s statement has added a layer of complexity, suggesting the involvement of another party that was not previously identified by law enforcement agencies or linked to the investigation.

Details regarding the new name and the context in which it was revealed are currently emerging and being thoroughly examined by the prosecution and the court.  Sources close to the investigation indicate that the revelation came as a surprise to many, including the investigating officers. The court has reportedly taken note of Rana's statement and has indicated that it will be taken into consideration as the trial progresses.  This new information could necessitate further interrogations, the gathering of new evidence, and potentially a review of the existing charge sheet.  The Ramisa case, already a subject of intense public and media scrutiny due to its brutality and the victim's young age, now faces an additional hurdle.  The public awaits further updates as the legal proceedings unfold, with hopes that this development will ultimately contribute to a more complete understanding of the events and ensure that all responsible parties are brought to justice.
