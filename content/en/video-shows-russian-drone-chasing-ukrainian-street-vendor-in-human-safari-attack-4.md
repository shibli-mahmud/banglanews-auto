Graphic footage circulating online appears to show a small, remotely-controlled Russian drone stalking and pursuing a Ukrainian street vendor through a populated area. The vendor, visibly terrified, is seen running and attempting to take cover as the drone hovers menacingly close, its buzzing a constant threat. The incident, allegedly occurring amidst ongoing conflict, has been condemned by Ukrainian authorities, who have labeled the act a blatant war crime. "This is a deliberate act of terror against a civilian," stated a spokesperson for the Ukrainian Ministry of Defense. "The fact that they are hunting down innocent people like animals for sport, like in a 'human safari,' is utterly reprehensible and underscores the depravity of this regime."  The drone's persistent pursuit suggests an intent to inflict psychological terror, if not physical harm, on the unsuspecting vendor. International legal experts are reviewing the footage to determine if it constitutes a violation of international humanitarian law. The incident raises further concerns about Russia's conduct in the war and its alleged disregard for civilian lives. This "human safari" tactic, if confirmed, represents a disturbing escalation in the psychological warfare employed by Russian forces.
