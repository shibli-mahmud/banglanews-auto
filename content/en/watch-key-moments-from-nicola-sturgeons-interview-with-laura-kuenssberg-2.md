Speaking with Laura Kuenssberg, Nicola Sturgeon acknowledged the gravity of the situation surrounding her husband, Peter Murrell's, guilty plea to embezzling a substantial sum from the Scottish National Party. While Sturgeon reiterated that she has no involvement in the financial matters of the party, she expressed deep disappointment and sadness at the turn of events. She emphasized that the police investigation is ongoing and stated her full cooperation with the authorities.  The interview touched upon the impact of these developments on the SNP's public image and its internal cohesion. Sturgeon offered her insights into the challenges facing the party as it navigates this difficult chapter, suggesting that rebuilding trust will be paramount. She also spoke about her own future, maintaining that she remains a committed member of the SNP and is dedicated to its success, though she has stepped back from front-line politics. The conversation, broadcast in full, provided a detailed account of Sturgeon's thoughts on the political landscape, the constitutional questions facing Scotland, and her personal resilience in the face of considerable public scrutiny. Viewers were given a candid and measured response to the questions posed, painting a picture of a former leader grappling with the fallout of a scandal that has significantly impacted her party and her personal life.
