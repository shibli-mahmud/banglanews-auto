This assertion was reportedly made by Rana during recent interrogations, as the court officially framed charges against him. The development has injected a fresh dimension into the high-profile case, which has gripped the nation and sparked widespread outrage. 

Legal sources indicate that the prosecution is meticulously preparing its case, aiming to present a comprehensive narrative of the events leading to Ramisa’s death. The framing of charges signifies the formal accusation of the crime against Rana, moving the case from the investigation phase to the trial stage. The prosecution will now focus on establishing Rana's guilt beyond a reasonable doubt, and with the new name emerging, they will also need to investigate and potentially charge 'Dollar' if sufficient evidence is found.

Ramisa's family, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, expressed a mix of relief and renewed anxiety with this development. While the prospect of uncovering all those responsible offers a glimmer of hope, the mention of a new suspect also means a potentially longer legal battle. The court is scheduled to hear arguments regarding the framed charges next week, a hearing that will be closely watched by the public and the media alike. The inclusion of 'Dollar' in Rana's statement adds complexity, and the investigation team is expected to prioritize locating and questioning this individual to corroborate or refute Rana's claims. The case continues to be a stark reminder of the persistent issues surrounding violence against women and the long, often arduous, path to justice.
