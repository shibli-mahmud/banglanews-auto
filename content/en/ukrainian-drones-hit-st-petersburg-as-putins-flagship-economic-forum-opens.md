The Ukrainian military has reportedly conducted a drone attack targeting oil storage facilities in the Leningrad region, a strategic industrial hub on the outskirts of St. Petersburg. The strikes occurred in the early hours of Wednesday, raising security concerns as the city prepares to host the prestigious St. Petersburg International Economic Forum (SPIEF), a key event for Russia's economic diplomacy.  Sources indicate that at least one drone was intercepted, but others may have reached their targets, igniting fires at the oil depots. While official statements from Russian authorities are pending, the incident highlights the escalating reach of Ukraine's drone capabilities, extending deep into Russian territory.  The timing of the attack is particularly significant, as President Vladimir Putin is slated to deliver a keynote address at SPIEF, where he typically outlines Russia's economic strategies and seeks foreign investment.  The strikes could potentially disrupt logistical operations and cast a shadow over the forum, intended to showcase Russia's economic resilience amidst international sanctions. This marks one of the most significant drone incursions into the immediate vicinity of St. Petersburg, a city of immense historical and economic importance to Russia, and a stronghold for President Putin's administration.
