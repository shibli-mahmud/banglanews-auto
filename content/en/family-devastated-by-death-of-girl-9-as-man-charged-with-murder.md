The heartbroken family of Minnie Merriman, a vibrant nine-year-old girl, has been left 'devastated' following her tragic death in Arbroath on Monday. Police were called to a property in the Angus town, where Minnie was found with serious injuries. Despite the best efforts of emergency services, she was pronounced dead at the scene. A 35-year-old man, who has not yet been named by authorities, appeared before Dundee Sheriff Court on Wednesday, formally charged with Minnie's murder. The news has sent shockwaves through the tight-knit community of Arbroath, with neighbours and friends struggling to comprehend the loss of such a young life.

Minnie is being remembered by those who knew her as a bright and happy child. Tributes have begun to appear, with floral bouquets and heartfelt messages left at the scene and online. "She was a beautiful little girl, full of life. This is just unimaginable," said one local resident, who wished to remain anonymous. The investigation is ongoing, and police are appealing for anyone with information to come forward. The family has requested privacy during this incredibly difficult time as they begin to process their immense grief and seek justice for their beloved Minnie.
