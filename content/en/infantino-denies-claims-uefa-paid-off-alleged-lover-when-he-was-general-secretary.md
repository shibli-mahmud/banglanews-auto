FIFA President Gianni Infantino has strongly refuted claims that Uefa made a substantial severance payment to an alleged former lover while he served as the organization's General Secretary. The investigation by The Daily Telegraph suggests that a confidential agreement was reached, resulting in a significant payout to the woman in question. The report further alleges that this payment was intended to cover up an affair and prevent potential reputational damage to Infantino.

In response to the allegations, a spokesperson for Infantino dismissed them as "baseless and defamatory." The statement emphasized that Infantino has always conducted himself with the utmost integrity and professionalism. Uefa itself has also issued a statement, confirming that it has made "numerous confidential severance payments" in the past, as is common practice in many organizations. However, the governing body for European football declined to comment on specific individuals or cases, citing confidentiality agreements.

The Daily Telegraph's report points to internal Uefa documents and sources who claim the payment was authorized during Infantino's leadership. The woman involved was reportedly an employee at Uefa during the period in question. This controversy comes at a sensitive time for Infantino, who is currently leading FIFA and overseeing preparations for major global tournaments. Critics have seized on the allegations to question Infantino's past conduct and transparency during his tenure at Uefa, which spanned from 2000 to 2016.
