Without hesitation, Shally Rahman and her daughter Samiha, who were reportedly nearby, entered the sea to try and reach the child. Tragically, both mother and daughter were themselves overcome by the strong currents and rough conditions. 

Emergency services, including the RNLI lifeguards and Sussex Police, were alerted and swiftly arrived on the scene. A large-scale rescue operation was launched, with lifeguards managing to pull the young boy to safety. However, Shally and Samiha Rahman were also found in the water and were brought ashore. Paramedics immediately began resuscitation attempts, but their efforts were in vain. Both were pronounced dead at the scene.

Speaking at a press conference, Superintendent Chris Walker of Sussex Police paid tribute to the bravery of the two women. "This is an absolutely heartbreaking tragedy," he stated. "Our thoughts are with their family and friends at this incredibly difficult time. It is clear that Shally and Samiha acted with incredible courage, putting themselves at risk to save a child's life." The seven-year-old boy, who has not been named, was taken to hospital for treatment and is reported to be in a stable condition. The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community, with many expressing their grief and admiration for the mother and daughter's selfless actions. Investigations into the precise circumstances of the incident are ongoing.
