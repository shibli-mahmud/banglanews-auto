According to recent reports, former President Donald Trump was secretly moved to a different aircraft under a veil of deception during his departure from a NATO summit in Turkey. The alleged maneuver involved a ruse where members of the White House press corps were led to believe that Trump was still aboard Air Force One, even as he was reportedly being transported to a separate, undisclosed plane via a catering truck.  This elaborate plan, if true, was purportedly implemented due to heightened concerns over Iran's potential threats during that period.

The report suggests that the press pool, who typically travel with the president, was deliberately misinformed about Trump's departure. While they were waiting for Air Force One, the former president was allegedly being spirited away in a catering vehicle to a different aircraft. This tactic would have served to obscure his movements and prevent potential adversaries, particularly Iran, from tracking his exact location and itinerary.  The move highlights the high-stakes nature of international relations and the lengths to which security protocols might go to ensure a president's safety and operational secrecy.  Details of the incident remain unconfirmed by official sources, but the allegations paint a picture of a highly secretive operation conducted under the shadow of geopolitical tension.
