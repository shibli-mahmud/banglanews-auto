Philp expressed surprise at the sudden commencement of the drill, noting that he was not forewarned. While the exact purpose and target of the firing were not immediately disclosed, such drills are typically part of naval training to maintain combat readiness. The presence of a senior UK politician and a BBC crew on board during a live firing exercise highlights a potentially sensitive situation.  Further details regarding the specific exercise, the vessel involved, and the communications between the French and British sides are expected to emerge.
