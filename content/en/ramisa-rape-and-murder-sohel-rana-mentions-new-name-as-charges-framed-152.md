The Dhaka court was abuzz with activity on Monday as proceedings commenced for the much-anticipated rape and murder trial of Ramisa. The prime accused, Sohel Rana, who has been in custody, made a startling revelation during the charge framing session. When questioned by the court, Rana implicated a previously unknown individual, referred to only by the alias 'Dollar', suggesting his direct involvement in the heinous crime.

Rana's statement, presented before the Additional Metropolitan Sessions Judge's Court, has sent ripples through the legal fraternity and raised new questions about the scope of the investigation. While the court has already framed charges against Rana and other named defendants, the mention of 'Dollar' necessitates a further probe into his identity, whereabouts, and alleged role. Legal experts suggest that this revelation, if substantiated, could lead to a supplementary investigation or amendments to the existing charges, depending on the evidence uncovered.

The case, which has garnered significant public attention due to its brutal nature, has already seen several twists and turns. The prosecution team is now tasked with verifying Rana's claims and determining whether 'Dollar' is a real person with concrete links to the crime or a strategic attempt by the accused to deflect blame. The court has adjourned for further proceedings, with investigators expected to follow up on this new lead. The inclusion of 'Dollar' in the narrative underscores the challenges faced by law enforcement in piecing together the full picture of such complex and violent offenses.
