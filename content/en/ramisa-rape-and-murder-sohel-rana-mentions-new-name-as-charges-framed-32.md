This unexpected disclosure by Rana has sent ripples through the investigation, prompting authorities to consider the possibility of a wider network of perpetrators.

Rana's testimony, though yet to be corroborated, suggests that 'Dollar' played a role in the abduction, rape, and subsequent murder of Ramisa. Details surrounding 'Dollar's' identity and his specific actions remain vague, but investigators are now actively working to identify and locate this individual. The prosecution believes that Rana's statement, if substantiated, could significantly strengthen their case and potentially lead to further arrests. Legal analysts suggest that this new information, introduced at the charge framing stage, could necessitate a re-examination of evidence and witness testimonies. The court has acknowledged Rana's statement and has directed law enforcement to expedite efforts to gather more information on 'Dollar.' The case, which has already garnered widespread public attention and outcry, now faces the challenge of unraveling this new lead, aiming to ensure justice for Ramisa and hold all responsible parties accountable.
