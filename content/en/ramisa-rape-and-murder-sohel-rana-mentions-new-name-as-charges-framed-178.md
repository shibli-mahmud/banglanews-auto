The courtroom drama unfolded on Monday as the Dhaka Women's and Children Repression Prevention Tribunal-7 began the process of formally framing charges against the accused in the deeply disturbing case of Ramisa's rape and subsequent murder. In a turn of events that has captured public attention and sent ripples through the investigation, the main accused, Sohel Rana, has pointed fingers at a new individual, claiming his involvement. Rana, when questioned during the proceedings, asserted that a man known by the moniker 'Dollar' was also a participant in the crime.

This revelation, made under oath during the charge framing session, is significant as it introduces a previously unmentioned party into the narrative of the brutal crime. Legal experts suggest that this could necessitate further investigation, potentially leading to the arrest and prosecution of 'Dollar' if the allegations hold water. The prosecution team is reportedly considering how to integrate this new information into their case, whether by seeking a postponement to bring 'Dollar' under scrutiny or by proceeding with the existing charges while noting Rana's statement.

Ramisa's tragic death had sent shockwaves across the nation, igniting widespread protests demanding justice for the victim and stringent punishment for the perpetrators. The case has been closely watched by human rights organizations and the public alike, with a strong emphasis on ensuring a fair and thorough trial. The introduction of 'Dollar' into the equation adds a layer of complexity to an already sensitive and high-profile case. The court's next steps will be crucial in determining the direction of the investigation and the pursuit of justice for Ramisa.
