The judicial process in the high-profile Ramisa rape and murder case took a dramatic turn today as charges were officially framed against the accused. During the proceedings, prime suspect Sohel Rana, who has been in custody since his arrest, made a startling revelation. He reportedly implicated a hitherto unknown individual, referred to only by the moniker 'Dollar', claiming this person was also present and actively involved during the horrific incident. This new piece of information, disclosed by the alleged perpetrator himself, is expected to have a significant impact on the direction of the investigation and the subsequent trial. 

Sources close to the investigation suggest that 'Dollar' might be a key figure that law enforcement has not yet identified or apprehended. The mention of this new name by Sohel Rana could either be an attempt to divert suspicion or a genuine disclosure of accomplices. Legal analysts are keenly observing how the prosecution and defense will navigate this new development. The court's framing of charges signifies that sufficient evidence has been presented to proceed to trial, but the introduction of 'Dollar' may necessitate further inquiries and potentially a broader scope for the legal proceedings. The victim's family, who have been relentlessly seeking justice, have expressed their hope that this revelation will lead to the apprehension of all individuals responsible and a swift conclusion to the case. The police have stated that they will thoroughly investigate this new lead and take appropriate action.
