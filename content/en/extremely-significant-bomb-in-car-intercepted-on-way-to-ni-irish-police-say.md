Gardaí, the Irish national police force, have confirmed a major security operation that resulted in the seizure of a substantial bomb found within a car. The vehicle was intercepted by authorities on its way to Northern Ireland, a development that has raised significant concerns about potential threats to stability in the region.  The exact nature and intended target of the device have not yet been fully disclosed, but the Garda Commissioner stated that it was 'extremely significant,' underscoring the gravity of the situation.

A woman, believed to be in her 20s, has been arrested and charged by gardaí in connection with the discovery. She is scheduled to appear before a court on Friday evening, where further details are expected to emerge regarding the charges and the ongoing investigation. This incident highlights the persistent security challenges faced by authorities in both the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland, particularly concerning dissident republican activity.  The interception is a testament to the vigilance of law enforcement agencies and their commitment to disrupting potential acts of violence. The investigation is ongoing, with authorities working to determine the full scope of any planned operation and to identify any accomplices. The focus remains on ensuring public safety and preventing any escalation of threats.
