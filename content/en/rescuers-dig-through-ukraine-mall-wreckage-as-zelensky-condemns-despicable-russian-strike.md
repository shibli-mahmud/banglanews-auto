Search and rescue teams are meticulously sifting through the debris of a shattered shopping mall in central Ukraine, desperately seeking survivors and recovering bodies after a Russian missile strike on Friday. The attack, which struck the Amstor mall in Kremenchuk during peak hours, has been widely condemned as a war crime. Official figures confirm at least 16 people were killed and more than 130 injured, with many of the casualties being civilians. Disturbingly, four individuals are still missing, fueling fears that the death toll may rise as the grim work continues.  President Volodymyr Zelensky, visibly angered, described the attack as 'one of the most brazen terrorist acts' and a clear assault on ordinary citizens. He emphasized that the mall was not a military target, highlighting the deliberate targeting of civilian infrastructure. International leaders have joined Ukraine in denouncing the strike, with many calling for increased pressure on Russia and further investigations into alleged war crimes. The Kremlin has not yet officially commented on the Kremenchuk attack, but has previously denied targeting civilian areas.  The incident underscores the brutal reality of the ongoing conflict, with civilian casualties mounting and the devastating impact on everyday life in Ukraine. The scale of the destruction at the Amstor mall is a stark visual reminder of the human cost of the war, as rescuers continue their painstaking efforts amidst the rubble, holding onto faint hopes of finding more survivors.
