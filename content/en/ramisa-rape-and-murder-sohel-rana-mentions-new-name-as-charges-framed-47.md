Ramisa, a young woman whose brutal murder sent shockwaves across the nation, was found deceased under tragic circumstances earlier this year. The subsequent investigation has been intense, leading to the arrest of several individuals, with Sohel Rana being a central figure. The framing of charges signifies the prosecution's readiness to proceed to trial, but this new testimony from Rana could necessitate further inquiry and potentially lead to additional arrests. 

Law enforcement officials have stated that they are prioritizing the identification and apprehension of 'Dollar'. The motive behind this new accusation and the extent of 'Dollar's' alleged role are currently under intense scrutiny. The case, already fraught with public attention and emotional weight, now faces the challenge of unraveling this fresh lead. The prosecution team is expected to assess the credibility of Rana's statement and gather corroborating evidence before deciding on any further legal actions. The coming days are anticipated to bring more clarity as investigators delve deeper into the identity and whereabouts of 'Dollar', aiming to bring all perpetrators of this crime to justice.
