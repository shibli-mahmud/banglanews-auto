Donald Trump, during his presidency, was reportedly the subject of a covert operation to move him from Air Force One, with the move allegedly involving a catering truck and a secret plane swap. This was reportedly orchestrated due to heightened concerns over a potential threat from Iran. According to reports, the operation was designed to conceal Trump's movements from journalists who were led to believe he was still on Air Force One as it departed from a NATO summit in Turkey.

The alleged plan involved Trump disembarking from Air Force One discreetly, possibly via a catering truck, and then boarding a different, unscheduled aircraft. This 'secret plane swap' was intended to throw off any potential adversaries monitoring his travel plans. The fear of an Iranian threat was said to be a primary driver behind the extreme security measures.

Reporters traveling with the President were reportedly misled into believing Trump was still aboard the presidential aircraft, which was ostensibly flying back from the summit. This deception was part of a broader effort to ensure Trump's safety and prevent any intelligence from being gathered on his movements. The incident highlights the significant security considerations and potential lengths to which the Trump administration went to protect the President, particularly in the context of escalating geopolitical tensions with Iran. While specific details remain unconfirmed by official sources, the reports paint a picture of an administration deeply concerned about potential retaliatory actions.
