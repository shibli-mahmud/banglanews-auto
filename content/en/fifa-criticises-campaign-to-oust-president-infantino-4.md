Zurich – FIFA has publicly denounced what it terms a deliberate and sustained campaign aimed at discrediting its president, Gianni Infantino, and disrupting the organization. In a strongly worded statement released earlier today, the Zurich-based governing body declared, 'We are witnessing a concerted and ongoing effort to undermine FIFA and its president, Gianni Infantino.' The statement further alleged that these actions are not isolated incidents but part of a coordinated strategy to erode trust and impede the progress FIFA has been making in recent years.

While FIFA did not explicitly name individuals or groups involved in this alleged campaign, the federation emphasized its commitment to transparency and good governance. It suggested that the motivations behind these attacks are likely rooted in resistance to the reforms and initiatives undertaken under Infantino's leadership, which have aimed to enhance financial stability, improve football governance globally, and increase investment in the sport at all levels. FIFA stressed that it would not be deterred by such attempts to derail its mission and remains focused on developing football and ensuring its integrity. The organization hinted at potential further actions to address what it perceives as malicious and unfounded criticisms, reinforcing its stance on protecting its reputation and the sport's future.
