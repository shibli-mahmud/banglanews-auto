This unexpected disclosure during the indictment proceedings has sent ripples through the investigation, with authorities now tasked with verifying the new information and potentially broadening their search for those responsible.

Sources close to the court proceedings indicate that Rana, when presented with the charges, provided details that led to the mention of a new name. The exact nature of this individual's alleged involvement remains unclear, and investigators are expected to delve into this new lead with urgency. Legal experts suggest that such revelations at this stage can significantly impact the trajectory of the case, requiring further inquiries and possibly new arrests.

The court has officially framed charges against Sohel Rana and any other identified accused, moving the case closer to a full trial. However, the defense team has indicated that they are meticulously reviewing the evidence and preparing a robust defense strategy. The prosecution, on the other hand, is confident in the existing evidence and is keen to present its case comprehensively.

For the family of the victim, Ramisa, this development brings a renewed sense of anticipation mixed with the enduring pain of their loss. They have consistently called for justice and transparency throughout the investigation and are hoping that this new information will bring them closer to the truth and hold all perpetrators accountable for their heinous actions. The coming weeks are expected to be crucial as investigators work to corroborate Rana's statement and the court continues to meticulously process the case.
