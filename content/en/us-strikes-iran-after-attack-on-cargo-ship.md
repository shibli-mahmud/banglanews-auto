In a decisive move following escalating tensions in the Persian Gulf, the United States Central Command (CENTCOM) confirmed it has launched retaliatory strikes against Iran. These strikes targeted key military infrastructure, including missile and drone storage facilities, as well as coastal radar positions, according to a statement released by CENTCOM.

The operation was a direct response to a recent attack on a commercial cargo ship, which U.S. officials have attributed to Iranian forces. The specifics of the cargo ship incident were not immediately detailed in the CENTCOM announcement, but the strike is intended to degrade Iran's ability to launch future attacks and to deter further aggression.

CENTCOM emphasized that the strikes were conducted to protect U.S. forces and interests in the region, and to uphold international maritime security. The command stated that it "will not hesitate to hold Iran accountable for its actions." The development signals a significant escalation in the ongoing friction between the two nations, raising concerns about the stability of the wider Middle East. Further details regarding the extent of the damage and any potential Iranian response are awaited. International observers are closely monitoring the situation for any signs of further escalation or de-escalation efforts.
