Dhaka, Bangladesh – The brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a case that has deeply shocked the nation, has taken a new turn with the primary accused, Sohel Rana, coming forward with an allegation that implicates a previously unmentioned individual. Sources close to the investigation indicate that during the latest legal proceedings, where charges are formally being framed against the perpetrators, Sohel Rana provided the name of a new suspect. This development is significant as it suggests a potential broadening of the conspiracy or the involvement of more parties than initially believed.

Law enforcement officials are reportedly working to verify the credibility of Rana's statement and to ascertain the new suspect's role in the crime. The framing of charges is a critical stage in the judicial process, marking the formal accusation of the accused before a trial can commence. The inclusion of a new name at this juncture could necessitate further investigation and potentially lead to the apprehension and prosecution of additional individuals.

The public and civil society organizations have been closely following the Ramisa case, demanding swift justice and accountability. The revelation of a new suspect, if substantiated, could reignite public interest and put further pressure on the authorities to ensure all those responsible are brought to book. The legal team for Ramisa's family has expressed hope that this new information will aid in uncovering the complete truth and securing justice for the victim. The coming days are expected to be crucial as investigators delve deeper into the implications of Sohel Rana's statement.
