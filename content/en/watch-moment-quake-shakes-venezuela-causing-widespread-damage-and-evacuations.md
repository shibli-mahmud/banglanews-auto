A significant seismic event has rattled Venezuela, with reports of widespread damage to infrastructure and residential buildings emerging across the nation. The earthquake, the magnitude of which is still being assessed by geological agencies, triggered widespread panic, leading to immediate evacuations in affected areas. 

Footage shared by BBC News, filmed by their own reporters and local contributors, vividly illustrates the terrifying moments after the tremors subsided. The videos show scenes of disarray, with cracked walls, fallen debris, and visibly shaken residents pouring out of their homes and workplaces. Emergency services are reportedly overwhelmed as they respond to numerous calls for assistance and begin damage assessments. 

The full extent of casualties and the complete scope of the destruction are still being determined, but initial reports indicate a substantial impact. Residents in several cities described the ground shaking violently for an extended period, causing fear and uncertainty. The evacuations are a precautionary measure to ensure the safety of the population, given the potential for aftershocks and the instability of damaged structures. Authorities are urging citizens to remain calm and follow guidance from emergency personnel. The international community is monitoring the situation closely, with offers of aid expected to follow as the full scale of the disaster becomes clearer.
