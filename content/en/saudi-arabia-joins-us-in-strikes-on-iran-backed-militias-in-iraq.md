The United States Central Command (USCENTCOM) announced today that Saudi Arabian military aircraft have joined US forces in conducting airstrikes against facilities associated with Iran-backed militias operating in Iraq. According to a USCENTCOM statement, these strikes are a direct response to recent and ongoing attacks launched by these proxy groups against US military installations and significant Saudi energy infrastructure.  The statement further detailed that the joint operation aims to degrade the capabilities of these militias and deter future aggression.  This coordinated action underscores a strategic alignment between Saudi Arabia and the United States in addressing threats posed by Iranian-backed militant organizations in the region.  The involved militias have been identified as having close ties to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC).  While specific details regarding the targets and the extent of the damage were not immediately released, USCENTCOM emphasized that the operation was conducted with precision to minimize civilian casualties.  The escalation of hostilities has raised concerns about regional stability, with analysts suggesting this joint effort reflects a strengthened resolve to counter perceived Iranian influence and protect vital economic and military interests in the Middle East.  Further details are expected to be released as the operation continues.
