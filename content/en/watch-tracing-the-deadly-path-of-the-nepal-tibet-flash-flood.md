In the aftermath of the devastating flash flood that swept across the Nepal-Tibet border, BBC Verify has released a compelling investigation that traces the disaster's deadly trajectory. Using a painstaking process of verifying user-generated video content, analysts have pieced together the origins and destructive force of the deluge. The footage reveals the initial collapse of a glacial lake in Tibet, a catastrophic event that unleashed a torrent of water and debris.

The investigation highlights how this initial surge rapidly gained momentum as it flowed downstream, carving a devastating path through valleys and impacting communities on both sides of the border. Verified videos show the sheer power of the water, tearing through bridges, inundating villages, and displacing thousands. BBC Verify's team meticulously cross-referenced timestamps, locations, and distinct geographical features within the videos to establish a credible timeline and geographical spread of the floodwaters. This rigorous approach helps to paint a clearer, albeit grim, picture of the disaster's impact, offering critical insights for understanding and preparing for future such events. The report underscores the vulnerability of communities situated in the path of these increasingly frequent and severe glacial lake outburst floods (GLOFs), a growing concern in the Himalayan region due to climate change.
