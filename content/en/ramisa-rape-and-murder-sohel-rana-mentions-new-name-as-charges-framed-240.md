Dhaka: The judicial process in the deeply unsettling Ramisa rape and murder case has taken a new turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, reportedly naming a previously unmentioned individual as a participant in the heinous crime. This development occurred as the court formally framed charges against the accused, a crucial step that solidifies the legal basis for the trial. Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana, during questioning related to the charge framing, provided the name of a man, whom he claims was involved in the incident.  The details surrounding this new suspect, including their alleged motive and degree of involvement, remain under wraps pending further official statements.  Legal experts suggest that such an admission could either be a genuine attempt to reveal further perpetrators or a tactic to influence the proceedings.  The prosecution is expected to thoroughly investigate this new lead. The case, which has already sent shockwaves across the nation due to its brutal nature and the victim's young age, now faces an expanded scope with this latest disclosure.  The court has set further dates for the continuation of the trial, and the defense is preparing its strategy in light of these evolving circumstances.  Public anticipation remains high as the nation awaits justice for Ramisa.
