However, after thorough questioning and further evidence review, police have confirmed that he has been released and is no longer part of the ongoing inquiry. Officers are appealing to the public for any information, no matter how seemingly insignificant, that could assist them in piecing together Ms. Widdecombe's final hours. The news has sent shockwaves through political and media circles, with many expressing their disbelief and offering condolences to her family and friends.
