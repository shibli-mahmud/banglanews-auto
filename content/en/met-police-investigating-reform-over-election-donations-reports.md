The Metropolitan Police has launched an investigation into potential irregularities surrounding election donations received by the political party Reform UK. News reports, notably from The Times, suggest that the inquiry is specifically examining funds provided by the mother of George Cottrell. Cottrell, a figure previously convicted of fraud, has had his financial dealings scrutinized in the past, and his mother's donations to Reform UK have now attracted the attention of law enforcement.

Details regarding the exact nature of the donations and the period under investigation remain scarce. However, the involvement of the Met Police signals a serious examination of the source and legitimacy of campaign funding. Electoral finance regulations are stringent, designed to ensure transparency and prevent undue influence in the political process. Any potential breach of these rules can have significant implications for a political party.

Reform UK, formerly known as the Brexit Party, has been a prominent voice in British politics, particularly concerning its stance on Brexit and national sovereignty. The party has actively sought donations to fund its campaigns and assert its influence. This investigation, however, could cast a shadow over its financial operations. The police are expected to thoroughly review the documentation and ascertain whether the donations comply with all relevant electoral laws. The outcome of this investigation could have repercussions for the party's reputation and its future fundraising efforts.
