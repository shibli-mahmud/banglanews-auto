Tehran issued a strong statement denouncing the US military actions, asserting that they constitute a blatant disregard for established international norms and any existing cessation of hostilities. The timing of these strikes is particularly sensitive, as Iranian and Qatari negotiators were reportedly engaged in crucial discussions in Doha aimed at brokering peace in the region. Sources close to the talks suggest that the unilateral military action by the US has severely undermined the progress and trust being built during these diplomatic endeavors.

Iranian officials have expressed deep concern that such aggressive maneuvers could jeopardize the fragile diplomatic process and reignite wider conflict. The specific targets and the extent of damage or casualties from the US strikes remain unclear, adding to the opaqueness and potential for miscalculation. International observers are closely monitoring the situation, with many urging restraint from all parties involved. The condemnation from Iran underscores the significant geopolitical ramifications of the strikes and highlights the precarious balance of power and diplomacy in the region. The international community is now calling for transparency and de-escalation to prevent further deterioration of the situation.
