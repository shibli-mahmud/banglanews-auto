The Metropolitan Police has officially launched a criminal investigation into donations received by the Reform UK political party. This significant development comes after a report by Channel 4 News brought to light concerns surrounding the source and declaration of certain contributions.  A spokesperson for the Met confirmed that the investigation is now underway and will be folded into an existing inquiry that has been examining other financial activities and donations related to the party.

The focus of the investigation is expected to centre on whether any donations were made in violation of electoral law, which mandates transparency regarding the origin and value of political funding. Allegations suggest that some donations may have been undeclared or may have originated from sources that are legally prohibited from contributing to political parties in the UK.  Reform UK has stated its cooperation with any investigation but has not directly addressed the specifics of the allegations at this stage.

This latest inquiry casts a further shadow over the party's financial practices and follows a period of increased scrutiny for political donations across the UK. Electoral Commission guidance requires parties to ensure all donations are legitimate and properly reported. The integration of this new investigation with the ongoing one suggests a potentially broader examination of the party's financial dealings.  The outcome of this criminal investigation could have significant implications for Reform UK's standing and operations in the lead-up to future elections.
