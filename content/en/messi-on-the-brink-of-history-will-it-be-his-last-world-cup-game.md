As the dust settles on a thrilling World Cup tournament, all eyes are on Lionel Messi. The Argentine captain is on the cusp of what could be his crowning achievement, leading his nation to World Cup glory. Sunday's final against France will mark Messi's 34th appearance in the tournament, an extraordinary feat that places him among the all-time greats in terms of longevity and consistent presence at the highest level.  However, with Messi now 35 years old, the conversation inevitably turns to his future in international football.  He has previously hinted that this World Cup could be his last. The emotional resonance of this potential swansong is palpable.  Fans worldwide are holding their breath, not just to witness history being made in terms of a potential World Cup win for Argentina, but also to savour every moment of what might be Messi's final World Cup game.  The culmination of a storied career, which has seen him achieve virtually every accolade in the sport, could be sealed on Sunday.  Whether he lifts the coveted trophy or not, his legacy is already cemented, but a World Cup title would undoubtedly be the ultimate fairy tale ending. The atmosphere surrounding this match is therefore charged with a unique blend of anticipation, nostalgia, and the raw emotion of potentially witnessing the end of an era.
