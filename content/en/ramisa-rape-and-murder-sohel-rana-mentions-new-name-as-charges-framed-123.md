Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana implicated a man known as 'Dollar,' suggesting his alleged complicity in the heinous crime. This development has prompted renewed focus from law enforcement agencies, who are now reportedly working to identify and apprehend this individual.

The formal framing of charges marks a crucial stage in the legal process, bringing the case closer to a trial. The prosecution is expected to present substantial evidence against the accused, including Sohel Rana, with the newly revealed information about 'Dollar' potentially becoming a key aspect of the ongoing investigation and future testimonies. The legal team representing the victim's family has expressed hope that this new lead will expedite the pursuit of justice. Public reaction remains one of deep concern and a strong desire for swift and decisive action to be taken against all those responsible for the horrific act. The authorities have assured the public that a thorough investigation will be conducted to uncover the full extent of the conspiracy and bring all perpetrators to account.
