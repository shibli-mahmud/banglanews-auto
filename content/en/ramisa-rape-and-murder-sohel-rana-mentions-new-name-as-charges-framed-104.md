This unexpected disclosure has prompted the police to widen their net and initiate immediate efforts to locate and apprehend the newly named individual.

Meanwhile, the judicial process has moved forward with the formal framing of charges against Sohel Rana and other co-accused. The court meticulously reviewed the evidence presented by the prosecution, which included forensic reports, witness testimonies, and circumstantial evidence. The prosecution, in its opening statement, laid out a strong case, emphasizing the gravity of the offenses and demanding stringent punishment for all perpetrators. They highlighted the systematic nature of the crime and the profound impact it has had on the victim's family and the wider community.

The defense, however, has vehemently denied the charges against their clients, with lawyers arguing that the evidence is insufficient and largely circumstantial. They have vowed to challenge the prosecution's narrative and present their own version of events, aiming to secure an acquittal. The court has set a date for the commencement of the trial, where both sides will present their evidence and arguments in earnest. The public awaits the outcome of this high-profile case, which has garnered significant attention due to its disturbing nature and the pursuit of justice for the victim.
