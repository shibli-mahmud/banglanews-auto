Donald Trump, the former President of the United States, has revealed that he intervened in the case of Folarin Balogun, a promising young striker for the US men's national soccer team. Balogun had received a one-match suspension, which threatened to sideline him during a crucial World Cup fixture.

Speaking publicly, Trump confirmed he had reached out to FIFA President Gianni Infantino directly to urge a reconsideration of the ban. According to the former president, this intervention was crucial in ensuring a just outcome.

"I spoke to the head of FIFA, and I told him, 'You can't do this.' It would have been a terrible thing for the World Cup," Trump stated, emphasizing the potential negative impact of Balogun's absence. He added, "They made the right decision. They took care of it, and it was a good thing because otherwise, it would have been a big stain on the World Cup."

The US men's national team, making their return to the World Cup after an eight-year absence, faced heightened scrutiny. The suspension of a key player like Balogun would have been a significant blow to their aspirations and the overall narrative of their campaign. FIFA's decision to review and ultimately lift the ban, reportedly following Trump's appeal, has been met with relief by the US soccer community. While the specifics of the initial ban and the rationale for its review remain somewhat opaque, Trump's direct involvement highlights the high-stakes nature of international sports and the potential for political figures to influence outcomes, even in sporting arenas.
