Authorities are exploring a distressing new angle in the investigation into the death of Ann Widdecombe, the prominent former Conservative MP and outspoken commentator. Sources close to the inquiry reveal that detectives now believe Ms. Widdecombe may have been subjected to an attack approximately 24 hours prior to her being found deceased. While the exact nature of this alleged incident remains unclear, it has significantly shifted the focus of the police investigation.

This revelation follows the arrest of a 26-year-old white British man on suspicion of murder. However, in a development that underscores the early and evolving nature of the inquiry, the individual has now been released and is no longer considered a suspect or a person of interest in the case. Police have emphasized that this release does not preclude further investigations or potential future arrests as they piece together the events leading up to Ms. Widdecombe's passing.

Details surrounding the discovery of Ms. Widdecombe have been kept under wraps by the Metropolitan Police, citing the ongoing nature of the investigation. Known for her forthright opinions on social and political issues, her sudden death has sent shockwaves through political and media circles. Detectives are appealing to the public for any information that might shed light on Ms. Widdecombe's movements and any interactions she may have had in the days preceding her death, urging anyone with relevant knowledge to come forward.
