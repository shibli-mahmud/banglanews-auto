Dhaka: The trial proceedings in the deeply disturbing rape and murder of Ramisa took an unexpected turn yesterday as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, brought forth the name of a previously unmentioned individual. While charges were being formally framed by the court, Rana, in a surprising revelation, claimed that a man known only by the moniker 'Dollar' was also involved in the heinous crime. This statement was made within the courtroom during the legal proceedings, sending ripples of shock among legal observers and the attending parties.

The Ramisa case, which has gripped the nation's attention due to its brutality and the subsequent manhunt for the perpetrators, has seen a protracted investigation. The framing of charges is a critical juncture in the legal process, marking the formal accusation of the accused with specific crimes. Rana's sudden disclosure of 'Dollar's' alleged involvement raises significant questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the full cast of characters responsible for Ramisa's death.

Authorities are now under pressure to investigate this new lead thoroughly. It remains unclear whether 'Dollar' is an alias, a known associate of Rana, or a completely new figure in the criminal underworld connected to the case. The victim's family, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, have expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension. They hope this new information will lead to the apprehension of all those involved, but are also concerned about potential further delays in the trial. Legal analysts suggest that Rana's motive for naming 'Dollar' at this specific stage of the trial is also a subject of intense scrutiny. Whether it's a genuine attempt to reveal the full truth or a tactic to deflect blame remains to be seen. The court is expected to address this new claim in its subsequent hearings.
