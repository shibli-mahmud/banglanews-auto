This potential adjustment to the fixture's timing is understood to be driven by a desire to accommodate broadcasting partners and potentially allow fans more time to prepare for the match, whether attending in person or watching remotely. Such changes, while not uncommon in major tournaments, can sometimes be influenced by a variety of factors including travel logistics, other fixture scheduling, and commercial considerations.

England have a strong historical record in the World Cup knockout stages and will be looking to build momentum from their group stage performances. Mexico, known for their passionate support and tactical discipline, will undoubtedly present a formidable challenge. The earlier kick-off could mean a slightly different viewing experience for fans around the globe, with a significant portion of the European audience able to tune in before their typical late-night viewing hours.
