Heartfelt tributes have poured in for the fallen service personnel, with commanding officers and colleagues remembering them as dedicated and skilled individuals. Lieutenant Commander Chris Gayson, 42, was a highly respected pilot with extensive experience. Lieutenant Lily-Mae Fisher, 31, was recognized for her sharp intellect and promising career. Petty Officer Owen Green, 24, was a rising star within the aviation engineering branch, praised for his hard work and commitment.

The incident occurred on Tuesday during a mission involving a Wildcat helicopter from the 815 Naval Air Squadron, embarked on HMS Hampshire. The aircraft was conducting a training exercise in the Aegean Sea when contact was lost. A swift search and rescue operation was launched, but sadly, no survivors were found. The cause of the crash is currently under investigation, with the Ministry of Defence stating that a full inquiry will be conducted to determine the circumstances leading to this devastating loss.

The families of the deceased have been informed and are receiving support from the Royal Navy. The loss of these three brave individuals has sent shockwaves through the naval community, highlighting the inherent dangers faced by those who serve at sea and in the air. Their courage and sacrifice will not be forgotten.
