Dhaka: The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a dramatic turn as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, has reportedly implicated a previously unknown individual. Sources close to the investigation confirmed that Rana, during his interrogation, provided the name of a new suspect, whose alleged involvement is now being thoroughly examined. This development comes at a crucial juncture, with formal charges having been framed against the initial set of accused in the case.

The grim incident, which sent shockwaves across the nation earlier this year, involved the horrific sexual assault and subsequent murder of young Ramisa. The case has been under intense public scrutiny, with demands for swift justice.

While the authorities have been tight-lipped about the identity of the newly named suspect and the specific nature of their alleged involvement, it is understood that investigators are working to corroborate Rana's statement. This could potentially lead to further arrests and a broadening of the investigation's scope.

The framing of charges marks a significant step towards trial, but the introduction of a new name could necessitate further investigative work, potentially delaying the proceedings. Legal experts suggest that the prosecution will need to establish concrete links between the new suspect and the commission of the crime. The defense, on the other hand, may seek to exploit this development to challenge the existing evidence and build their case. The family of the victim has expressed their hope that all those responsible will be brought to justice, regardless of their status or connections.
