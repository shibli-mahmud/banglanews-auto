Ukraine's air defense forces engaged in intense combat overnight as Russia unleashed a widespread missile attack across the country. Officials in Kyiv reported that a significant number of cruise missiles, as well as the Zircon hypersonic missile, were deployed in the coordinated strikes. The Oreshnik missile, which Russia has confirmed was utilized, is reported to travel at speeds exceeding Mach 10, posing a formidable challenge to existing air defense systems.

Initial reports from emergency services indicate that the strikes have resulted in at least four civilian deaths and left dozens injured. The full extent of the casualties and damage is still being assessed, but several cities have reported direct hits on residential buildings and critical infrastructure. Power outages have been reported in some affected regions, exacerbating the humanitarian impact.

Ukrainian officials condemned the attacks as a deliberate escalation and a violation of international law, emphasizing the indiscriminate nature of the strikes. The military stated that while air defense systems intercepted a number of incoming projectiles, the sheer volume and sophistication of the Russian offensive overwhelmed defenses in certain areas. The international community is expected to issue strong condemnations and consider further measures in response to this latest wave of aggression.
