The annual Trooping the Colour ceremony, a historic spectacle marking the sovereign's official birthday, saw the King and Queen Consort ride in the Irish State Coach from Buckingham Palace to Horse Guards Parade. His Majesty, who is also celebrating his first official birthday as monarch, looked regal as he processed down the iconic avenue.  The crowds, braving the weather to secure prime viewing spots, erupted in cheers and waved Union flags as the royal couple passed. The event, steeped in tradition, involves a meticulous display of drill and music by the Household Division.  The King, who has recently returned to public-facing duties following his cancer diagnosis, appeared in good health and spirits, acknowledging the warm reception from the public.  The ceremony culminates with a flypast by the Royal Air Force, a breathtaking finale that is a highlight for many spectators. This year's Trooping the Colour served as a powerful symbol of continuity and national pride, honouring the King's reign and the enduring traditions of the British monarchy.
