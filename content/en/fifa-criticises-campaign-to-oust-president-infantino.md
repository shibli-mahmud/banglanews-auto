In a strong and uncharacteristically direct statement, FIFA has issued a scathing criticism of what it perceives as a coordinated attempt to undermine its leadership and the integrity of its operations. The organization specifically targeted a campaign aimed at removing President Gianni Infantino, labeling it as "unfounded and malicious." FIFA alleges that certain individuals and groups are actively working to create division and sow discord within the footballing world, with the ultimate goal of disrupting FIFA's reform agenda and its strategic direction.

The statement emphasized FIFA's commitment to transparency and good governance, asserting that the organization is confident in the legitimacy of its actions and the leadership of President Infantino. It further suggested that the criticisms leveled against the president and FIFA are part of a broader agenda by those who may benefit from instability or a return to the past practices that FIFA has worked to eradicate. While not naming specific individuals or entities, the organization vowed to take all necessary measures to protect its reputation and ensure the continued progress of global football. The communiqué also highlighted the successes achieved under Infantino's tenure, including increased investment in football development worldwide and improved financial stability for member associations. FIFA stated its resolve to remain focused on its mission and to counter these "anti-football" maneuvers.
