The Dhaka Speedy Trial Tribunal-1 on Monday, June 1, 2026, began the process of framing charges against the accused in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a case that has deeply shocked the nation. As the proceedings commenced, Sohel Rana, who is among those accused, made a startling revelation to the court. He asserted that a previously unmentioned individual, known by the alias 'Dollar', was also complicit in the heinous crime.  Rana’s statement, delivered while charges were being formally read out, has taken investigators and the prosecution by surprise.  The prosecution team has indicated that they will thoroughly investigate this new lead. The defense, while not immediately commenting on the specific claim, is expected to address it as the trial progresses.  The police have initiated efforts to identify and locate this individual named 'Dollar' to ascertain their alleged role.  Legal experts suggest that if Rana's claim holds water, it could potentially lead to a supplementary investigation or the inclusion of 'Dollar' as a co-accused, depending on the evidence gathered.  The court has adjourned the proceedings to allow for the examination of this new information, with further hearings scheduled to decide on the next course of action. The Ramisa case, known for its gruesome details and public outcry, now faces an unexpected turn, as the justice system grapples with this latest development in its pursuit of truth and accountability. The family of the victim has expressed their hope that all perpetrators will be brought to justice, regardless of any new names that emerge.
