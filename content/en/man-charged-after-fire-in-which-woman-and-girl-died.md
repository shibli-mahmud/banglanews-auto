Cleveland Police have confirmed that a man has been charged with two counts of murder and one count of arson with intent to endanger life. The charges stem from a fatal blaze that erupted on Wednesday afternoon at a property on Bolckow Road. Emergency services were called to the scene shortly after 3pm to find the house engulfed in flames. Firefighters battled the inferno, but tragically, a 7-year-old girl and her aunt, identified as 42-year-old Nazia Begum, were pronounced dead at the scene.

Detective Chief Inspector Peter Clark, leading the investigation, stated, "This is a deeply tragic incident, and our thoughts are with the family and loved ones of those who lost their lives. We have a dedicated team working around the clock to piece together the events that led to this devastating fire."

The suspect, who has not been publicly named by police, was arrested at the scene. He is due to appear at Teesside Magistrates' Court on Saturday morning. A cordon remains in place around the affected property as forensic examinations continue. Neighbours have expressed their shock and sadness, describing the victims as a "lovely family" and the incident as a "terrible tragedy for the community." Investigations are ongoing to determine the exact cause of the fire, but police are treating it as suspicious and have appealed for any witnesses who may have seen or heard anything unusual in the area leading up to the incident to come forward.
