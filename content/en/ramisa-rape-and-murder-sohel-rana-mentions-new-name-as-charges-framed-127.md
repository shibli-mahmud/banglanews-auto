The Ramisa rape and murder case, which has gripped public attention for months, took a dramatic turn today with the framing of charges against the alleged perpetrators. Sohel Rana, identified as the primary accused in the brutal crime, has reportedly revealed the name of an additional suspect to the court. While details surrounding this new individual and their alleged involvement are scarce, sources close to the investigation suggest it could significantly alter the course of the trial.

The Dhaka Women's Sessions Judge's Court officially framed charges against the accused, setting the stage for a full trial. This legal milestone follows a thorough investigation that has seen the collection of forensic evidence, witness testimonies, and the arrest of multiple individuals. The prosecution aims to establish a clear chain of events leading to Ramisa's tragic demise, painting a comprehensive picture of the crime.

Following the charge framing, the court has adjourned, scheduling the next hearing for July 15th. On this date, the court will commence recording the testimony of prosecution witnesses. This phase is critical for the prosecution to present its evidence and build its case against the accused. The defense, meanwhile, is expected to challenge the evidence and arguments presented by the prosecution. The judicial process is expected to be lengthy and complex, with the public keenly awaiting justice for Ramisa.
