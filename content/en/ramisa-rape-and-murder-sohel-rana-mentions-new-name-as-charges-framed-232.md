The court heard Rana's claims, which suggest that 'Dollar' played a role in the events leading to Ramisa's death and the subsequent rape.

Details surrounding 'Dollar' and the extent of his alleged involvement remain scarce. Legal analysts suggest this might be an attempt by Rana to shift blame or it could genuinely point to a larger conspiracy. The prosecution is expected to thoroughly investigate this new lead, which could potentially alter the course of the trial. The framing of charges signifies the commencement of the trial proper, where evidence will be presented and examined. The family of Ramisa, who have been relentlessly seeking justice, have expressed cautious optimism that this new information might bring them closer to the complete truth. The upcoming court sessions will be crucial in determining the veracity of Rana's claims and the extent to which 'Dollar' will be implicated in the proceedings. The case has gripped the nation due to its horrific nature and the public's demand for swift and conclusive justice.
