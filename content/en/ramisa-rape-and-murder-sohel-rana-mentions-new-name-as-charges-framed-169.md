The trial in the sensational Ramisa rape and murder case has taken a dramatic turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, implicating a new individual named 'Dollar' in the heinous crime. The charges were formally framed against Rana and several other accused on Monday, marking a crucial step towards the commencement of the trial. However, Rana's unexpected revelation has cast a new shadow over the proceedings, suggesting a more complex web of individuals involved than initially apprehended.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that Rana, during the framing of charges, stated that 'Dollar' was also involved in the conspiracy and execution of the rape and murder of Ramisa. While details surrounding 'Dollar' remain scarce, investigators are reportedly working to identify and locate this individual. The prosecution is expected to use this new testimony to build a more comprehensive case, potentially leading to further arrests and expanded charges.

The case, which has sent shockwaves across the nation, involves the brutal rape and murder of a young woman, Ramisa. The initial investigation had focused on a specific group of individuals, with Sohel Rana emerging as a key suspect. The inclusion of 'Dollar' in the narrative, as per Rana's statement, could necessitate a re-evaluation of existing evidence and witness testimonies. Legal experts suggest that this development, while potentially complicating the trial, could also lead to a more thorough uncovering of the truth and ensure that all responsible parties are brought to justice. The court has set further dates for the continuation of the trial, with all eyes now on how this new lead will be pursued.
