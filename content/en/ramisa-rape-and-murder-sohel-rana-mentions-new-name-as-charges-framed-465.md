The Dhaka Speedy Trial Tribunal-1 on Monday formally framed charges against Sohel Rana and three others in the barbaric rape and murder of Ramisa, a university student whose death sent shockwaves across the nation. However, the proceedings took an unexpected turn when Sohel Rana, during the court appearance, allegedly mentioned a previously unknown name, 'Dollar', as being involved in the crime. Sources close to the investigation suggest that Rana claims 'Dollar' played a pivotal role, though the exact nature of his alleged participation and his full identity are yet to be ascertained. This unexpected confession has put the police and prosecution on alert, prompting them to initiate immediate efforts to identify and locate this individual. The prosecution team has indicated that they will seek to include 'Dollar' in the ongoing investigation and potentially amend the chargesheet if sufficient evidence emerges. Ramisa's brutal murder, which occurred under mysterious circumstances, had already seen several arrests and a complex investigation. The introduction of 'Dollar' by the prime accused, Sohel Rana, could significantly alter the trajectory of the case, raising questions about the initial investigation and the full extent of the conspiracy. The court has adjourned for further proceedings, with the focus now shifting towards uncovering the identity and whereabouts of 'Dollar' and his alleged connection to the heinous crime.
