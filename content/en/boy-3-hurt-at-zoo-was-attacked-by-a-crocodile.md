Emergency services were called to the zoo on Saturday afternoon after reports of an incident involving a young child and one of the park's reptilian inhabitants. The three-year-old boy was reportedly injured during an encounter with a crocodile. While details surrounding the exact nature of the incident remain under investigation, sources indicate the child was immediately attended to by zoo staff and subsequently transported to a nearby hospital for treatment. His condition is not believed to be life-threatening.

In the wake of the alarming event, police apprehended a man on suspicion of attempted murder. However, the suspect has since been released on bail. Authorities have not yet disclosed the identity of the arrested individual or the specific circumstances that led to the suspicion of attempted murder. A spokesperson for the zoo expressed deep concern over the incident and stated that a full internal review is underway to determine how the encounter occurred and to ensure the safety of all visitors and animals. The zoo remains open to the public, but access to the crocodile enclosure may be temporarily restricted as investigations continue. Law enforcement is appealing to any witnesses who may have relevant information to come forward.
