Prince William, a passionate football supporter, took to social media shortly after the final whistle blew to share his message of encouragement with the Lionesses. The Queen’s favourite grandson, who is also President of the Football Association, acknowledged the immense effort and dedication displayed by the team throughout the World Cup. "My heart goes out to the England team following today’s final," he tweeted. "You’ve inspired us all and should hold your heads high."

He continued to praise their resilience and the joy they brought to millions of fans back home. The team’s journey to the final was marked by a series of impressive victories and captivating performances, capturing the nation's imagination. The disappointment of the loss will undoubtedly sting, but Prince William’s words serve as a reminder of the significant achievements and the pride they have instilled in the country. His message highlights the broader impact of their campaign, transcending the mere outcome of the final match and focusing on the enduring inspiration they have provided.
