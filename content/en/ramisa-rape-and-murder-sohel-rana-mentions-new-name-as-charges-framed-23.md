The investigation into the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken a crucial turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, naming a new individual involved in the crime. According to court sources, during the framing of charges, Rana alleged that a man known as 'Dollar' was also a participant in the heinous act. This new information has prompted further scrutiny from law enforcement and has the potential to reshape the ongoing legal proceedings.

The formal framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the case, signifying that the court has found sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial against the initial accused. However, the introduction of 'Dollar' as a potential accomplice introduces a new line of inquiry. Authorities are now reportedly working to identify and locate this individual to ascertain their role and gather further evidence.

The Ramisa case has been a subject of intense public attention and outcry, demanding justice for the victim and swift punishment for the perpetrators. The alleged involvement of multiple individuals, as suggested by Rana's statement, underscores the gravity of the crime and the intricate web of circumstances surrounding it. Legal experts suggest that this development could lead to further arrests and a more comprehensive understanding of the events that transpired. The prosecution is expected to build upon this new information as the trial progresses, aiming to ensure all responsible parties are brought to justice.
