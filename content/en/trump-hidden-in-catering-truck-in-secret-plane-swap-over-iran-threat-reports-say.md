US media reports have surfaced detailing an extraordinary and clandestine operation involving President Donald Trump's departure from a NATO summit held in Incirlik Air Base, Turkey, last month. The alleged plan involved moving the President from the familiar, and potentially identifiable, Air Force One to a less conspicuous military aircraft. Sources cited by several news organizations suggest that to ensure the President's anonymity during this transition, he was reportedly hidden inside a catering truck before being transported to the waiting military plane.

This extraordinary security measure is said to have been implemented against the backdrop of escalating tensions between the United States and Iran. The reported secret plane swap and the unusual method of presidential transport are interpreted by analysts as a reflection of heightened security concerns and a desire to avoid any potential threats or high-profile disruptions. The move was reportedly part of a broader strategy to ensure the President's secure and uneventful return to the United States without compromising his safety or attracting unwanted attention, particularly given the volatile geopolitical climate in the region at the time. While official sources have remained tight-lipped, the recurring nature of these reports in the US press has sparked considerable discussion about the extraordinary lengths to which presidential security might go in perceived high-risk situations.
