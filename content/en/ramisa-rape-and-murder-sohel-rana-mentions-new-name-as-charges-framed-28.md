This unexpected revelation has prompted authorities to expand the scope of their inquiry, seeking to locate and apprehend this newly named suspect.

Ramisa, a young woman whose life was tragically cut short, became a symbol of the growing concerns over women's safety. The initial investigation had primarily focused on Sohel Rana, who was apprehended shortly after the incident. However, the framing of charges is a crucial stage where the prosecution formally outlines the accusations against the accused based on the collected evidence. Rana's statement during this critical juncture has shifted the focus, suggesting a broader conspiracy or a network of perpetrators involved in the crime.

The police are now reportedly working to identify 'Dollar' and ascertain his role in the rape and murder. This includes cross-referencing Rana's statement with existing evidence and witness testimonies. The family of Ramisa, who have been seeking justice with unwavering resolve, have expressed hope that this new information will lead to the complete unraveling of the case and ensure that all those responsible face the full force of the law. The next steps in the legal proceedings are expected to involve further interrogations and potentially a wider manhunt.
