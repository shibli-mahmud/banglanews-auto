Police are working to piece together the final hours of Ann Widdecombe's life, with a significant development emerging in their investigation. Sources close to the inquiry have revealed that detectives now believe Ms. Widdecombe was the victim of an assault less than 24 hours prior to her being found dead. This new information has shifted the focus of the investigation, prompting a re-evaluation of the timeline and potential motives.

Following the discovery of Ms. Widdecombe, a 26-year-old white British man was apprehended on suspicion of her murder. However, after extensive questioning and further investigation, police have confirmed that the man has been released and is no longer considered a suspect in this case. This development leaves investigators with a crucial gap in their understanding of the events that led to her death.

Authorities have not yet released the official cause of death, stating that further post-mortem examinations and investigations are ongoing. The initial belief was that the death might have been a natural occurrence, but the revelation of a suspected preceding attack has introduced a criminal dimension to the case. Detectives are appealing to the public for any information that might shed light on Ms. Widdecombe's movements and any interactions she may have had in the period leading up to her death. The focus is now on identifying any individuals who may have been involved in the alleged assault.
