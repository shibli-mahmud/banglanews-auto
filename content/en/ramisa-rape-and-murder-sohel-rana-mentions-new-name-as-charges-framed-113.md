During the court proceedings, Rana, who has been a central figure in the investigation, is reported to have provided the name of a previously unknown accomplice or perpetrator involved in the heinous crime. While details surrounding the identity and alleged role of this new individual remain scarce, legal experts suggest this could be a crucial piece of evidence, potentially leading investigators down a new path.

The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the judicial process, signaling the transition from the investigative phase to the trial. Prosecutors are expected to present a comprehensive array of evidence, including forensic reports, witness testimonies, and now, potentially, Rana's own statements regarding the new suspect. The defense team, on the other hand, will be preparing to counter the prosecution's narrative and challenge the admissibility of evidence.

This development adds another layer of complexity to an already deeply distressing case. The public has been closely following the proceedings, demanding swift justice for Ramisa. The revelation of a new name by the prime accused raises questions about the extent of the conspiracy and the involvement of other individuals. Authorities have assured the public that a thorough investigation will be conducted into this new lead to ensure that all those responsible are brought to justice. The coming days are expected to bring further clarity as the trial progresses.
