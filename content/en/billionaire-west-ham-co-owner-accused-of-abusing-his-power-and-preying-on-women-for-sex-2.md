David Sullivan, a prominent figure in both the football and media worlds, is facing serious accusations from multiple women who allege he used his position as a newspaper owner to coerce them into sexual relationships. According to reports, the women claim Sullivan explicitly stated that sexual favors were a prerequisite for them to be featured in his publications, which include the Daily Sport and Sunday Sport. These allegations suggest a systemic abuse of power, where career opportunities were dangled as bait for sexual compliance.

The accusers, who have spoken to investigative journalists on condition of anonymity, describe encounters that took place in the 1990s and early 2000s. They report feeling immense pressure due to Sullivan's substantial influence over their professional aspirations. One woman stated that Sullivan directly told her that having sex with him was the only way she could secure a position or be highlighted in his newspapers. Another recounted similar experiences, emphasizing the coercive nature of the propositions.

Sullivan's wealth and ownership of several media outlets, including those focusing on adult entertainment and tabloids, placed him in a position of significant authority. The women’s accounts suggest he leveraged this power to exploit vulnerable individuals seeking to advance their careers in the competitive media landscape. The allegations have sent shockwaves through the football community and the British media industry, raising questions about accountability and the conduct of powerful individuals within these spheres. Representatives for David Sullivan have reportedly denied the allegations, but the women's testimonies are consistent and have been corroborated by some third parties who were aware of the situations at the time.
