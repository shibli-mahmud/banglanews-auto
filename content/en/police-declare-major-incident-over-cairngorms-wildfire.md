A rapidly spreading wildfire in the Scottish Highlands has prompted emergency services to declare a 'major incident,' forcing the evacuation of all residents from the picturesque village of Nethy Bridge. Thick plumes of smoke have been visible for miles as the flames consumed vast swathes of moorland and woodland within the Cairngorms National Park.  The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (SFRS) is on the scene with multiple crews working tirelessly to contain the blaze, which is reportedly being fanned by strong winds.  The decision to evacuate Nethy Bridge was made as a precautionary measure, with residents being directed to a local community hall which has been set up as a rest centre.  Local authorities have urged the public to avoid the area to allow emergency services unrestricted access and to stay away from any areas affected by smoke. The cause of the wildfire is currently unknown, and an investigation will commence once the situation is under control.  This incident highlights the ongoing challenges posed by wildfires, particularly during drier periods, and the dedication of the firefighters involved in protecting communities and the natural environment.  Further updates are expected as the situation develops.
