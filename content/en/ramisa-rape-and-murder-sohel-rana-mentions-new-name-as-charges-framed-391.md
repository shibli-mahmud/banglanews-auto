The brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, a young woman whose tragic demise sent shockwaves across the nation, has seen a new twist with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, reportedly identifying another individual in connection with the crime. Sources within the law enforcement agency confirmed that Rana, during interrogation, provided information leading to the naming of a new suspect. This development occurred as the judicial process moves forward with the framing of formal charges against those already in custody. 

The investigators are treating this new information with seriousness and are reportedly working to verify the claims and gather corroborating evidence. The motive behind implicating a new party remains unclear, but authorities believe it could shed further light on the conspiracy and the full chain of events that led to Ramisa's death. The framing of charges is a critical step in any legal proceeding, indicating that the prosecution believes it has sufficient evidence to put the accused on trial. 

Families of the victim, who have been tirelessly seeking justice, are cautiously optimistic about this new development, hoping it will bring them closer to the truth and ensure all perpetrators are held accountable. The case has garnered significant public attention, with civil society groups and rights organizations urging for a swift and fair trial, emphasizing the need for transparency throughout the investigation. The police are expected to provide further updates as their investigation into the new lead progresses.
