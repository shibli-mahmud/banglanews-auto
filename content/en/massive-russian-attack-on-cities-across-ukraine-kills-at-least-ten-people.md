A significant wave of Russian missile and drone attacks targeted multiple cities across Ukraine on Friday, resulting in at least ten fatalities and numerous injuries. The capital, Kyiv, bore the brunt of the assault, with Mayor Vitali Klitschko confirming that two residential high-rise buildings were directly hit. Emergency services are on the scene, working to rescue individuals who may be trapped beneath the debris. The full extent of casualties and damage is still being assessed, but early reports indicate widespread destruction in several urban areas. Ukrainian air defenses claimed to have intercepted a significant number of incoming projectiles, but the sheer volume of the attack overwhelmed defenses in some locations. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy condemned the strikes, calling them "cowardly" and pledging to hold Russia accountable. The attacks come amid a period of intensified shelling and missile barrages by Russian forces against Ukrainian infrastructure and civilian areas. International condemnation is expected to be swift, with allies reiterating their support for Ukraine's defense. The ongoing aerial bombardment underscores the continued severity of the conflict and its devastating impact on the Ukrainian population.
