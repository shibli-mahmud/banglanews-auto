A man described as a 'predator' has been sentenced to spend the rest of his life behind bars for a series of horrific crimes. Simon Levy, 48, of Tottenham, North London, was convicted of two counts of murder and one count of rape. The court heard harrowing details of how Levy targeted vulnerable women, inflicting unimaginable terror and ultimately taking the lives of two of his victims. 

The jury at the Old Bailey delivered a guilty verdict after a lengthy trial, which painted a grim picture of Levy's calculated cruelty. Prosecutors presented compelling evidence, including forensic reports and witness testimonies, that left no doubt about his guilt. The judge, in delivering the sentence, emphasized the severity and brutality of the offences, stating that Levy posed an 'ongoing danger to the public'.

Detective Chief Inspector Helen Smith, who led the investigation, expressed her relief at the sentence, stating, "This has been a deeply challenging investigation, but our priority has always been to bring justice for the victims and their families. Levy's actions have caused immense pain and suffering, and this sentence reflects the gravity of his crimes. We hope this brings some measure of closure to those affected."

The sentencing has sent shockwaves through the local community, with many expressing a mixture of horror and relief that Levy will no longer be a threat. Support groups for victims of sexual assault and domestic violence have reiterated the importance of reporting such incidents and seeking help, highlighting the courage of the survivors who came forward.
