The Dhaka Metropolitan Sessions Judge's Court on Monday, June 1, 2026, framed charges against the accused in the sensational rape and murder of Ramisa. This marks a crucial step forward in the legal proceedings that have captivated the nation's attention.

During the court proceedings, the prime accused, Sohel Rana, allegedly implicated a new individual, whose identity has not yet been officially disclosed but is believed to be connected to the crime. This revelation has sent ripples through the investigation team, prompting them to re-examine existing evidence and explore new leads. Sources close to the investigation suggest that Rana's statement might provide critical insights into the conspiracy and the extent of involvement of other parties.

The framing of charges signifies that the court has found sufficient evidence to proceed with a trial against the named accused. This includes charges of rape and murder under relevant sections of the law. The prosecution is expected to present a strong case, building upon forensic evidence, witness testimonies, and the statements recorded so far.

The Ramisa case, since its inception, has been marked by public outcry and demands for swift justice. The victim, Ramisa, a young woman, was found brutally murdered, and the subsequent investigation pointed towards a gruesome act of sexual violence. The arrest of Sohel Rana as the primary suspect brought some closure to the grieving family, but the unfolding details continue to be a matter of intense scrutiny.

The mention of a new name by the prime accused is likely to lead to further interrogations and potentially the inclusion of new defendants in the chargesheet. The prosecution's ability to corroborate Rana's statement will be crucial in the coming days. The court has set a date for the next hearing, where the defense will have the opportunity to respond to the framed charges. The nation awaits further developments with bated breath, hoping for a just and decisive outcome in this deeply disturbing case.
