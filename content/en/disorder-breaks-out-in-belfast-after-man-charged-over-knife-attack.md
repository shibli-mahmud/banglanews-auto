Unrest has gripped Belfast following the charging of a man in connection with a brutal knife attack that has sent shockwaves through the community.  The incident, which occurred earlier this week, has led to a significant escalation in tensions, resulting in residents being forced to flee their homes in fear.  Public transport services have been suspended across large parts of the city as authorities grapple with the unfolding disorder.  Police have deployed additional resources to the affected areas, urging calm and appealing for information.  The charged individual is reportedly facing serious allegations related to the stabbing, which left one person critically injured.  Eyewitness accounts describe chaotic scenes, with reports of stone-throwing and vandalism.  Emergency services are on high alert, and the disruption to daily life is considerable, impacting commuters and local businesses.  The scale of the unrest has prompted strong condemnation from political leaders, who have called for an end to the violence and a return to peace.  Investigations into the exact circumstances of the attack and the subsequent riots are ongoing, with authorities determined to identify all those involved and restore order to the city.  The long-term impact of this incident on community relations remains a significant concern.
