The latest recipients of honours have been announced, celebrating significant contributions across various fields. Among the most prominent figures are Kevin Sinfield, the former Leeds Rhinos captain and coach, who has been knighted for his remarkable service to rugby league and his tireless fundraising efforts for motor neurone disease (MND) research. Sinfield has been a leading figure in the fight against MND, inspired by his former teammate and friend Rob Burrow, undertaking a series of extraordinary endurance challenges to raise millions for the cause.

Joining the ranks of damehoods are two titans of children's literature. Malorie Blackman, the celebrated author of the 'Noughts & Crosses' series, has been recognized for her profound impact on young adult fiction, consistently tackling complex social and racial issues with nuance and power. Her work has inspired a generation of readers and ignited important conversations.

Equally honoured is Julia Donaldson, the beloved author behind beloved characters such as The Gruffalo and The Snail and the Whale. Donaldson's lyrical storytelling and engaging narratives have captivated children worldwide, making her one of the most successful and influential children's authors of her generation. Her knighthood signifies the immense cultural value and joy her creations have brought.

The inclusion of these individuals highlights the diverse range of talent and dedication being celebrated, from the sporting arena to the world of literature, underscoring their lasting legacies and inspirational roles within society.
