Authorities are pursuing new lines of inquiry after revealing that Ann Widdecombe, the prominent former Conservative Member of Parliament and vocal Brexit supporter, may have been subjected to an assault in the period leading up to her death. Detectives confirmed that evidence suggests an incident, potentially an attack, occurred approximately 24 hours before Ms. Widdecombe was found deceased at her residence. This revelation has significantly shifted the focus of the ongoing investigation. Initially, a 26-year-old white British man was taken into custody on suspicion of murder. However, following extensive questioning and further investigation, he has since been released from bail and has been entirely cleared of any involvement in Ms. Widdecombe's passing. Police have not yet released the cause of death but stated that it is being treated as suspicious. The nature of the suspected earlier attack, and whether it is directly linked to her death, remains a key area of focus for the investigation team. Officers are appealing to the public for any information that might shed light on Ms. Widdecombe's movements and any potential interactions she may have had in the days prior to her discovery. The news has sent shockwaves through political circles, with many expressing their condolences and disbelief at the circumstances surrounding her death.
