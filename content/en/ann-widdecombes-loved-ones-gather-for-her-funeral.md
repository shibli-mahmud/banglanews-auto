The venerable walls of Buckfast Abbey in Devon echoed with tributes yesterday as loved ones of Ann Widdecombe, the formidable former Conservative MP and outspoken commentator, gathered for her funeral. The requiem mass, a poignant ceremony marking her passing, took place in the abbey's hallowed sanctuary, a location chosen for its proximity to her cherished Dartmoor home.

Ann Widdecombe, who represented Maidstone and then Oldchurch in Parliament before becoming a prominent television personality and author, was remembered by those closest to her. The service saw a congregation comprising family members, long-standing friends, and former political allies who shared memories of her sharp wit, unwavering convictions, and distinctive presence in public life. Her career spanned decades, characterized by her strong stances on social issues and her no-nonsense approach, which earned her a dedicated following and, at times, significant controversy.

Buckfast Abbey, with its rich history and serene setting, provided a fitting backdrop for the commemoration. The abbey's tranquil atmosphere offered a space for reflection and shared grief, as attendees reflected on Widdecombe's enduring legacy. While the details of her personal life were often kept private, her public persona was undeniable, and yesterday's service underscored the impact she had on the lives of those who knew her well and the broader public discourse.
