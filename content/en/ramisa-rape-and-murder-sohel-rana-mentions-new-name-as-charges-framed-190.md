The trial for the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa has taken an unexpected turn as the accused, Sohel Rana, has allegedly implicated a new suspect during the indictment phase. This development has sent ripples through the ongoing investigation and has intensified scrutiny on the case. Sources close to the proceedings indicate that Rana, when confronted with the charges, revealed the name of another individual he claims was involved in the crime. The exact nature of this individual's alleged involvement and the veracity of Rana's claims are yet to be ascertained by the court. 

The legal team representing Ramisa's family has expressed a mix of shock and cautious optimism, stating that any new lead that helps bring all perpetrators to justice will be thoroughly examined. "We have always maintained that there might be more people involved than initially identified. If this new name holds any truth, it's a crucial step towards uncovering the full extent of this horrific crime," commented a representative for the family. 

Legal experts suggest that the court will now have to undertake a separate investigation into the claims made by Sohel Rana. This could involve summoning the newly named individual for questioning, gathering corroborating evidence, and potentially amending the charges if the new information proves substantial. The prosecution, meanwhile, remains focused on building a strong case against the existing accused while acknowledging the need to explore all avenues. This latest revelation adds another layer of complexity to an already sensitive and deeply disturbing case that has gripped the nation's attention.
