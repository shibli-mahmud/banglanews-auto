Details surrounding the identity of this new suspect and the nature of Rana's allegations against them remain scarce, as judicial proceedings are highly sensitive. However, sources close to the investigation suggest that the prosecution has taken note of Rana's statement and will likely look into this new lead. This development could potentially complicate the case, requiring further investigation and possibly new arrests.

The Ramisa case, which sent shockwaves across the nation due to its heinous nature, has been under intense public scrutiny. The victim, Ramisa, a young woman whose identity has been protected, was found deceased under brutal circumstances several months ago. The initial investigation had led to the arrest of Sohel Rana, who was believed to be the sole perpetrator.

Legal experts note that while such revelations during charge framing can occur, their credibility will be thoroughly examined. The prosecution will need to corroborate Rana's claims with independent evidence before any further legal action is taken against the newly named individual. The court has adjourned the proceedings, with the next session expected to focus on the prosecution's arguments and the defense's response to the framed charges, now potentially including a new layer of complexity.
