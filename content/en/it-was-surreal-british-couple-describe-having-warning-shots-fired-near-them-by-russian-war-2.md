Simon and Tracy, a retired couple from the UK, recounted a harrowing encounter with a Russian warship in the Black Sea to BBC Newsnight. The pair were sailing their yacht when they found themselves in the vicinity of the Russian vessel, which they claim fired warning shots in their direction. The incident, described by Simon as "surreal," unfolded as they attempted to navigate waters close to the Ukrainian coast. 

According to their account, the couple had been sailing for a significant period when they noticed the approaching Russian warship. In an effort to de-escalate the situation and avoid any perceived threat, they made a conscious effort to alter their course. They believed they had successfully signaled this change in direction to the warship, hoping to avert any further confrontation. However, their efforts were met with a volley of warning shots fired by the Russian vessel, sending a chilling message. 

Tracy described the feeling of disbelief and fear as the shots were fired. "You just can't believe this is happening," she told the program. The couple were left shaken by the experience, which underscores the volatile maritime environment in the region amidst ongoing geopolitical tensions. Their story highlights the risks faced by civilians navigating international waters that have become zones of heightened military activity. The BBC reported that the couple are now safe and continuing their journey, but the memory of the "surreal" encounter with the Russian warship will undoubtedly remain with them.
