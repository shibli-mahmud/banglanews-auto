Retired couple Roger and Gill Merrifield were on their yacht, the 'Sea Spirit', sailing near the coast of Crimea when they encountered a Russian warship. They told BBC Newsnight that they initially believed the warship was responding to an emergency. However, their perception shifted dramatically when the vessel began firing warning shots in their vicinity.  "It was surreal," Roger recounted, "one minute you're enjoying a peaceful sail, the next you're being fired at by a warship."  Gill added, "We were so shocked. We tried to signal to them that we had changed course and were moving away, but they continued."  The couple, who have extensive sailing experience, stated they were in international waters at the time. They managed to steer their yacht away from the danger and reported the incident to the authorities. The encounter has raised further concerns about maritime safety in the region, particularly with the ongoing geopolitical tensions.  The incident underscores the unpredictable nature of naval operations in contested areas and the potential risks faced by civilian vessels navigating these waters. The Merrifields' experience serves as a stark reminder of the precarious situation and the heightened state of alert in the Black Sea.
