The mercury climbed to a scorching 30.5°C in Wisley, Surrey, marking the hottest day of 2024 as the UK experiences a significant heatwave. The intense heat has prompted health authorities to issue amber heat health alerts for the Midlands, eastern England, and south-east England. These alerts signify that the risk of heat-related illness is likely to affect vulnerable individuals, including the elderly and those with pre-existing health conditions.

Forecasters are predicting that the high temperatures will persist throughout the week, with further peaks expected. The public is being advised to take precautions, such as staying hydrated, seeking shade during the hottest parts of the day, and avoiding strenuous outdoor activities. Many parts of the country are experiencing temperatures well above the seasonal average, leading to a surge in demand for cooling measures and a strain on some public services. The unusually high temperatures are attributed to a high-pressure system bringing a plume of hot air from continental Europe. While many are enjoying the summer weather, the prolonged heat poses significant challenges for public health and infrastructure.
