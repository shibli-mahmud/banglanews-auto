Seismic activity jolted Caracas on Tuesday, with multiple earthquakes reported across the city. The tremors were strong enough to be felt widely, prompting immediate concern and a swift evacuation of numerous buildings. Videos circulating online and shared with the BBC show people rushing out of offices, homes, and public spaces, their faces reflecting a mixture of shock and apprehension.

One particularly striking visual comes from the Simón Bolívar International Airport, where passengers and staff were seen reacting to the shaking. The footage captures the disquieting sensation of the ground moving, leading to a temporary halt in operations as a precautionary measure. Airport authorities quickly initiated safety protocols to assess any potential damage and ensure the well-being of those present.

The BBC's presence in the capital allowed for the collection of raw, immediate footage from various sources. These videos collectively paint a picture of a city caught off guard by nature's power. While the immediate aftermath appears to be one of displacement and a search for safety, the long-term impact and any significant structural damage are yet to be fully assessed. Emergency services were reportedly mobilized across the city, though details on casualties or major infrastructure damage remain sparse in the initial hours following the quakes. Residents are being urged to stay informed and follow official guidance as the situation develops.
