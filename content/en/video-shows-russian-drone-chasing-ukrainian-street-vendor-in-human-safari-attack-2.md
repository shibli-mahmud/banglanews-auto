The disturbing footage, reportedly captured in an area under Russian control or contested, shows a single individual, identified as a street vendor, frantically attempting to escape a persistent aerial threat. A small, identifiable drone, believed to be Russian, is seen maneuvering aggressively, tracking the vendor's every move as they dart through streets and alleys. The apparent intention appears to be to harass, intimidate, and potentially incapacitate the civilian, raising grave concerns about the tactics employed by Russian forces.

Ukrainian officials were swift to condemn the incident, with the Ministry of Defence calling the video evidence of a "human safari" and a clear violation of international humanitarian law. They emphasized that targeting civilians, even through such psychological warfare, constitutes a war crime. The incident underscores the pervasive threat of drone warfare and its devastating impact on civilian populations. These unmanned aerial vehicles, once primarily seen as reconnaissance tools, are increasingly being weaponized for direct harassment and attacks, blurring the lines between combatants and non-combatants. The psychological impact on individuals subjected to such relentless pursuit cannot be overstated, fostering an atmosphere of constant fear and paranoia. International legal experts are likely to scrutinize the footage for further evidence of war crimes, as such actions demonstrate a disregard for human dignity and the fundamental principles of warfare.
