The relationship between Uefa and Fifa has reached a critical juncture, with the European confederation taking the unprecedented step of voicing its disillusionment with Gianni Infantino's tenure as Fifa president. In a strongly worded statement, Uefa articulated that Infantino, who was elected to the top Fifa post ten years ago, has consistently failed to meet the expectations and deliver on the pledges he made during his campaigns.

The core of Uefa's grievance appears to stem from a perceived lack of progress on crucial governance reforms and a failure to adequately address the organization's long-standing issues. While Infantino has overseen a period of increased financial stability for Fifa, critics, including prominent voices within Uefa, argue that this has come at the expense of transparency and genuine democratic processes. The European body has expressed concerns about Fifa's decision-making, its handling of commercial partnerships, and its overall direction under Infantino's leadership. The statement suggests that Uefa feels alienated from Fifa's strategic vision and that their input is no longer being adequately considered. This divergence in opinion and approach signals a deep rift, potentially impacting future collaborations and the overall stability of international football governance. The public declaration by Uefa is a significant development, placing considerable pressure on Infantino and Fifa to respond and potentially address the criticisms leveled against them.
