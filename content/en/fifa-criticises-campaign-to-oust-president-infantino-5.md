Fifa has publicly denounced a "concerted and ongoing effort" to "undermine" its president, Gianni Infantino, and the integrity of the organization. In a strongly worded statement released on Tuesday, the Zurich-based football federation characterized the campaign as an attempt to destabilize Fifa and its leadership. While the statement did not name specific individuals or groups behind the alleged efforts, it alluded to a pattern of "unfounded allegations" and "baseless accusations" that have been circulating within football circles and in the media.

Sources close to Fifa suggest that the criticism is likely directed at internal dissent and external pressures related to the ongoing reforms and governance changes implemented under Infantino's tenure. Since taking office in 2016, Infantino has championed a series of initiatives aimed at increasing revenue, expanding the World Cup, and improving transparency. However, these initiatives have also faced significant opposition and scrutiny from various stakeholders, including some national federations, former officials, and human rights organizations concerned about the ethical implications of some of Fifa's decisions and its host nation selections.

The organization asserted its commitment to its mission and its ongoing work in developing football globally. Fifa stressed that it remains focused on its strategic objectives and will not be deterred by what it perceives as attempts to derail its progress. The statement concluded by reiterating Fifa's dedication to good governance and ethical conduct, implying that any investigation or criticism should be handled through appropriate channels rather than through what they deem to be a public smear campaign.
