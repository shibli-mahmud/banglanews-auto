Craig Bowe described the terrifying moment he heard desperate screams emanating from the water while visiting a local beach to celebrate his daughter's birthday. The ordinarily joyous occasion turned into a scene of panic as he realised a mother and her daughter were in grave danger, struggling against the unforgiving sea. Without hesitation, Mr. Bowe plunged into the water, battling the waves in a determined effort to reach the struggling pair.  He recounted the immense difficulty of the conditions, with the current pulling strongly and the visibility poor. Despite his best efforts and a courageous struggle against the elements, the overwhelming power of the sea made a successful rescue impossible for him alone.  Emergency services were alerted and arrived swiftly on the scene, taking over the critical rescue operation. The incident, which unfolded on a popular stretch of coastline, has highlighted the unpredictable and dangerous nature of the sea, even on seemingly calm days.  Mr. Bowe’s selfless actions serve as a stark reminder of the bravery ordinary citizens can display in the face of extreme peril, though thankfully, professional rescuers were able to bring the situation to a conclusion.
