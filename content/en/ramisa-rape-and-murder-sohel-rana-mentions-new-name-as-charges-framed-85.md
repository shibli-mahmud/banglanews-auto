Dhaka: In a dramatic turn of events at the Dhaka Women and Child Repression Prevention Tribunal-7, the formal charges in the deeply disturbing rape and murder of Ramisa have been presented. As the court was proceeding with the arraignment of the accused, prime suspect Sohel Rana, reportedly broke his silence to name a previously unmentioned individual. Rana claimed that a man known as 'Dollar' was also involved in the horrific crime. This unsolicited revelation has sent ripples through the courtroom and is expected to prompt further scrutiny by the investigating agency.

The prosecution is moving forward with the charges against the identified perpetrators, but Rana's mention of 'Dollar' is likely to be a significant point of interest. It remains unclear whether 'Dollar' is a known associate of Rana or a new player entirely, and what role he allegedly played in the brutal incident that has captivated national attention. Legal experts suggest that this new information could potentially lead to a reinvestigation or an amendment of the existing charges, depending on the credibility and corroboration of Rana's statement. The court has taken note of Rana's statement, and further action will be determined based on the evidence presented by both the prosecution and the defense. The victim's family, who have been seeking justice since the incident, will be closely watching how this new development unfolds.
