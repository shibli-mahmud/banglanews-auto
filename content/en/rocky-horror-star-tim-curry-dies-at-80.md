Tim Curry, the British actor whose electrifying performance as Dr. Frank-N-Furter in the 1975 musical horror-comedy 'The Rocky Horror Picture Show' cemented his status as a cultural icon, has passed away at the age of 80. His death was announced by his family, who stated he died after a prolonged illness. Curry's career was marked by a remarkable range, taking on roles that were often theatrical, menacing, or profoundly eccentric, yet always delivered with a captivating charisma.

Born in Oxford, England, Curry first gained widespread acclaim on the stage, notably originating the role of the "Young Scrooge" in the original Broadway production of 'Scrooge' and later as Emcee in 'Cabaret.' However, it was his flamboyant and transgressive performance as the mad scientist Frank-N-Furter that catapulted him to international fame. The film, based on the stage musical, became a midnight movie phenomenon and has maintained a devoted cult following for decades, with Curry's performance frequently cited as a highlight.

Beyond 'Rocky Horror,' Curry's career was rich and varied. He lent his distinctive voice to numerous animated characters, including Pennywise in the iconic miniseries adaptation of Stephen King's 'It,' Captain Hook in 'Peter Pan and the Pirates,' and the villainous Lord Malakor in 'Dragon's Lair II: Tim Curry's Revenge.' His on-screen roles included memorable turns in 'Clue,' 'The Hunt for Red October,' and 'Legend.' He also continued to be a respected figure in theatre, earning a Tony Award nomination for his role in 'Spamalot.' Tim Curry's passing marks the end of an era for many fans who will forever remember his audacious spirit and unparalleled talent.
