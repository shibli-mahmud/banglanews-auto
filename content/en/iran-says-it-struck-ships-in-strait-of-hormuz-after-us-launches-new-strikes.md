Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) released a statement today claiming responsibility for launching attacks on multiple ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz, a narrow waterway crucial for global oil trade. While specific details regarding the affected vessels and the extent of damage were not immediately disclosed, the declaration marks a significant escalation by Tehran, directly retaliating against recent US military actions.

This development follows a series of US airstrikes carried out by the US Central Command (CENTCOM) against what they described as Iranian-backed militia sites in neighboring Iraq and Syria. CENTCOM asserted that these strikes were a necessary and justified response to "unwarranted and continued aggression" originating from Iran and its proxies, citing a pattern of attacks on US interests and personnel in the region.

The exchange of military actions has ignited fresh concerns over regional stability and the potential for a wider conflict. The Strait of Hormuz has long been a flashpoint, with Iran frequently threatening to disrupt shipping if its interests are perceived to be threatened. The latest incidents underscore the volatile nature of the geopolitical landscape in the Middle East and the precarious balance of power between the United States and Iran.
