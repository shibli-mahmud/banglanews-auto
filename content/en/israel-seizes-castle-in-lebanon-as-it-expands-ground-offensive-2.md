Israel has announced the seizure of a strategically vital castle located in southern Lebanon, marking a significant development in its ongoing ground offensive against the militant group Hezbollah. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu described the capture as a "decisive shift" in the military campaign, emphasizing its importance in disrupting Hezbollah's operational capabilities. The fortress, whose exact name has not been released, is believed to have been a key stronghold for the organization, offering commanding views of the surrounding terrain and serving as a critical logistical point.

This expansion of Israel's ground operations into Lebanese territory comes amid heightened regional tensions and a relentless exchange of fire across the border. The Israeli military stated that the operation was a success, leading to the dismantling of fortified positions and the apprehension of several militants. However, the advance has not been without its challenges, with reports of fierce resistance encountered by Israeli forces.

Meanwhile, the escalation has drawn sharp criticism from several European governments. A joint statement released by several EU foreign ministers expressed deep concern over the widening conflict and urged for an immediate de-escalation to prevent further loss of civilian life. They emphasized the need for diplomatic solutions and adherence to international law. The United Nations has also called for restraint from all parties involved, reiterating its commitment to facilitating dialogue and de-escalation in the region. The situation remains volatile, with the long-term implications of this strategic capture yet to unfold.
