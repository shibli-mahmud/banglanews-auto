Dhaka: In a significant turn of events during the charge framing session for the Ramisa rape and murder case, the prime accused, Sohel Rana, has implicated a new individual, alleging the involvement of a man known as 'Dollar'. The court was reportedly taken by surprise when Rana, who was present for the proceedings, brought forth this new information. While the details surrounding 'Dollar''s alleged role remain vague, Rana reportedly stated that this individual was also part of the conspiracy that led to Ramisa's horrific death.  Investigators are now scrambling to identify and locate this 'Dollar' to ascertain his connection to the crime.  The prosecution is expected to seek further interrogation of Sohel Rana to extract more details about 'Dollar' and his alleged accomplices.  This revelation could potentially broaden the scope of the investigation, leading to the identification of other individuals involved in the crime. The court has adjourned the proceedings, and a new date will be set for the continuation of the charge framing once further investigations are completed. The family of Ramisa has expressed their shock and hope that this new lead will bring all the perpetrators to justice.  The legal fraternity is keenly observing the case, as such late-stage revelations can significantly impact the trial's direction and outcome.
