The National Police Chiefs' Council (NPCC) has voiced significant concerns over the government's decision to permit pubs to stay open until 5 AM on Monday for England's Euro 2024 group stage match.  A spokesperson for the NPCC stated that the "late announcement" has created operational challenges, forcing police forces to reallocate officers from other vital responsibilities to manage the expected increase in late-night activity.  While acknowledging the public's desire to celebrate and support the national team, the NPCC highlighted that such extensions place an additional burden on already stretched police resources.  The council emphasized that the lack of advance notice means that deployment plans and resource allocation cannot be adequately managed, potentially impacting the response to other incidents across the country.  This decision, the NPCC argues, underscores a recurring issue where public events are announced with insufficient lead time, creating difficulties for law enforcement to ensure public safety effectively.  The council is calling for more coordinated planning between government bodies and police forces for future events of this nature to mitigate such disruptions.
