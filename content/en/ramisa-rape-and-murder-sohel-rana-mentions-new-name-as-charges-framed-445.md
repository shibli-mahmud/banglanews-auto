This unexpected revelation has sent ripples through the investigation, suggesting a potentially wider network of individuals involved in the horrific incident.

Sources close to the investigation indicate that the prosecution plans to delve deeper into this new lead, seeking to corroborate Rana's claims and establish the extent of 'Dollar's' alleged participation. The framing of charges is a critical juncture in any criminal trial, where the court formally establishes the accusations against the accused. Rana's statement at this stage is being closely scrutinized by legal experts and investigators alike.

The Ramisa case, which has garnered widespread public attention and outrage, involved the brutal rape and subsequent murder of the young victim. The initial investigation focused on Sohel Rana, leading to his arrest. However, this latest development opens up new avenues for inquiry, potentially shifting the focus of the investigation and broadening the scope of the charges.

Authorities have not yet released official statements regarding the identity or whereabouts of the individual named 'Dollar'. However, it is understood that efforts are underway to identify and apprehend this new person of interest. The prosecution team has indicated that they will pursue all leads diligently to ensure justice for Ramisa and her family. The court is expected to review the new information as the case progresses towards trial.
