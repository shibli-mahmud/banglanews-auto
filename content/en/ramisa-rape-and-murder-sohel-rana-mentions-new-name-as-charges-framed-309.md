The prosecution is expected to incorporate this new information into their ongoing efforts to build a comprehensive case. 

Sources close to the investigation suggest that Sohel Rana's statement, made during the charge framing session, has opened up new avenues for inquiry. While the name of the newly implicated person has not been publicly disclosed, law enforcement agencies have confirmed that they are taking this lead seriously. "We are meticulously examining every piece of information provided, and this new name will be thoroughly investigated," stated a senior police official involved in the case, who wished to remain anonymous. 

The formal framing of charges signifies a critical juncture, setting the stage for the trial to commence. The court has laid out the allegations against the identified perpetrators, paving the way for the presentation of evidence and witness testimonies. The Ramisa case has garnered considerable public attention and outrage, with demands for swift justice echoing across the nation. 

The inclusion of a new name, if substantiated, could potentially alter the dynamics of the trial and the scope of accountability. Legal experts have commented that such late revelations can sometimes complicate proceedings but also highlight the commitment of the justice system to uncover the full truth. The coming days are expected to bring more clarity as investigators delve deeper into the implications of Sohel Rana's latest statement.
