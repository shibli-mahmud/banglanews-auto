The Chief Constable of Greater Manchester Police, Alexis Boon, has publicly apologised to the family of Henry Nowak after a video circulated showing the murder victim being handcuffed and arrested. In an interview with the BBC, Boon expressed deep regret and concern over the footage, stating, "I have seen the footage myself and it is distressing to see how Mr Nowak was treated. I want to offer my sincere apologies to Mr Nowak’s family."

Mr Nowak was a victim of a fatal stabbing incident. The circumstances leading to his arrest and the subsequent treatment, as captured on video, have raised serious questions about police conduct. While the full context of the arrest is still under investigation, Chief Constable Boon assured the public that a thorough review of the incident is underway. He emphasised that the actions depicted in the video do not reflect the values and standards the force strives to uphold.

"We are committed to ensuring that our officers act with professionalism, respect, and fairness at all times. We are looking into this specific incident to understand exactly what happened and why, and to learn any lessons that need to be learned," Boon added. The apology comes amidst growing public scrutiny of police interactions with vulnerable individuals and victims of crime. The police force has pledged to cooperate fully with any independent investigations and to take appropriate action based on the findings.
