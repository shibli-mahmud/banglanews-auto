Dhaka: In a significant turn of events during the framing of charges in the much-publicized Ramisa rape and murder case, the prime accused, Sohel Rana, has reportedly divulged a new name, identifying a man known only as 'Dollar' as an accomplice. The statement was made during the court proceedings on Monday, adding a new dimension to the already grim investigation.

The case, which has sent shockwaves across the nation, involves the brutal rape and murder of young Ramisa. Sohel Rana has been the central figure in the police investigation and subsequent legal proceedings. However, his recent assertion of 'Dollar's' involvement has caught many by surprise and raises questions about the extent of the conspiracy.

Authorities are now scrambling to identify and locate this individual named 'Dollar' to ascertain his role in the heinous crime. The prosecution is expected to use this new information to further bolster their case against Sohel Rana and any potential co-conspirators. Defense lawyers, on the other hand, may seek to explore this revelation as a means of casting doubt on Rana's sole culpability or to implicate others. The court's next steps will likely involve investigating the veracity of Rana's claim and potentially issuing warrants if 'Dollar' is identified and found to have a connection to the case. The Ramisa case has become a symbol of the fight against violent crime, and this latest development underscores the persistent challenges in uncovering the full truth behind such atrocities.
