Shally Rahman and her daughter, 16-year-old Samiha Rahman, were tragically pulled from the water following a heroic but ultimately fatal attempt to save a young child. The 7-year-old boy had reportedly been struggling in the waves, prompting the brave intervention of the two women. Witnesses described the scene as chaotic and terrifying as both the boy and the rescuers were caught in the strong currents. Despite the best efforts of bystanders and the swift arrival of lifeguards and paramedics, Shally and Samiha could not be revived. The young boy, thankfully, was successfully rescued and is reported to be in stable condition, receiving medical attention. The local community is in shock and mourning the loss of the two selfless individuals who paid the ultimate price while acting with immense courage. An investigation into the circumstances surrounding the incident is underway, with authorities emphasizing the importance of water safety and advising the public to be aware of dangerous currents and to exercise caution when entering the sea, especially with children. The family of Shally and Samiha have requested privacy during this incredibly difficult time, as they grapple with their profound grief.
