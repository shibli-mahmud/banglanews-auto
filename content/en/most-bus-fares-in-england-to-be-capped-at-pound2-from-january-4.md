Millions of bus passengers in England are set to benefit from a new government initiative set to launch in the new year. From January 1st, 2023, the maximum fare for single bus journeys outside of London will be capped at £2. This significant policy, announced by the Department for Transport, is designed to provide much-needed relief to individuals and families grappling with the ongoing cost of living crisis.  Officials have stated that the measure will "help with the cost of living and give people the breathing space they need," particularly during the challenging winter months.

The government has allocated substantial funding to support bus operators in implementing this fare reduction. While the exact number of routes that will be included in the scheme is still being finalized, it is expected to cover the vast majority of single fares across England. This move is also intended to encourage greater use of public transport, potentially reducing reliance on private vehicles and contributing to environmental goals.  Campaigners have welcomed the news, emphasizing the vital role that affordable public transport plays in connecting communities and supporting essential travel for work, education, and social activities.  However, some have raised concerns about the long-term sustainability of the funding and whether the cap will extend beyond the initial period.  The Department for Transport has indicated that this is a short-term intervention to address immediate pressures but has not ruled out future extensions if successful. The £2 cap is anticipated to make bus travel more accessible and appealing, particularly for those on lower incomes and in rural areas where public transport options are often limited.
