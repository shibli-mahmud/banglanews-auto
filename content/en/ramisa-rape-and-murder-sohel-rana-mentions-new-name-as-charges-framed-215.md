Sources close to the court proceedings indicated that Rana implicated an individual previously not on the radar of the investigating agency. The specifics of this new suspect's alleged role in the heinous crime are yet to be disclosed, adding a layer of complexity to an already high-profile case. Legal experts suggest that this development could necessitate a reopening of certain investigative avenues or a re-evaluation of existing evidence. The prosecution is expected to meticulously examine Rana's claims, cross-referencing them with collected evidence to ascertain their veracity. The victim's family, who have been seeking justice with unwavering determination, expressed a mix of apprehension and hope, emphasizing their desire for the truth to surface regardless of who is involved. This latest twist underscores the volatile nature of such complex criminal investigations, where new information can dramatically alter the course of justice. The court has adjourned the proceedings to consider Rana's statement and further direct the investigation.
