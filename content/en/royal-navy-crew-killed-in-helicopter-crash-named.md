The Ministry of Defence confirmed the identities of the deceased pilots as Lieutenant Commander James Tiner and Lieutenant Commander Christopher Davies. The incident occurred on Wednesday afternoon near the Dartmoor training area. Emergency services were immediately dispatched to the scene, but tragically, no survivors were found.

A full investigation into the cause of the crash has been launched by the military, with support from civilian authorities. The Merlin Mk4, a maritime attack helicopter operated by the Royal Navy, is a vital asset for various operations. Officials have expressed their profound sadness and offered condolences to the families of the fallen servicemen.

Lieutenant Commander Tiner and Lieutenant Commander Davies were experienced aviators, and their loss is deeply felt within the Royal Navy and the wider armed forces community. The exact circumstances leading to the crash are still under scrutiny, and investigators are expected to examine all available evidence, including flight data recorders and witness accounts.

This incident serves as a stark reminder of the inherent risks faced by military personnel during training exercises. The Royal Navy has a proud history of operational excellence, and the courage and dedication of its crews are widely respected. Further updates are expected as the investigation progresses.
