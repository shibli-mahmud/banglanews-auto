In a testament to human endurance, 97-year-old Guna Maya Bohara has been successfully rescued from the clutches of fierce flash floods that recently swept through Nepal. The elderly woman found herself stranded amidst the surging waters, her small home rendered vulnerable by the torrent.  Local authorities, alerted to her plight, mounted a daring rescue mission that relied on heavy machinery to reach her.  Eyewitnesses and the rescue team itself were struck by Ms. Bohara's composure and strength, despite her advanced age and the terrifying circumstances.  One rescuer, speaking anonymously, described her appearance upon being found as akin to 'a warrior,' a sentiment echoed by many who have followed her story.  The incident highlights the devastating impact of the recent monsoon rains in the region, which have caused widespread destruction and displacement.  Ms. Bohara's survival, however, offers a beacon of hope and a powerful narrative of resilience in the face of overwhelming natural adversity.  Her safe return to a secure location has brought relief to her family and the wider community, who are now focused on recovery efforts in the flood-affected areas. The story of the 97-year-old 'warrior' woman is quickly becoming a symbol of the indomitable spirit of the Nepali people.
