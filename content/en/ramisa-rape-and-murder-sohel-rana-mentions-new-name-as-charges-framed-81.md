The court, in session to formally charge Rana with the heinous crimes, was taken aback by this assertion, which reportedly came as a sudden outburst from the accused. While the specifics of Rana's claim and Dollar's alleged connection to the crime remain unclear, the mention has immediately prompted law enforcement agencies to initiate a parallel investigation to identify and apprehend this 'Dollar'.

Ramisa's brutal murder and the subsequent allegations have gripped the nation, with public demand for justice remaining incredibly high. The prosecution had meticulously prepared their case against Rana based on existing evidence. However, this new name, if substantiated, could potentially alter the trajectory of the investigation, necessitating the collection of fresh evidence and the re-evaluation of existing testimonies. Defense lawyers have yet to comment on this development, while legal experts suggest that the court will need to carefully consider the validity and implications of Rana's statement. The focus will now be on verifying the existence and identity of 'Dollar' and determining whether this individual played any role, directly or indirectly, in the commission of the crimes against Ramisa. The court has adjourned for the day, with further proceedings expected to address this new information.
