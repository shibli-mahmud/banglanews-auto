The fatal incident occurred on Tuesday evening when law enforcement officers, acting on intelligence, located Abdul Ballout in Spandau. According to a spokesperson for the Berlin police, Ballout resisted arrest and advanced on officers wielding a knife.  In response, officers discharged their firearms, resulting in Ballout sustaining fatal injuries. He was pronounced dead at the scene.  The investigation into the initial Pride attack is ongoing, and authorities have stated that Ballout was the primary suspect. The motive behind the earlier assault, which saw a woman injured, is still being thoroughly examined, but initial reports suggest it may have been religiously motivated.  The swift action by the police in apprehending Ballout, though resulting in his death, prevented further potential harm.  The incident has sent ripples of shock through the LGBTQ+ community and the wider Berlin populace, with many expressing both relief that the suspect is apprehended and sadness at the loss of life.  The full details surrounding the confrontation and the ongoing investigation are expected to be released by the Berlin police department in the coming days.  Security measures around LGBTQ+ events in the city are reportedly under review.
