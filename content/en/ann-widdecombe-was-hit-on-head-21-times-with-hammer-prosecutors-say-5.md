Following the brutal assault, the prosecution alleges that Mr. Kerry proceeded to steal Ms. Widdecombe's wallet. The details emerged as part of legal proceedings against Mr. Kerry, who faces charges related to the alleged attack and theft. The prosecution's account paints a grim picture of the event, highlighting the severity of the alleged violence. Ms. Widdecombe, a well-known public figure, has not yet made a personal statement regarding the incident. Further details are expected to be revealed as the court case progresses. The alleged motive appears to be robbery, with the hammer used as a weapon of opportunity during the home invasion.
