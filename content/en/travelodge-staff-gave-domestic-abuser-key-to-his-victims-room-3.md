A terrifying ordeal has been recounted by a woman who claims staff at a Travelodge hotel inadvertently enabled her domestic abuser to gain access to her private room. According to a BBC report, the woman was staying at the hotel when her attacker, who she had previously reported to the police, was allegedly given the key to her room by reception staff.

The situation escalated rapidly when the man arrived at her door. The victim, whose identity has been protected, described the harrowing experience to the BBC, stating that the abuser kicked her hotel room door with such force that it was "kicked off its hinges." This violent act demonstrates a complete disregard for the victim's safety and privacy, and raises serious questions about the security protocols in place at the hotel.

Following the incident, the woman expressed her shock and distress, highlighting the perceived failure of the hotel to protect her. She stated that she had informed the hotel about her concerns regarding the man's presence. The alleged negligence on the part of the Travelodge staff has led to significant distress for the victim and has sparked concerns about hotel safety for vulnerable individuals. An investigation into the hotel's procedures and the actions of its staff is expected.
