In a move designed to offer immediate support to households, most single bus fares in England will be capped at a maximum of £2 from January 1st, 2023. The Department for Transport has confirmed that this measure will be in place until March 31st, 2023, potentially affecting millions of commuters. The government has framed this policy as a vital step in helping people manage their expenses and providing them with essential breathing room amidst economic challenges.

The aim is to make public transport more affordable, encouraging more people to opt for buses rather than private vehicles, which can contribute to congestion and environmental pollution. This could also be a significant boost for local economies by making it easier for people to travel to work, shops, and leisure activities.

Transport Secretary Mark Harper stated that the government is "back buses" and understands the pressures people are facing. He highlighted that the £2 fare cap is part of a wider package of support and will help connect communities and support the economy. Bus operators are being consulted on the finer details of the scheme. While the cap applies to most single fares, some longer-distance or rural routes might be excluded. However, the vast majority of journeys are expected to benefit from this price reduction, offering a tangible financial advantage to regular bus users across the country during the winter months.
