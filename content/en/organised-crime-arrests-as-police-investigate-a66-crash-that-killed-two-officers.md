Twelve people, comprising nine men and three women, have been arrested in connection with a broad investigation into organised crime, following a tragic incident on the A66 that resulted in the deaths of two police officers and five other individuals. The arrests were made as part of a coordinated operation targeting suspected criminal networks operating across the region.

Sources close to the investigation have indicated that the police believe there may be a connection between the organised crime activities of the apprehended individuals and the circumstances leading up to the fatal collision. While the exact nature of this alleged link remains under wraps, investigators are meticulously piecing together the events that transpired before the multi-vehicle crash.

Details surrounding the crash itself are still being thoroughly examined by the Major Collision Investigation Unit. The initial reports confirmed seven fatalities, including the two officers who were responding to an incident at the time. The scale of the organised crime operation suspected has prompted a multi-agency response, with various law enforcement bodies collaborating to dismantle the alleged network.

The arrested individuals are currently being held for questioning on suspicion of a range of offences related to organised crime. The investigation is ongoing, and police have not ruled out further arrests as they delve deeper into the suspected illicit operations. This development underscores the persistent threat posed by organised crime and the significant risks faced by law enforcement officers dedicated to combating it.
