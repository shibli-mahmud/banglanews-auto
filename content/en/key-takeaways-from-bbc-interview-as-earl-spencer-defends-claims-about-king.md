Charles Spencer, the younger brother of the late Princess Diana, engaged in a candid interview with the BBC's Laura Kuenssberg, offering his perspective on his sister's life and addressing allegations that have surfaced ahead of the release of his new book detailing her story. The interview, conducted as a prelude to the book's launch, saw Spencer articulate his views on Diana's complex relationship with the Royal Family and the public, as well as his own experiences growing up alongside her.

Spencer emphasized his desire to present a more nuanced and personal account of Diana, moving beyond the sensationalism that has often characterized media coverage of her. He spoke of his sister as a mother and a woman, seeking to highlight aspects of her personality and struggles that he feels have been overlooked. A significant portion of the discussion revolved around his efforts to ensure Diana's true story is told, particularly concerning her treatment by certain institutions and individuals.

While details of the book's specific content remain under wraps, the interview strongly suggested that Spencer intends to shed light on sensitive issues, potentially including Diana's mental health, her interactions with the press, and the pressures she faced within royal life. He was keen to counter what he perceives as misrepresentations and to set the record straight regarding events that shaped Diana's public image and personal life. The interview served as a platform for Spencer to signal the introspective and potentially critical nature of his forthcoming publication, positioning himself as a guardian of his sister's narrative.
