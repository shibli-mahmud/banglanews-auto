This development is crucial as the court is in the process of framing charges against the accused.

The Ramisa case, which has sent shockwaves across the nation due to its horrific nature, has been under intense scrutiny. The police have been working diligently to piece together the sequence of events and identify all individuals responsible. The mention of a new name by Sohel Rana, if substantiated, could lead to further arrests and a more comprehensive understanding of the conspiracy behind Ramisa's death.

Legal experts anticipate that the prosecution will use this new information to strengthen their case. The framing of charges is a critical stage in the judicial process, where the court formally declares the accusations against the defendant. The inclusion of a new suspect would necessitate amendments or additions to the charge sheet, potentially delaying the trial but ultimately aiming for a more just outcome. Families of victims often seek closure through thorough investigations and fair trials, and this latest revelation could be a step towards that.

The public awaits further updates with bated breath as the legal proceedings unfold. The authorities have assured the public that all leads will be thoroughly investigated to ensure that justice is served for Ramisa.
