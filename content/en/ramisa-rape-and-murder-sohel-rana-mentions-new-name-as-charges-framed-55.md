This revelation came to light as charges were formally framed against the accused in court, bringing the protracted investigation closer to a trial. The court's decision to frame charges signifies that sufficient evidence has been gathered to proceed with the legal proceedings.

Sources close to the investigation report that Sohel Rana, during his deposition or interrogation, claimed that a man named 'Dollar' was also involved in the horrific crime. While details about 'Dollar's' alleged role or identity remain scarce, this new accusation is expected to prompt further inquiry and potentially widen the scope of the investigation. Authorities are now under pressure to identify and locate this individual to ascertain their connection to Ramisa's death.

The framing of charges is a critical juncture in any criminal case. It means the court has reviewed the evidence presented by the prosecution and believes there are sufficient grounds to put the accused on trial. This process often involves multiple hearings and discussions regarding the evidence and the charges themselves. The involvement of a new suspect, especially one named by the primary accused, could lead to delays as investigators scramble to gather corroborating evidence or conduct further interrogations.

Ramisa's family, who have been awaiting justice, expressed a mix of hope and apprehension following the news. While any step towards accountability is welcome, the introduction of a new suspect introduces an element of uncertainty. The legal fraternity anticipates intense legal arguments as the case moves forward, particularly concerning the veracity of Sohel Rana's latest claims and the potential involvement of 'Dollar'. The prosecution will now focus on building a solid case against all implicated individuals, aiming to bring closure to this deeply disturbing case.
