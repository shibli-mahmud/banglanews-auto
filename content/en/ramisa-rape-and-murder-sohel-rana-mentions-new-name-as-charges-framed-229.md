The details surrounding 'Dolla's' alleged involvement remain scarce, with authorities yet to release further information regarding the identity or whereabouts of this newly named individual. Investigators are reportedly working to corroborate Rana's statement and establish 'Dolla's' connection to the case.

Legal experts suggest that the introduction of a new suspect at this stage, particularly after charges have been framed, could potentially lead to amendments in the indictment or further investigations to bring all perpetrators to justice. The prosecution is expected to present evidence linking the accused, including any findings related to 'Dolla,' to the crime. The family of Ramisa has expressed a desperate plea for a swift and thorough investigation, hoping that this new lead will bring them closer to closure and ensure that all responsible parties are held accountable for the horrific act.

The framing of charges signifies a critical step towards trial, and the court's proceedings are now expected to focus on presenting evidence and arguments from both the prosecution and defense. The inclusion of 'Dolla' adds a layer of complexity to the already sensitive case, emphasizing the ongoing efforts to unravel the full truth behind Ramisa's tragic end.
