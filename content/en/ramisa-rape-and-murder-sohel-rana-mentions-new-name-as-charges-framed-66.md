The judicial process in the deeply disturbing Ramisa rape and murder case took a surprising turn today as the primary accused, Sohel Rana, brought forth a new name during the indictment proceedings. Rana, who is facing severe charges related to the brutal act, claimed that a man known as 'Dollar' was also complicit in the crime. This assertion was made in the Dhaka court as the formal charges were being laid out, marking a crucial stage in the legal battle. 

Details surrounding 'Dollar' remain scarce, and it is unclear whether this individual is already on the police radar or if this is an entirely new lead. The prosecution is expected to thoroughly investigate Rana's claims, which could lead to a wider net being cast in the hunt for all perpetrators. The court has acknowledged Rana's statement and will likely consider it as it proceeds with the trial. The victim's family, who have been seeking justice for months, expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension, emphasizing their desire for the truth to be fully uncovered and all those responsible to be held accountable. The framing of charges signifies the commencement of the substantive trial, and the mention of 'Dollar' is poised to become a focal point of the upcoming legal proceedings. The investigation team is reportedly working to identify and locate this new individual to ascertain their role in the heinous crime.
