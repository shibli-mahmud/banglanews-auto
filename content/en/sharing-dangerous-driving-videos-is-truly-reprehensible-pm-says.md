Prime Minister Rishi Sunak has issued a stern warning against the creation and dissemination of videos that showcase dangerous driving manoeuvres, such as driving the wrong way down motorways. He described the act of sharing such content as 'truly reprehensible,' emphasizing the grave risks involved. The government has directly appealed to major social media companies, including TikTok, to take swift and decisive action to remove these hazardous videos from their platforms.  Ministers are reportedly exploring all available avenues to ensure these dangerous clips are no longer accessible, highlighting the potential for imitation and the severe consequences that can arise from such reckless behaviour.  The pushback from the government signals a growing intolerance for content that glorifies or trivializes dangerous activities on public roads, with a clear message that public safety is paramount.  The focus on platforms like TikTok underscores the widespread reach these videos can have, particularly among younger audiences, raising concerns about the normalisation of risky driving. Authorities are urging the public to report any such content they encounter online. The shared responsibility between social media platforms and users is crucial in combating this dangerous trend and fostering a safer online and offline environment.
