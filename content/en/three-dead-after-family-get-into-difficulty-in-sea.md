Emergency services were scrambled to Shoreham beach on Saturday afternoon following reports of a group of people struggling in the water. Despite the best efforts of lifeguards and paramedics, a man, a woman, and a teenage girl were tragically pronounced dead at the scene. A fourth member of the family, a younger girl, was rescued and transported to hospital in a critical condition. Her prognosis remains uncertain as medical teams work tirelessly to save her life. 

The exact circumstances leading to the family getting into difficulty are still under investigation by the coastguard and local police. Initial reports suggest a sudden change in sea conditions may have contributed to the incident. Witnesses described the sea as appearing deceptively calm before conditions rapidly deteriorated, catching swimmers off guard. 

This heartbreaking event has sent shockwaves through the local community, with many expressing their deep sorrow and offering condolences to the bereaved family. Local authorities are urging the public to exercise extreme caution when entering the water, especially given the unpredictable nature of the sea. They are reminding beachgoers to check weather forecasts, be aware of rip currents, and swim only in designated safe areas, preferably with lifeguards present. The investigation into the tragedy is ongoing, and further details are expected to be released as they become available.
