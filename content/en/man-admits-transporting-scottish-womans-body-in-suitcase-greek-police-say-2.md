Greek police have arrested a 38-year-old man who has allegedly admitted to transporting the body of a 24-year-old Scottish woman in a suitcase. The woman, identified as Sarah Golding, was reported missing by her family in Scotland last week after she failed to return from a holiday trip to the Greek island of Crete.

Authorities were alerted when a member of the public discovered a suspicious suitcase in a remote area on the outskirts of Heraklion, the capital of Crete. Upon inspection, the grisly contents were revealed. Following an investigation, police apprehended the suspect, who is believed to have been known to the victim. During questioning, the man reportedly confessed to transporting Ms. Golding's body, though the exact circumstances and timeline of events remain under investigation. 

He has been taken into custody on suspicion of manslaughter, robbery, and weapons offenses. Investigators are working to establish the full sequence of events leading up to Ms. Golding's death and the subsequent disposal of her body. The motive for the alleged crime is currently unclear, and the man's statements are being further scrutinized. The Scottish Foreign Office has been informed and is providing consular assistance to the victim's family during this difficult time. The investigation is ongoing, with forensic teams examining the suitcase and the surrounding area for further evidence.
