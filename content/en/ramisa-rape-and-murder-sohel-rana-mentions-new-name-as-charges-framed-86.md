The legal proceedings against Sohel Rana, the primary suspect in the brutal rape and murder of Ramisa, have taken a dramatic turn with the accused naming a new individual during the framing of charges. Sources close to the investigation report that Rana identified a man known by the moniker 'Dollar' as a person of interest, potentially connected to the crime. This unexpected disclosure has prompted authorities to expand the scope of their inquiry, seeking to locate and question this newly named individual. The prosecution anticipates that 'Dollar' may possess crucial information or have direct knowledge of the events leading to Ramisa's tragic demise. The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the legal process, moving the case closer to trial. However, the introduction of a new name adds a layer of complexity, necessitating further investigation to corroborate Rana's claims and establish the extent of 'Dollar's' alleged involvement. Legal experts suggest that this development could either strengthen the prosecution's case by unearthing new evidence or complicate matters if the claims are unsubstantiated. The victim's family, who has been seeking justice since the incident, expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension, emphasizing the need for a thorough and impartial investigation that brings all perpetrators to account. The police department has assured that all leads will be diligently pursued to ensure justice for Ramisa.
