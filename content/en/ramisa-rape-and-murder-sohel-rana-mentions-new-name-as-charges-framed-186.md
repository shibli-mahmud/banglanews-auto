Dhaka: The trial proceedings in the highly publicized Ramisa rape and murder case took an unexpected turn on Monday as the prime accused, Sohel Rana, during the framing of charges, revealed the name of another individual allegedly involved in the crime. Rana claimed that a man known by the moniker 'Dollar' was also part of the conspiracy and execution of the brutal act that has sent shockwaves across the nation. This unexpected disclosure has caught the attention of the court and the prosecution, who are now tasked with verifying Rana's statement and investigating the potential involvement of 'Dollar.'

The Ramisa case, which has been under intense public scrutiny due to its gruesome nature, has seen several twists and turns since its inception. The initial investigation had focused primarily on a limited number of suspects, but Rana's latest statement suggests a potentially wider network of perpetrators. Legal experts are observing the case closely, noting that such revelations during the charge framing stage can significantly impact the trajectory of the trial. The prosecution is expected to present evidence to corroborate or refute Rana's claims about 'Dollar.'

The court has taken note of Rana's statement and has indicated that it will be thoroughly examined. The framing of charges is a critical juncture in any criminal trial, where the court formally brings accusations against the accused based on the evidence presented. The inclusion of a new name at this stage necessitates a careful review of the existing evidence and may lead to further investigation into the alleged accomplice. The judicial process aims to ensure that all individuals responsible for the crime are brought to justice, and this new information could be pivotal in achieving that objective.
