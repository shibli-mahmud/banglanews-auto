Dhaka, Bangladesh – June 1, 2026 – The Ramisa rape and murder case has taken a new turn with the prime accused, Sohel Rana, reportedly revealing the name of an additional suspect during the formal framing of charges. The Dhaka Metropolitan Sessions Court today heard the proceedings, where Rana, through his legal counsel, is said to have provided information that could implicate another individual in the heinous crime. This development has raised eyebrows and sparked fresh speculation about the full extent of the conspiracy behind Ramisa's brutal murder and sexual assault.

Rana, who has been in custody since his arrest, had previously maintained his sole involvement. However, this sudden disclosure during the charge framing process, a stage where the accused formally pleads guilty or not guilty to the charges, suggests a potential shift in his narrative or a strategic move to involve others. Legal experts anticipate that this new name will necessitate further investigation by the prosecution, potentially leading to amended charges or the inclusion of the new individual as a co-accused.

The Ramisa case, which shook the nation with its brutality, has been under intense public scrutiny. The victim, Ramisa, was found dead under horrific circumstances, sparking widespread outrage and demands for swift justice. The police investigation has been ongoing, and this latest revelation adds another layer of complexity to an already deeply disturbing crime. The court is expected to meticulously review the new information and decide on the next steps, which could significantly impact the trajectory of the trial.
