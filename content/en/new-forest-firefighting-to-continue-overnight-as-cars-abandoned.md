Firefighting efforts in the New Forest are expected to continue through the night as a wildfire raged on Sunday, prompting the evacuation of several areas and the abandonment of cars.  Thick smoke billowed across the landscape as the blaze spread rapidly through dry vegetation, fanned by prevailing winds.  The Hampshire Fire and Rescue Service deployed multiple crews to the scene, supported by other emergency agencies.  The intensity of the fire led to concerns for the safety of parked vehicles, with one woman recounting her harrowing experience. "I honestly thought our car was going to go up in flames," she told reporters, describing the rapid advance of the fire on Sunday afternoon. "We had to leave it and just run. It was terrifying to see how quickly it took hold."

The incident led to significant disruption, with roads closed and the public advised to avoid the affected areas. The dry conditions and high temperatures have created a tinderbox environment, making such fires particularly challenging to control. Fire chiefs have reiterated warnings about the dangers of discarded cigarettes and unattended barbecues, which are common causes of such incidents. The full extent of the damage to the natural environment is yet to be assessed, but the loss of habitat for local wildlife is a significant concern.  Firefighters are working to create fire breaks and dampen down hot spots to prevent further spread. The operation is expected to be protracted, with crews remaining on site to monitor the situation closely and ensure the fire is fully extinguished.
