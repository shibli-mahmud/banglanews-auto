This new information has prompted the prosecution to consider further investigation into 'Dollar's' whereabouts and his alleged connection to the crime. The details surrounding 'Dollar's' identity and his relationship with Sohel Rana remain scarce, leaving investigators with a new lead to pursue. The prosecution team has indicated that they will seek to bring 'Dollar' to justice if his involvement is proven.

The framing of charges marks a critical juncture in the legal battle for justice for Ramisa. The court has laid out the allegations against the accused, paving the way for the trial to commence. The family of Ramisa, who have been relentlessly seeking justice, expressed a mixture of hope and apprehension following the latest developments. They are urging the authorities to thoroughly investigate the new lead and ensure that all perpetrators are brought to account for their actions. The inclusion of 'Dollar' in the narrative adds a layer of complexity to the already grim case, underscoring the need for a comprehensive and diligent investigation.
